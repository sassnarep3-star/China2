Flagged for review: source links & cost assumptions

Independent targeted review · snapshot 11 November 2026. No account was accessed and no funds were transferred.

Most important findings

  1. Unverified spread assumptions are displayed as numeric all-in estimates and rankings. The cost scenarios and 40-tier ladder disclose that they use modeled spreads, but showing assumed numeric ranges conflicts with the request to leave unverified values blank. They cannot identify the cheapest actual option. See assumptions and the strict-blank alternative. The bank's published tariff, important note 7, excludes other institutions' charges and government taxes; it is not an all-in quote. Status: needs review.
  2. Direct row-level source links are not universal. In particular, ladder cost and tax rows link to an internal source page rather than directly to the authority. This can help navigation but does not satisfy the literal request for an official-site link in every row. Some other rows have no source link at all. See the scoped inventory below. Status: needs review.
  3. Personal tax and actual cost remain unknown. SAFE Tianjin says lawful current-account RMB income of overseas individuals can be converted at a bank with valid ID and source documentation including tax documents; that does not establish eligibility for every source of funds. IRS guidance notes that foreign currency gains or losses may need recognition; its 2025 average of 7.129 is historical, not a live executable rate. Status: needs review.
  4. Reporting is separate from tax. IRS FBAR guidance describes an aggregate foreign-account threshold exceeding $10,000 at any time in the year, subject to exceptions. That threshold is not a tax on transfers. Status: review individual filing history.

What the static scan found

Scanned 139 HTML pages and 11317 data rows; 1491 rows on 36 pages have no direct link to an allowlisted government, institutional or primary-provider domain in their own <tr>.

These are review candidates, not a count of unsupported facts. Internal links may lead to official sources on another page; some rows are repository inventories or unverified placeholders rather than claims needing a legal source. Conversely, a matching domain does not prove a link works, that the source supports the row, or that all cells are verified. Bank and provider websites are primary commercial sources, not government authorities.

The scan reads static <tr> elements containing <td>; it does not execute JavaScript, check CSV/JSON, follow indirect links, verify facts, or check remote link health. It excludes this audit's own folder, Git metadata, caches and dependencies. Results are a dated snapshot, not an ongoing certification. The finite host allowlist can miss other valid official domains.
