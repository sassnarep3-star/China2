M01 · Onshore Bank Documented Current-Account Conversion + SWIFT Wire (SHA / OUR / Private Banking)

Lawful · Documented · Category A: Documented Current-Account Bank & Licensed Channels

Best for: U.S. citizen with lawful onshore RMB income (salary, labor remuneration, rent, royalties) backed by contracts and PRC tax vouchers (完税证明 / 纳税记录), including multi-year historical earnings

Statutory limit & gate: No statutory upper dollar cap — limited strictly to documented after-tax lawful RMB income verified by the bank (Hui Fa [2007] No. 1 Art. 13(1); SAFE Henan Q&A 5 confirms no statutory age limit on historical salary records)

PRC tax rule: 0% conversion tax; 0% extra PRC income tax if statutory IIT (3%–45% comprehensive / 20% lease / 10% treaty royalty) was already withheld/paid
U.S. tax & reporting impact: Principal transfer is not U.S. income; PRC IIT paid is creditable on Form 1116 (unless excluded via Form 2555 FEIE up to $132,900 in 2026); personal FX gain > $200 vs USD tax basis is capital gain (26 U.S.C. § 988(e)(2); Pub. 525)

Pros (Verified)
  • No USD 50,000/year cap — moves the entire documented after-tax balance ($10,000 to $400,000+) in a single lawful wire.
  • SAFE confirms no statutory expiration window on older lawful RMB salary (even 10-year-old earnings qualify if original contracts and tax proofs are presented).
  • Published sender wire fee is capped: at HSBC China, Private Banking / Premier Elite individuals pay $0.00; Premier / Advance pay 0.1% capped at CNY 500 + CNY 120 cable ($86.97 at 7.129 reference rate, or $115.02 with optional CNY 200 Pay Exact OUR arrangement).
  • Creates an ironclad audit trail (bank FX memo + SWIFT MT103) for U.S. bank AML review and IRS / FinCEN compliance.
Cons & Restrictions (Verified)
  • Cannot convert undocumented RMB or deposits whose PRC tax has not been settled and evidenced by official STA tax records.
  • Requires in-person branch FX counter review (or formal proxy under M05); mobile banking apps generally block non-PR foreign passports from self-service RMB→USD purchase.
  • Bank CNY→USD customer selling-rate spread and any SHA intermediary/receiving bank fees are not fixed by statute and must be quoted live.
Exact Step-by-Step Execution
  1. Download official PRC Individual Income Tax Payment Records (个人所得税纳税记录) and income details from the PRC Personal Income Tax App (个人所得税 App) or print 税收完税证明 at the local STA tax hall for every year that funded the RMB balance.
  2. Compile matching underlying contracts (stamped employment contracts, payroll slips, lease registration + tax invoices) and bank statements tying deposits to net after-tax income.
  3. Confirm U.S. FinCEN Form 114 (FBAR, >$10,000 aggregate) and Form 8938 filings are current before wiring large sums into a U.S. bank.
  4. Book an FX counter appointment at your account-holding China bank branch; bring valid U.S. passport, old passport if account/tax records used an earlier passport number, residence/work permit history, contracts, and STA tax vouchers.
  5. Request a written pre-execution quote showing: (a) applied CNY→USD spot selling rate, (b) sender wire commission + cable fee, and (c) SHA vs OUR (Pay Exact) intermediary fee option.
  6. Convert CNY to USD onshore and wire via SWIFT to your own-name U.S. bank account; retain the FX purchase slip (购汇水单), SWIFT MT103, and STA tax records for at least 5 years.

M02 · Same-Name Multinational Bank Book Transfer After Lawful FX Conversion (HSBC Global Transfer)

Lawful · Documented · Category A: Documented Current-Account Bank & Licensed Channels

Best for: U.S. citizen who qualifies for lawful onshore CNY→USD conversion (under M01, M06, M07, M09, or M10) and holds self-named HSBC accounts in both Mainland China and the United States

Statutory limit & gate: Same SAFE conversion gate as the underlying FX basis (unlimited up to documented after-tax income under M01; ~USD 50,000/yr if using PR-ID quota M06)

PRC tax rule: 0% conversion tax; requires same STA tax vouchers as M01 if converting onshore RMB income
U.S. tax & reporting impact: Same as M01: non-taxable principal transfer; check § 988(e)(2) > $200 personal FX gain and FBAR / Form 8938 thresholds

Pros (Verified)
  • Published HSBC Global Transfer fee to self-named overseas HSBC account is $0.00 for Private Banking, Premier Elite, and Qualified Premier (TRB >= CNY 500,000 or Home-Country Premier), and a flat USD 20.00 (CNY 145 indicative) for Advance.
  • Avoids third-party SWIFT intermediary lifting fees between unrelated banks and settles near-instantly once USD sits in the HSBC China FX savings sub-account.
  • Can convert up to $400,000+ in one branch visit (under M01) and sweep to HSBC U.S. at $0 Premier transfer fee.
Cons & Restrictions (Verified)
  • Does NOT bypass SAFE's CNY→USD conversion gate — RMB still must be converted to USD at the counter with passport + contracts + tax vouchers first.
  • Qualified HSBC China Premier requires CNY 500,000 Total Relationship Balance (or qualifying overseas HSBC Premier link) to avoid monthly service fees; new Advance account opening has been restricted since 18 March 2026.
  • HSBC's live CNY→USD FX conversion spread still applies and is unverified in static tariffs.
Exact Step-by-Step Execution
  1. Open and link same-name HSBC China and HSBC U.S. accounts under Global View / Global Transfer (confirm Premier qualification under Important Note 3 of the HSBC China tariff).
  2. Complete onshore CNY→USD conversion into your HSBC China USD savings sub-account at the branch counter using M01 documentation (passport + contract + STA tax vouchers).
  3. Initiate HSBC Global Transfer to Self (Tariff Item 2.2.6.1) online or at the branch from your HSBC China USD sub-account to your HSBC U.S. USD account ($0 PB/Premier; USD 20 Advance).
  4. Archive the onshore FX purchase memo, Global Transfer confirmation, and year-end peak balances for FBAR and Form 8938.

  3. Set up a CNY→USD transfer in Wise to your own-name U.S. bank account (<= CNY 300,000 per batch) to lock in the live fee and rate quote.
  4. Pay CNY from your own-name Chinese mobile banking app to the designated domestic partner account; retain Wise receipts and tax records.

Pros (Verified)

  • Requires strict document formalities: signed/notarized/apostilled Power of Attorney (per branch policy), original or verified IDs of both principal and agent, employment contract, and STA tax records.

Exact Step-by-Step Execution
  1. Call your Mainland account-holding branch first to confirm its exact proxy template, whether an apostilled/consular-authenticated Power of Attorney is required, and how account-holder identity original-sight is satisfied.

  3. Provide the agent with your STA IIT payment records (个人所得税纳税记录), employment contract, payroll vouchers, identity copies, and receiving U.S. bank wire instructions.
  4. Agent submits the dossier at the Mainland bank FX counter, obtains the written FX rate + fee quote, and executes the SWIFT wire directly to your U.S. account.
