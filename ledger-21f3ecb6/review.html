FR01The 'considerable taxes' in the brief cannot be identified
No rule read here imposes a tax on converting CNY to USD. The IIT Law taxes listed income categories, and a conversion is not one of them. Until the origin of the money and its PRC tax history are known, any tax figure would be an invention. This page therefore states no tax liability for the reader.		needs reviewS19 S20
