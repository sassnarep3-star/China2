Executive summary

Ten findings, and the one question that decides everything

Written for a US citizen with CNY in a mainland bank account who wants USD with the least tax and penalty exposure. Each finding names the official page it rests on. Nothing about the reader's own facts is assumed.

The decisive question is not which bank or which fee. It is what the money IS. After-tax salary, a property sale, an inheritance and an untaxed payment each take a different legal route, and only the last one creates a tax problem. Everything else on this site prices the plumbing around that answer.

#FindingOfficial source
1Nothing read here taxes the act of converting CNY into USD.
The Individual Income Tax Law taxes listed categories of income: wages, remuneration, royalties, business income, interest, dividends, property rental, property transfer and incidental income. A currency conversion is not on the list. What attracts PRC tax is the income that produced the money, and that tax is due whether or not the money is ever converted.		S19
2The real barrier is convertibility, not tax.
A domestic individual may buy foreign exchange up to the equivalent of USD 50,000 per person per calendar year on ID alone; above that the bank needs documents evidencing the transaction. USD 400,000 is therefore eight calendar years at one person's annual total, before any tax question is reached.		S01
3The published out-of-pocket cost of converting is small.
Using Bank of China's 11 November 2026 board and its published online-remittance schedule, USD 10,000 costs about USD 53.41 (0.534 percent) and USD 400,000 about USD 900.50 (0.225 percent), including the bank's spread against its own midpoint, the 0.1 percent commission with its cap, and the CNY 150 cable fee.		S09 S10
4The cheapest route changes at the smallest size.
At exactly USD 10,000 the HSBC Global Transfer to your own HSBC account abroad is cheaper on published terms (USD 50.03) than Bank of China (USD 53.41), because BOC's flat CNY 150 cable fee weighs most at small amounts. From USD 20,000 upward BOC is cheaper at every step. HSBC Global Transfer requires Premier status (total relationship balance of at least CNY 500,000) and an HSBC account in the destination country.		S09 S10 S11 S12
5Above the annual total, the gate is documentary, and which article applies depends on residency.
A domestic individual presents documents for each purchase above the total. A foreign national who is not a domestic individual converts lawful current-account RMB income on identity plus documents evidencing the transaction, including tax documents. The residency test is defined in the FX Regulations as a Chinese citizen, or a foreign national continuously resident in China for one full year.		S01 S26 S03
6If the money is property or an inheritance, a dedicated capital-account route exists.
Emigrant property transfer and inherited-property transfer are approved by the SAFE branch where the household was registered, need a tax certificate, and must be paid to the applicant's own account abroad. The 2014 amendment abolished the requirement to remit in stages and the national filing above RMB 500,000.		S04 S05
7The shortcuts are expressly unlawful, not grey.
Borrowing another person's annual total to get around the quota is named in the rules; it puts the individual on a watch list for the current year and the two following years. Private or indirect FX trading and exchanging legal tender for virtual currency as a business are prohibited, with fines of up to 30 percent of the amount, rising to 100 percent if serious, plus confiscation and possible criminal liability.		S02 S03 S08
8On the US side, converting principal is not income, but the currency movement can be.
Selling CNY for USD is a disposition of nonfunctional currency and therefore a section 988 transaction; the exchange-rate gain or loss is ordinary income or loss. The foreign tax credit is conditional, is limited to foreign income taxes, and only the treaty-rate tax qualifies; the treaty's 10 percent ceiling covers dividends, interest and royalties only.		S22 S21 S27
9Reporting duties attach to the account, not to the transfer.
A US citizen must file an FBAR if the aggregate balance of foreign accounts exceeded USD 10,000 at any time in the year, whether or not the money earned anything; Form 8938 has its own thresholds; a genuine gift from a nonresident alien above USD 100,000 in the year goes on Form 3520. The inflation-adjusted FBAR maximum is USD 16,536 for a non-willful violation and USD 165,353 for a willful one, and the Form 8938 penalty can reach USD 60,000.		S15 S16 S17 S18
10What cannot be answered from the facts available.
Whether any PRC tax is due at all, whether a tax certificate exists for the bank, whether the account holder is a domestic individual, and which calendar years are already overdue. Until those are known, any tax figure would be invented. The next page lists each question with the way to answer it. The rules that make it unknowable are the income categories in the tax law and the residency test in the FX regulations.		S19 S03

What follows from the findings

If the money is after-tax income

There is no PRC tax left to pay and none arises from converting. The task is documentary: establish which article applies to you, keep the tax record, and convert within the annual total or with documents above it. The published cost is under one percent at every size tested.

If the money is property or an inheritance

It is a capital-account item and cannot use the annual purchase total. The emigrant or inheritance transfer route applies, needs SAFE approval and a tax certificate, and — since 2014 — can be paid out in one go within the approved amount.

If PRC tax was never paid on it

That is the only path to a large liability, and it exists before any transfer. The surcharge runs at 0.05 percent per day and an evasion fine runs from 50 percent to 5 times the tax. Voluntary correction is a different conversation from an enquiry, and the order of operations matters.

On the US side

Converting principal does not create income. Currency movement between acquisition and conversion does, under section 988, as ordinary gain or loss. Reporting duties attach to the account each year regardless of whether anything moved.
