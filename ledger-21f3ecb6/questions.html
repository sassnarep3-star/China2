What has to be established before any decision

Sixteen questions. For each: why it changes the answer, what the rules already tell us, what is still missing, and the concrete step that produces the missing fact. No route can be chosen and no tax figure computed until the first four are answered.

Order matters. Q01 to Q04 decide everything downstream. If the answer to Q03 is "PRC tax was never paid on this income", the sequence changes completely: correcting the tax position comes before choosing a transfer method, because the bank will ask for the tax record anyway.

Q01Are you a 'domestic individual' (境内个人) for PRC FX purposes?This decides whether the USD 50,000 annual total applies to you, or whether you convert as a foreign individual on documents.The FX Regulations define it as a Chinese citizen or a foreign national continuously resident in China for one full year.Your citizenship, household registration status, and days present in China in the current year.Check your entry/exit records and, if needed, ask the bank's FX desk which article it will apply to your file.S03 S01 S26
Q02Where did the CNY come from?Salary, service income, property sale, inheritance or a gift each reach a different route, and some are capital-account items that cannot use the annual total at all.The routes are split between current-account purchase and capital-account transfer, and the latter needs SAFE approval.The actual origin and the documents that prove it.Gather the contract, payslips, deed, probate or gift record; the bank will ask for exactly this.S01 S04 S26
Q03Has PRC individual income tax been paid or withheld on that income?If it has, there may be nothing left to pay and the tax certificate the bank wants already exists. If it has not, the exposure is tax plus a 0.05 percent per day surcharge and a fine of 50 percent to 5 times the tax.The rates and the penalty rules are published; the tax status of this money is not.Whether tax was withheld, and for which years.Request the individual income tax record from the tax authority (个人所得税纳税记录) and compare it with the income.S19 S20
Q04Is the account in your own name, or do you only have signature authority?FBAR and Form 8938 both turn on financial interest or signature authority, and the answer changes what must be reported.Both tests are published in detail.The account opening documents and the mandate.Read the account mandate; the bank can issue a balance certificate for the relevant year-ends.S15 S16
Q05Did the aggregate balance exceed USD 10,000 at any point in each year?This is the FBAR trigger, independent of income and independent of any transfer.The threshold and the due date, including the automatic extension to 15 October.The maximum balance in each calendar year.Obtain year-end and peak-balance statements from the bank.S15 S16
Q06Which calendar years are involved, and were any FBARs or 8938s filed?Penalties are assessed per year and per violation, so the number of years drives the exposure more than the amount does.The penalty maxima and the reasonable-cause framework.Whether filings were made, and when you first had the account.Check prior returns and FinCEN filings; the IRS streamlined procedures page describes the voluntary route.S17 S16
Q07Do you hold, or can you open, an HSBC account in China and one in the US?This is the only way to reach the free Premier Global Transfer, which is the cheapest published route at exactly USD 10,000.Premier needs a total relationship balance of CNY 500,000; new Advance accounts are restricted.Whether you qualify, and whether HSBC US will open the receiving side.Ask both banks; the balance test is stated in the tariff.S12
Q08Do you need the whole amount in one year, or can it move over several?Pacing decides whether the USD 50,000 annual total is a hard blocker or merely a schedule.The annual total and the day-level outward remittance rule.Your actual cash need.Map the requirement against eight calendar years for USD 400,000 at one person's total.S01
Q09Have you emigrated, with household registration cancelled?That fact unlocks the capital-account route, which is not limited by the annual total and is not staged.The documents required and the abolition of staging.Whether the cancellation certificate exists.Apply to the police for the cancellation record; the SAFE branch will require it.S04 S05
Q10Was any part of the money a gift from a nonresident?A genuine foreign gift above USD 100,000 in the year must be reported on Form 3520, and a purported gift can be recharacterised.The threshold, the aggregation rule and the due date.Whether the transfer was a gift and from whom.Identify the donor and the relationship; keep the transfer records.S18
Q11Where do you live, and how do you file in the US?Form 8938 thresholds differ by filing status and by residence (USD 50,000/75,000 in the US versus 200,000/300,000 abroad).All four threshold pairs.Your filing status and residence.Your last return shows both.S16
Q12Do you have PRC tax records showing tax paid in the last three years?Wise's CNY-out service is limited by exactly that record, and the overall limit is salary minus tax paid.The eligibility test and the CNY 300,000 single-transfer cap.Whether such a record exists and what it shows.Request the tax record; Wise asks for it at verification.S25
Q13When did you acquire the CNY, and at what rate?Section 988 gain or loss is measured between the acquisition rate and the disposition rate, so the date and rate are the whole calculation.The rule that the disposition is a section 988 transaction with ordinary gain or loss.The acquisition date and rate for each tranche.Bank statements and the original conversion receipts establish the basis.S22 S14
Q14What documents exist to prove the source?Every lawful route above the annual total runs on documents; the bank's review is the gate.Which documents each route requires.Whether the documents are complete, translated and notarised where needed.Assemble them before approaching the bank; a partial file is a refusal.S01 S04
Q15Is any part of the balance already invested, lent or held for someone else?That changes both the route and the reporting, and a nominee arrangement can be a separate offence.The capital-account rules and the definition of financial interest.The actual legal and beneficial ownership.Reconstruct the account history from statements.S03 S15
Q16Are you under any PRC tax or FX enquiry already?An open enquiry changes the order of operations and rules out some voluntary routes.SAFE will not accept an emigrant-transfer application while a relevant case is open.Whether any notice has been received.Check with the tax authority and the SAFE branch before filing anything.S04 S20
