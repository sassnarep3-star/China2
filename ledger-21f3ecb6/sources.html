Read 2026-11-11. Two USD 50,000 annual totals exist: one for converting FX into RMB (结汇) and one for a domestic individual buying FX (购汇), each per person per year. Outward remittance from an FX savings account is ID-only up to USD 50,000 per day; above that, authenticity documents are required.

S27Internal Revenue Service (US–China income tax convention, with Senate documents)officialUnited States–People's Republic of China Income Tax Convention (1984, in force 1 January 1987)
Letter of Submittal, 24 July 1984, paragraphs on source rules		Business profits will not be taxable by China unless attributable to a "permanent establishment" … Taxation by China of the remuneration of United States citizens who are self-employed or employed by private firms is generally permissible only if they remain in China more than six months a year.
