Conversion penalty

The 'penalty' is twelve different things, and only two of them are tax

People say converting costs "a lot in taxes". Decomposed, the cost is mostly a bank spread, a commission and market movement. Tax enters only through the income behind the money, and penalties enter only if something was not declared. Each layer below names its source and states whether it is a fee, a tax, a fine, or simply unknown.

The arithmetic on this page is arithmetic, not a determination. Where a tax rate appears, it is the statutory rate applied to a hypothetical amount. No rate is asserted to apply to any person, because the type of income is unknown.

The order of operations that minimises exposure

  1. Establish what the money is. Tax and route both follow from it, and no fee optimisation matters if the route is wrong. S19
  2. If PRC tax is unpaid, deal with that first and voluntarily. The surcharge is 0.05 percent per day and the evasion fine is 50 percent to 5 times the tax; an unprompted correction is a different conversation from an enquiry, and the bank will want the tax record anyway. S20 S04
  3. Then pick the cheapest route you are actually eligible for. On published terms that is Bank of China online from USD 20,000 upward. S10
  4. Then file the US reports the balance triggers, in the year the threshold is crossed, not the year of the transfer. S15 S16
  5. Never use the shortcuts. The fine band is 30-100 percent of the amount, which is 100 to 300 times the cost of doing it lawfully. S03
