What the matrix reveals

  • Only three channels scale without a dollar ceiling: M01 (documented income), M02 (exchange memo) and M04 (existing FX balance). Everything else is capped, slow, or a one-off event.
  • The "quota" is not the main road for a US citizen. If M11 does not apply to you, or it does but is capped at $50,000 a year, the documented route in M01 is the only lawful way to move a large balance in one cycle.
  • Card and cash channels are structurally too small for anything above a few tens of thousands, and they carry separate declaration duties.
  • Rows marked unv are honest gaps, not zeroes: the ceiling depends on a personal fact (how much was originally converted) or on a rule not re-verified in this session.

Research, not a tax opinion. No personalised eligibility, lowest-cost provider or individual tax amount is certified. No funds were moved and no returns were filed. Blank cells are unverified or not stated by any official source — they are not zero.
