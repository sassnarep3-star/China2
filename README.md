## Key Conclusions (Source-Backed, Not a Personal Determination)

1. **No separate CNY→USD conversion tax was identified in the provisions reviewed; that does not make the transaction tax-free.** The analysis must separate underlying PRC income or asset tax, the bank's documentary review, exchange-rate and transfer charges, possible U.S. foreign-currency gain, and reporting duties. SAFE's 13 July 2026 employment-income FAQ requires valid identity documents and transaction-amount/source materials including tax vouchers: https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html . The source, amount and tax history of the user's balance are unknown, so no personal tax amount is stated.
2. **Use the route that matches the source of funds, then obtain an executable bank quote.** For lawful current-account RMB income, assess documented bank conversion and an outbound wire first. Property, inheritance, emigrant assets and prior foreign-currency conversion use different procedures. Citizenship alone does not settle every SAFE classification question. SAFE states that Foreign Permanent Resident ID holders can use the annual purchase facilitation quota; other foreign individuals use documented current-account income procedures: https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0621/2529.html .
3. **Published-input estimates are dated illustrations, not all-in quotes.** The latest route-by-origin playbook computes all 680 method/amount rows from dated bank boards and published tariffs, leaving intermediary and receiving charges blank: [cost tables](playbook-c1d73006/costs.html). Bank of China's fee table publishes a cross-border wire commission of 1‰, CNY 50 minimum/CNY 260 maximum, plus CNY 150 cable outside Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan: https://www.boc.cn/ebanking/service/cs1/200905/t20090506_990712.html . There is **no supported generic 0.30%–1.50% spread assumption** in this summary.
4. **U.S. compliance is separate from moving principal.** FBAR generally applies when aggregate foreign financial accounts exceed USD 10,000 at any time in the year; FATCA/Form 8938 has separate tests. A foreign tax credit may be available only within its rules and not for tax on excluded income: https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-tax-credit . Section 988(e)(2)'s personal-transaction currency-gain exclusion does not apply when gain exceeds USD 200 (2024 GPO edition): https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm . Acquisition dates, basis, filing history and residence are unknown and remain decision inputs.
5. **Avoid evasion channels.** Quota splitting, false documents, underground swaps and prohibited virtual-currency arrangements can create penalties and do not solve the underlying tax/reporting issue. See the site's [Flagged for Review](acceptance-382482c0/index.html#flags) and complete method catalogues before acting.
