KEY FINDING

A conversion threshold is not a tax bill.

The SAFE USD 50,000 annual convenience amount is an FX-administration threshold. China's income-tax rates apply to specified income categories, not automatically to the total value of a bank account.

HOW TO THINK ABOUT YOUR BILL

A useful decomposition

01Tax on original income

Was the CNY earned as salary, interest, dividends, sale gain, inheritance or business proceeds? Identify the relevant Chinese tax base and whether tax was withheld/paid.

02U.S. income & FX result

U.S. citizens report worldwide income. Separately analyze any currency gain/loss and possible foreign tax credit; preserve dated bank and tax records.

03Execution cost

Conversion spread + sending fee + correspondent/intermediary charges + receiving fee. These are provider charges; request a live quote rather than relying on estimates.

Critical point: receiving a demand for “tax” does not establish that it is a tax on conversion. Ask the bank or tax authority to identify the tax and its statutory basis before authorizing deductions. Do not conceal, relabel or route around a valid assessment.
