Q01 Where did the yuan come from?Each origin has its own tax rule and its own exit route: pay, home sale, inheritance, gift, investment income, business profit, or foreign money you brought in and exchanged.Income earned in China under the current account can be converted on documents. Home-sale proceeds and inherited property have separate routes.The origin of this balance, split by amount and year.List every large credit on the bank statements and label its origin. Q02 Was Chinese tax paid on it, and can you prove it?The tax record is the paper the bank asks for. Without it the bank cannot sell you the dollars under the article it works from.Purchase needs source-of-funds papers showing amounts, including tax records. A payer who withholds tax must give a withholding receipt on request.Whether tax was withheld; whether records for those years can still be issued.Ask the employer or payer for withholding records. Ask the tax office that covers the payer for a tax payment record in your name.CN-01 ↗ words CN-18 ↗ words
Q03 How does your bank classify you, and do you hold a Foreign Permanent Resident ID?It decides whether the US$50,000 yearly allowance exists for you at all.The yearly total covers PRC residents. A foreign passport holder gets it only with the permanent resident card. Two official texts define 'domestic individual' differently (flag F12).Your status in the bank's system.Ask the branch: 'Am I recorded as 境外个人? Q04 Were you ever a PRC citizen who emigrated?A former citizen moving pre-emigration property uses the emigrant route, approved by the foreign-exchange office where the household registration was.The route needs proof of cancelled household registration, proof of settlement abroad, proof of ownership and a tax certificate. No tax certificate is needed below CNY 150,000.Whether this applies to you.If yes, ask the SAFE branch of your former household registration for its checklist.CN-08 ↗ words CN-11 ↗ words
Q05 Did the money first arrive as foreign currency that you exchanged into yuan?Unused yuan can be changed back on the original exchange slip alone, with no tax record.The slip is good for 24 months from the exchange date. Without a slip the limit is US$500 a day (US$1,000 on departure, airside).Whether you have the slips and their dates.Ask the bank that did the original exchange for copies of the exchange memos.CN-03 ↗ words
Q06 Which bank holds the money, and what does that branch require and charge?Document lists and fees differ by bank and by branch; published boards are indicative only.Each board read today says its rates are for reference and the dealing rate applies.Your bank, branch, account tier and its checklist.Ask the branch for its written checklist for an overseas individual buying and remitting foreign exchange, and its fee sheet.BK-01 ↗ words BK-03 ↗ words
Q07 How many days were you in China in each tax year?183 days or more in a year makes you a tax resident, taxed on income from inside and outside China; fewer makes you a non-resident, taxed on China-source income only.The 183-day test and the two bases of tax are in Article 1.Your day counts by calendar year.Count from passport stamps or the immigration entry-exit record.CN-12 ↗ words
Q08 Are you in China now, and if not can someone act for you?It decides whether the purchase is done in person or by an agent with papers.An overseas individual's purchase may be entrusted to another person on the supporting documents.Whether your bank accepts an agent and what authorisation it wants.Ask the branch what power of attorney it accepts and whether it must be notarised.CN-13 ↗ words
Q09 How much, and how fast do you need it?No ceiling is written into the article for a documented bank purchase; cash and card routes are capped so low that large sums take years.Card cash withdrawals abroad are capped at RMB 100,000 a year; carrying cash above US$10,000 needs a SAFE permit.Your target amount and deadline.Decide the amount, then read the cost table for that row.CN-06 ↗ words CN-07 ↗ words
Q10 Have you reported the Chinese account to the US every year?Reporting is owed whether or not the account earned income; moving the money makes old gaps visible.FBAR is due when foreign accounts exceed $10,000 in total at any time in the year. Form 8938 has higher thresholds. The IRS says to file late FBARs as soon as possible.Your filing history.Check past returns and FinCEN filings; ask a US tax preparer about late filing if years are missing.US-04 ↗ words US-05 ↗ words
Q11 Was the income behind the money reported on your US returns when it was earned?US tax falls on income when earned, not on the later transfer. Chinese income tax paid on the same income may be credited.Citizens abroad file on worldwide income; foreign income tax can be taken as a credit, with ten years to claim a refund for foreign tax credits.What your past US returns show.Compare each year's Chinese income with the US return for that year.US-08 ↗ words US-09 ↗ words
Q12 What is your dollar cost basis in the yuan?If the yuan is worth more dollars now than when you received it, converting shows a currency gain.Income in foreign money is translated at the rate on the day received. Gain on a personal transaction is ignored only up to $200.Dates and amounts of each receipt.From the statements, list each receipt with its date; a US preparer applies the rates.US-07 ↗ words US-02 ↗ words
Q13 Was any of it a gift or inheritance from someone who is not a US person?Above $100,000 in a year it must be reported on Form 3520 even though it is not income.The threshold is $100,000 from one foreign individual or estate, counting related persons together.Who gave it, when, and how much.Collect the giver's identity and transfer records by year.US-03 ↗ words
Q14 What will the receiving US bank charge and ask?The receiving fee and any intermediary deductions come off the amount that lands.One large US bank publishes $16 for an incoming international wire and says other banks may charge more. Your bank's figure is unknown.Your US bank's fee and wire instructions.Ask your US bank for its incoming international wire fee and exact instructions (SWIFT code, intermediary bank).PV-02 ↗ words
Q15 Is any of the money in a company's name?Company money leaves as profit or payment under company rules, not personal ones.For profit remittances above US$50,000 the bank checks the board resolution and the tax filing form.Whose name is on the account.Check the account name on the statements.CN-09 ↗ words
Q16 Is the money frozen, disputed, or tied to unpaid tax?Disputed or restricted property is not accepted for transfer, and unpaid tax can block departure.SAFE does not accept applications for property restricted by courts or without proven lawful origin.Whether any restriction exists.Ask the bank whether any hold is recorded on the account.CN-08 ↗ words CN-18 ↗ words
