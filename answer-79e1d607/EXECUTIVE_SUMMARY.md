# China money — executive summary (answer-79e1d607)

Read from the publishers' own pages on 2026-11-11. Estimates are arithmetic on published figures, not quotes. Not legal or tax advice.

- **No tax on the conversion itself was found.** In every rule read, the bank's condition for selling dollars to a foreign passport holder is paperwork: valid ID plus source-of-funds papers that show the amounts, including tax records. No rate, levy or percentage on the exchange or the wire appears in those rules. Sources: [CN-01](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html) [CN-05](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/english/130733/2025080815071447034/index.html)
- **The tax that hurts is ordinary Chinese income tax on how the money was first earned or received.** Pay is taxed at 3% to 45%; rent, dividends, gains on selling assets and windfalls at a flat 20%; interest on bank savings is exempt. If that tax was already paid and you can show it, no further Chinese tax on converting was found. If it was never paid, it is still owed whether or not you convert. Sources: [CN-12](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html) [CN-14](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5203286/content.html)
- **Unpaid tax grows fast.** The collection law adds 0.05% of the unpaid tax per day and allows a fine of 50% to five times the tax for not filing. A person who owes tax can be stopped from leaving China. Sources: [CN-18](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5195081/content.html)
- **Cheapest computed lawful route today: Agricultural Bank of China wire.** Exchange-rate margin plus the bank's own wire fee comes to $43.00 on $10,000 and $881.74 on $400,000 (0.430% falling to 0.220%). Fees taken by intermediary banks and by your US bank are not published in advance and are not in these figures. Sources: [BK-08](https://ewealth.abchina.com/app/data/api/DataService/ExchangeRateV2) [BK-07](https://www.abchina.com/cn/PersonalServices/Forex/)
- **The US$50,000 yearly allowance is not yours by default.** The yearly total applies to dollar purchases by PRC residents. A foreign passport holder is covered only when holding a Foreign Permanent Resident ID card; otherwise each purchase rests on documents, with no ceiling written into the article. Sources: [CN-13](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0430/2464.html) [CN-02](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0621/2529.html)
- **On the US side no tax on the transfer itself was found either, but three things can cost money.** None of the IRS pages read taxes the act of moving your own money. The US taxes citizens on worldwide income as it is earned, with a credit available for foreign income tax paid. Foreign accounts above $10,000 in total must be reported every year. And because the yuan is stronger today than the IRS average rate for each of 2022 to 2025, converting now can show a currency gain in dollars; the law waives it only up to $200 per personal transaction. Sources: [US-08](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/us-citizens-and-residents-abroad-filing-requirements) [US-09](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-tax-credit) [US-04](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/report-of-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts-fbar) [US-02](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm)
- **Shortcuts are the expensive option.** Borrowing other people's quotas, underground money changers, false papers and crypto are unlawful in China. Fines run up to 30% of the amount, 30% to 100% in serious cases, with criminal liability possible. Sources: [CN-17](http://www.safe.gov.cn/shanghai/2019/1213/1198.html) [CN-04](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2008/0806/5321.html) [CN-15](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html)
