Source policy

Research claims and table entries link to official government sources: China's SAFE, PBC, STA and GACC; the U.S. IRS and FinCEN. We did not use commercial remittance blogs or estimate bank prices. Some Chinese FX administration pages are historical; later changes are called out and bank/SAFE confirmation is necessary.

Verification method & limits

  • Each displayed research row has a direct official link. Sources were opened and the relevant rule or explanation was checked on the source page where available.
  • Official text is the authority; English translations may be unofficial or lag later Chinese amendments. The SAFE-hosted Chinese implementing rules are included for that reason.
  • Rates are shown only where official laws or tax authorities state them. Empty rate cells mean no official source value was verified; they are not estimates.
  • This review cannot determine the individual account's status, fund origin, taxes paid, bank fees, conversion quote, or approval. Those remain flagged.
  • Sources and policy status reviewed on November 7, 2026. Check each agency's current instructions before acting.
