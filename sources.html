Art. 13: a non-domestic individual may convert legitimate RMB current-account income and remit abroad with valid ID and relevant documents. Art. 7 prohibits evading limits by splitting amounts or fabricating vouchers.re-read this session

Art. 13(1): non-domestic individual converting lawful current-account RMB income presents ID plus supporting documents including tax vouchers (含税务凭证). Art. 13(2): unused RMB from an original conversion may be re-converted against the original exchange memo, valid 24 months; same-day amounts of USD 500 or less (USD 1,000 or less at pre-departure port outlets) need only ID. S03State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)Circular on Regulating Large-sum Overseas Cash Withdrawals with Bank Cards (汇发[2017]29号, English)Arts. I, II, IV, VIIWithdrawals with a domestic bank card shall not exceed the equivalent of RMB 100,000 in every civil year per individual across all cards; excess triggers a ban on overseas cash withdrawal in the current and the second year.
