Exactly how to do it, in order

Two realistic paths with the paper required at each step, plus the questions to put to the bank in writing.

This page is the execution order. It assumes nothing about your facts: where a step depends on a fact only you know, it says so and sends you to the two-branch decision in step 1.

If you are not sure which row you are in, the bank decides it at the counter, and the answer changes the paperwork. Ask them to confirm the characterisation in writing before you assemble the file.

Step 3 — M01 in exact order

  1. Get the tax record. Download the personal income tax payment record (纳税记录) from the national individual income tax app, or request a tax certificate (完税证明) at the tax service hall, for every year the balance represents. This is the document the FX rule names SAFE 2026 guidance S02 Art. 13(1).
  2. Tie the file to the balance. Employment contract or service agreement, payslips or invoices, bank statements showing each credit, and the tax record. Amounts must reconcile. A file that does not add up is refused at the counter, not argued.
  3. Book a counter appointment at the branch that holds the account. Do not rely on a machine or the app for this category.
  4. Ask for a written all-in quote using the wording in the box below. You are entitled to know the applied rate and every deduction line before you instruct the transfer S11 2.2.4.1-2.2.4.2.
  5. Convert and keep the exchange memo (水单). It is proof the conversion was authorised, and it is the document that would allow a re-conversion within 24 months if a transfer fails S02 Art. 13(2).
  6. Instruct the wire to your own US account in the same name. Keep the SWIFT MT103 and the advice. The bank's tariff does not cover what intermediaries deduct S11 Important Note 7; reconcile what actually arrives against the amount sent before you convert the next tranche.
  7. Report on the US side. FBAR by 15 April with an automatic extension to 15 October S15; Form 8938 attached to the return if the threshold applies 26 USC 6038D. Consider a foreign tax credit claim for PRC tax already paid — the mechanics were not re-verified in this session and are flagged on Flagged for Review.

Step 4 — M03 in exact order, if you qualify

  1. Assemble the status documents. Cancelled Chinese household registration certificate from the police, and proof of settlement abroad issued or certified by a Chinese embassy or consulate S06 Art. 8(4). Without these the route is closed; this is the step that stops most applicants.
  2. Realise the assets and pay tax on the realisation. Obtain the tax certificate or tax payment voucher from the competent tax authority where the assets or income are located S06 Art. 8(6).
  3. File one application for the whole amount at the SAFE branch for your former household registration location; a central sub-branch may accept it S06 Arts. 5, 6.
  4. Approval. Up to RMB 500,000 equivalent the local branch approves; above that it goes to SAFE head office S06 Art. 10.
  5. Remit in the staged pattern the measure dictates: no more than half first, no more than half the remainder after one year, the balance after two years; up to RMB 200,000 may go in one transfer after approval S06 Art. 6. This staging is authorised, so it is not structuring.
  6. The bank must pay your own overseas account and may not hand you FX cash in China S06 Art. 11. Keep the approval letter and the capital-account approval document for the second and later tranches.
  7. Report as in step 3.7.

Exactly what to ask the bank, in writing

  1. Is my refusal a tax assessment, a request for a tax certificate, an eligibility decision, or a fee? Please identify which, in writing.
  2. If a tax is said to be due: what is the tax type, the taxpayer, the period, the tax base, the amount and the legal basis? A tax payment is not the same thing as a tax certificate, and neither is the same thing as a bank fee.
  3. Under which article is my conversion being handled — Art. 12 (domestic individual, above the annual total) or Art. 13(1) (non-domestic individual, lawful current-account income) S02?
  4. Please list the source-of-funds documents you will accept, item by item.
  5. Please give an executable quote showing: the CNY/USD rate applied, the commission with its floor and cap, the cable charge, any intermediary or correspondent charge, and what the receiving bank will deduct S11.
  6. If you will not process it, please confirm that in writing, and tell me which document would change the answer.

A refusal that cannot be put in writing in those terms is usually an incomplete file, not a legal prohibition. That distinction is the whole practical content of this project.

What not to do, and why

  • Do not use a friend's or relative's USD 50,000 allowance. Borrowing or lending a quota puts you on SAFE's watch list for three years S09 point 2, and splitting to evade the limits is expressly prohibited S01 Art. 7.
  • Do not use an underground bank or a private swap. Fines up to 30% of the amount, higher where serious, plus confiscation S05 Art. 45.
  • Do not use crypto or USDT. Converting between legal tender and virtual currency is expressly prohibited, and offshore exchanges serving China residents are treated as illegal financial activity S21 points 1(2)-(3).
  • Do not manufacture documents. Illegal FX arbitrage: the funds are ordered converted back and a fine of up to 30% applies, plus up to CNY 50,000 for untrue documents S05 Arts. 40, 48.
  • Do not carry more than USD 10,000 out. USD 5,000 without a permit, USD 10,000 with one, and in principle nothing above that S07; up to 20% fine for breach S05 Art. 42.
  • Do not skip the FBAR because nothing moved. The obligation attaches to the account, not to the transaction S15.
