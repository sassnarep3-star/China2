What this page is for

Every value on this site that could not be confirmed from an official primary source is listed here with the reason it is unconfirmed. Blank means unverified, not zero, and not free. Nothing on this page was filled in by inference, and nothing here is hidden in a footnote. There are 12 substantive flags and 15 method-level cost gaps.

Two of these matter far more than the rest: F01 (the spread is one bank's board rate, applied as an assumption) and F03 (whether your balance can be documented at all is unknown). If you read nothing else here, read those two.

high F01 — The spread used in every cost table is an assumption drawn from one bank's board rate

The 0.419% spread comes from Bank of China's published USD board rates on 10 November 2026 (buy 665.72, sell 668.52 per 100 USD), on a page that states the table is for reference only and that the rate actually applied at the counter or in the app governs. It is applied uniformly to every method and every tier. Your bank's executable rate will differ, and the spread dominates the total at large amounts.

high F02 — No intermediary or receiving-bank fee could be sourced

The HSBC China tariff expressly excludes charges collected by other institutions and by regulators. No official source publishes what a US receiving bank or a correspondent will deduct. Those cells are blank, not zero, and the totals on this site are therefore lower bounds for the delivered amount.

Sources: S11

high F03 — Whether your specific balance is documentation-eligible is unknown

Every lawful route in this guide turns on documents: a PRC tax record, a source-of-funds file, cancelled household registration, or an original exchange memo. None of those facts were supplied. The guide states what each rule requires and does not conclude that any particular balance qualifies.

Sources: S02 · S03 · S06

high F04 — The U.S. foreign tax credit mechanics were not re-verified in this session

The PRC tax already paid on the income may be creditable in the US by treaty or under section 901/903, and Form 1116 is the usual mechanism. That path was named but not read in this session, so no conclusion is drawn and no credit amount is computed.

Sources: S15

medium F05 — Whether you are a domestic or a non-domestic individual under PRC FX law is unknown

The operative rule differs: a domestic individual's FX purchases face the annual total (S02 Art. 12), while a non-domestic individual converts lawful current-account income on identity plus source and tax documents (S02 Art. 13(1); S03). The test turns on residence and household registration, neither of which is known.

Sources: S02 · S03 · S05

medium F06 — Earlier pages in this repository carried a superseded Global Transfer fee

A "USD 0-7" same-name Global Transfer figure and a "USD 7" reference appear in earlier material here. In the tariff effective 31 October 2026 the USD 7 figure belongs to International Education Payment (item 2.2.7), and same-name Global Transfer is Free for Private Banking and Premier, with Advance at CNY 145 indicative / actual USD 20 (item 2.2.6.1). This guide uses the current text. The earlier pages were not edited by this session because they are outside its scope.

Sources: S11

medium F07 — An earlier page's section 988 illustration did not reproduce exactly

An earlier page stated a +0.83% exchange gain for renminbi acquired in 2024 and a USD 200 threshold at about $23,963. Recomputing from the same IRS yearly averages gives +0.84% and a threshold of about $23,800. The difference is rounding. This guide publishes its own recomputation on the Tax page rather than restating the earlier figure.

Sources: S14 · S12

medium F08 — The emigration route's practical availability was not tested

The measure requires cancelled Chinese household registration and certified proof of settlement abroad. Many naturalised US citizens no longer hold the first document. The rule is verified; whether it is achievable for any particular person is not, and no adviser or SAFE branch was contacted.

Sources: S06

Whether, and on what basis, an individual may move a personal RMB balance offshore outside the documented current-account and capital-account channels was not established from the sources read. The method is listed as conditional with no cost, not as a recommendation.
