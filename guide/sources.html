S01 Administrative Rules on Individuals' Foreign Exchange (PBOC Decree [2006] No. 3)
People's Bank of China · regulation · EN
 Articles 3, 7, 9, 13, 14, 15 Article 3: "Full convertibility principle shall apply to individuals' foreign exchange business under the current account." Article 7: banks and individuals "must not evade limit administration by splitting large amount into small batches or tamper with authenticity administration by using fabricated commercial bills or vouchers." Article 9: "An individual's foreign exchange sales and domestic individual's foreign exchange purchases shall be imposed an annual limit." Article 13: "A non-domestic individual can convert legitimate renminbi income obtained in China under the current account into foreign exchange and remit abroad at a bank by showing valid identity documents and relevant documents." Legal basis that current-account conversion is a documentation question, not a prohibited act; the anti-splitting rule; the annual limit exists for sales and for a domestic individual's purchases.
S02 Implementation Rules of the Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange (Hui Fa [2007] No. 1)
State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) · regulation · ZH
 Articles 2, 4, 10, 11, 13, 14, 15, 20, 30, 31, 38 Art. 2: "对个人结汇和境内个人购汇实行年度总额管理。年度总额分别为每人每年等值5万美元。" Art. 13(1): "在境内取得的经常项目合法人民币收入，凭本人有效身份证件和有交易额的相关证明材料（含税务凭证）办理购汇。" Art. 13(2): re-conversion against the original memo, valid "自兑换日起24个月", with ID-only handling for same-day totals up to USD 500 (USD 1,000 at port-side locations). Art. 14: FX account remittance abroad up to USD 50,000 same-day on ID; above that, authenticity documents. Art. 20: emigration/inheritance transfers follow the separate 2004 measure. Art. 38: a direct relative (parents, children, spouse) may act for you within the annual total on ID + authorisation + relationship proof. Scope of the USD 50,000 annual total; the documentary requirement (including tax vouchers) for converting lawful current-account RMB; 24-month re-conversion; relative agency.
S03 2026 FX administration internet information - individual FX (08): how a non-domestic individual purchases FX to remit labour remuneration earned in China
SAFE Tianjin branch · official-guidance · ZH
 Answer quoting Guideline Hui Fa [2020] No. 14, Art. 59 "境外个人在境内取得的经常项目合法人民币收入，凭本人有效身份证件和有交易额的购汇资金来源材料（含税务凭证）在银行办理购汇" - a non-domestic individual's lawful current-account RMB income in China is purchased into FX at the bank on valid ID plus source-of-funds materials proving the transaction amount, including tax vouchers. Current (2026) restatement that a US citizen characterised as a non-domestic individual converts lawful, documented, taxed income without using a USD 50,000 purchase quota.
S04 2026 FX administration internet information - individual FX (10): converting unused RMB back to FX
SAFE Tianjin branch · official-guidance · ZH
 Answer quoting Guideline Hui Fa [2020] No. 14, Art. 60 "境外个人原兑换未用完的人民币兑回外汇，凭本人有效身份证件和原兑换水单办理，原兑换水单的兑回有效期为自兑换日起24个月；对于当日累计兑换不超过等值500美元（含）以及离境前在境内关外场所当日累计不超过等值1000美元（含）的兑换，可凭本人有效身份证件办理。" The cleanest documentary route when the RMB originally came from FX you brought in: original exchange memo, 24 months.
S05 Regulations of the PRC on Foreign Exchange Administration (State Council Decree No. 532)
State Council of the PRC (published by SAFE) · regulation · ZH
 Articles 5, 8, 12, 14, 39, 40, 41, 42, 45, 48 Art. 5: "国家对经常性国际支付和转移不予限制。" Art. 12: current-account FX receipts and payments must have a "真实、合法的交易基础". Art. 39 (evasion, 逃汇): fine of up to 30% of the evaded amount, and where serious 30% up to the equivalent of the full amount. Art. 40 (illegal FX arbitrage, 非法套汇): same range, and the funds must be converted back. Art. 41/42/45: illegal conversion, illegal carrying of cash, and private/parallel FX dealing each carry fines of up to 30% of the amount involved. Art. 48: individuals may be fined up to CNY 50,000 for failing to submit valid documents or submitting untrue documents. The statutory penalty ranges used in the penalty column, and the statement that no separate tax on conversion appears in the FX instruments.
S06 Provisional Measures for the Administration of Sale and Payment of Foreign Exchange for Individuals' Outward Asset Transfers (2004)
People's Bank of China · regulation · ZH
 Articles 2, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13 Art. 6: "移民转移必须一次性申请拟转移出境的全部财产金额，分步汇出。首次可汇出金额不得超过全部申请转移财产的一半；自首次汇出满一年后，可汇出不超过剩余财产的一半；自首次汇出满两年后，可汇出全部剩余财产。全部申请转移财产在等值人民币20万元以下（含20万元）的，经批准后可一次性汇出。" Art. 10: applications up to RMB 500,000 are approved by the local SAFE branch; above that, by SAFE head office. Art. 11: the bank must remit directly to the applicant's own overseas account and may not pay out FX cash inside China. Art. 8(6)/9(5) require a tax certificate or tax-payment voucher. The emigration/inheritance channel: unlimited in principle, but approved and staged over up to two years, with a tax certificate required.
S07 Provisional Measures on Carrying Foreign Currency Cash Across the Border (Hui Fa [2003] No. 102)
SAFE and General Administration of Customs · regulation · ZH
 Notice points 2 and 3 "出境人员携带不超过等值5000美元（含5000美元）的外币现钞出境的，无须申领《携带证》，海关予以放行；出境人员携带外币现钞金额在等值5000美元以上至10000美元（含10000美元）的，应向外汇指定银行申领《携带证》，海关凭加盖外汇指定银行印章的《携带证》验放；出境人员原则上不得携带超过等值10000美元的外币现钞出境" (with listed exceptions that require a SAFE-issued permit). The 5,000 / 10,000 USD cash-carry limits used by the cash methods.
S08 Notice on Regulating Large Cash Withdrawals Abroad with Bank Cards (Hui Fa [2017] No. 29)
SAFE · regulation · ZH
 Points 1 and 4 Point 1: "个人持境内银行卡在境外提取现金，本人名下银行卡（含附属卡）合计每个自然年度不得超过等值10万元人民币。超过年度额度的，本年及次年将被暂停持境内银行卡在境外提取现金。" Point 4: foreign-currency cards, per card per day, "不得超过等值1万元人民币". The hard annual ceiling on the ATM route: CNY 100,000 equivalent per person per calendar year, with a two-year suspension for exceeding it.
S09 Notice on Further Improving Individual Foreign Exchange Administration (Hui Fa [2015] No. 49)
SAFE · regulation · ZH
 Point 2 "个人在办理外汇业务时，应当遵守个人外汇管理有关规定，不得以分拆等方式规避额度及真实性管理。" Lending your own quota to help another person evade the limit management triggers a risk notice, and a repeat puts you on the "关注名单" (watch list); borrowing another person's quota puts you on the watch list, for that year plus the following two years. Why quota-splitting and quota-borrowing are separately penalised even when each individual transaction looks compliant.
S10 Individual FX in plain terms (2019): the USD 50,000 annual total and prohibited uses
SAFE Shanghai branch · official-guidance · ZH
 Points (1)-(3) and closing paragraph "境内外个人结汇、境内个人购汇年度总额分别为每人每年等值5万美元。" - and "个人购付汇不得用于境外购房、证券投资、购买人寿保险和投资性返还分红类保险等资本项目业务。" Card spending abroad repaid in FX after return does not count against the annual total. The prohibited declared purposes (overseas property, securities, life/endowment insurance) and the card-spending carve-out.

S12 26 U.S.C. 988 - Treatment of certain foreign currency transactions (U.S. Code 2024 edition)
U.S. Congress / GPO (govinfo) · statute · EN
 sec. 988(c)(1)(C)(i)-(ii), 988(e)(1)-(3) 988(c)(1)(C)(i): "In the case of any disposition of any nonfunctional currency - (I) such disposition shall be treated as a section 988 transaction". 988(e)(1): "The preceding provisions of this section shall not apply to any section 988 transaction entered into by an individual which is a personal transaction." 988(e)(2): "no gain shall be recognized for purposes of this subtitle by reason of changes in exchange rates after such currency was acquired by such individual and before such disposition. The preceding sentence shall not apply if the gain which would otherwise be recognized on the transaction exceeds $200." 988(e)(3) excludes transactions whose expenses meet section 162 or 212 requirements (other than travel, or the tax part of 212). The only U.S. provision that can tax a pure currency conversion by an individual on a personal transaction, and its USD 200 threshold.
S13 Publication 525 (2025), Taxable and Nontaxable Income - Other Income: Foreign currency transactions
Internal Revenue Service · guidance · EN
 Other Income, 'Foreign currency transactions' "If you have a gain on a personal foreign currency transaction because of changes in exchange rates, you don't have to include that gain in your income unless it's more than $200. If the gain is more than $200, report it as a capital gain." Plain-language IRS restatement of the USD 200 personal-transaction exclusion. Note the wording difference from 26 U.S.C. 988(e)(2): the publication says to report it as a capital gain, while the statute speaks of recognition of gain; both are cited rather than reconciled here.

S15 Report of Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts (FBAR)
Internal Revenue Service · guidance · EN
 Who must file; When to file; Penalties "A U.S. person ... must file an FBAR to report ... a financial interest in or signature or other authority over at least one financial account located outside the United States if the aggregate value of those foreign financial accounts exceeded $10,000 at any time during the calendar year reported." Due "April 15 following the calendar year reported" with an automatic extension to October 15. "Whether the account produced taxable income has no effect on whether the account is a foreign financial account for FBAR purposes." Civil maximums "are adjusted annually for inflation". The FBAR obligation that exists whether or not you move any money, and its due dates.

S17 31 CFR 1010.350 - Reports of foreign financial accounts
FinCEN via the eCFR (31 CFR 1010.350) · regulation · EN
 Paragraphs (a), (b), (c)(1), (e)(1) (a) "Each United States person having a financial interest in, or signature or other authority over, a bank, securities, or other financial account in a foreign country shall report such relationship to the Commissioner of Internal Revenue for each year in which such relationship exists". (b)(1) a United States person includes "A citizen of the United States". S18 26 U.S.C. 6038D - Information with respect to foreign financial assets (Form 8938)
U.S. Congress / GPO (govinfo) · statute · EN
 6038D(a), (d)(1), (d)(2), (g) (a) an individual must attach the information "if the aggregate value of all such assets exceeds $50,000". (d)(1) "such person shall pay a penalty of $10,000". S19 Do I need to file Form 8938, Statement of Specified Foreign Financial Assets?
Internal Revenue Service · guidance · EN
 Thresholds, third bullet group "Unmarried taxpayers living in the US: The total value of your specified foreign financial assets is more than $50,000 on the last day of the tax year or more than $75,000 at any time during the tax year". Married filing jointly, living in the US: "more than $100,000 on the last day of the tax year or more than $150,000 at any time". Taxpayers living abroad filing other than jointly: "more than $200,000 on the last day of the tax year or more than $300,000 at any time". The 8938 thresholds by filing status and residence.
S20 Money and Other Monetary Instruments (FinCEN Form 105 reporting)
U.S. Customs and Border Protection · guidance · EN
 Federal Mandate to Report Currency Exceeding $10,000; Who Must File; What if I don't file "Federal law mandates that when entering or leaving the United States you must report amounts exceeding $10,000 to U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP)."
