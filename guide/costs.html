Fee, spread and total for each method, with a source link in every row. Blank cells are values no official source publishes.

Most important first: what the cost actually is

Converting USD 400,000 through the documented-income wire costs $1,763.32 in published fees plus spread — about 0.441%. That is the honest answer to "how big is the conversion penalty": it is a fraction of a percent, not a confiscation. If you have been quoted something much larger, the large part is almost certainly a tax on the underlying income, a fine, or somebody's fee for an unlawful workaround — not the price of exchanging currency.

The second thing to know: the lawful routes cost almost the same as each other. Most of them pay the same published commission and the same CNY 120 cable charge S11 and the same spread, so the choice between them is about whether you qualify, not about price. Only two things move the total materially: a route whose bank fee is published Free (a same-name HSBC Global Transfer, or money that is already in USD), and leaving the money in renminbi and spending it there.

At the small end the fixed fee dominates: USD 10,000 costs $72.89 (0.729%), because the CNY 120 cable charge and the CNY 100 minimum commission are the same whatever the amount S11.

What these numbers are not

  • Not a quote. No bank was asked for an executable rate. The spread cell is one bank's published board rate from 10 Nov 2026, labelled and applied uniformly S22.
  • Not all-in. Intermediary and receiving-bank fees are outside the tariff S11 Important Note 7, and no published figure for them was located. They are blank, not zero.
  • Not tax. No PRC income tax and no personal U.S. tax is in these totals. If the underlying income was never taxed in China, the tax is on the income (up to 45% on comprehensive income under IIT Law Art. 3(1)), and it dwarfs every number in these tables. See Tax & penalties.
  • Not available for the blank rows. Fifteen of the twenty-seven methods have no published fee in the official sources read. They are listed on Flagged for Review rather than filled in.
