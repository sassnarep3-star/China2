Q01 Where did the RMB come from — salary, business income, sale of property, inheritance, gift, investment?Decides the route: salary → bank purchase with tax proof (M01–M04); pre-emigration property → M05; unproven source is refused.A foreigner's lawful work pay can be bought as FX with ID and source documents including tax records [S10]; emigrant property needs source and title proof [S14]; unproven-source property is refused [S13 Art. 12].The origin of your balance.List each deposit by date and source; gather contracts, payslips, sale deeds or estate papers.S10S13S14
Q02 Was PRC income tax paid on it, and can you get a tax certificate (完税证明)?The bank's documentary check and the emigrant route both ask for tax proof.Tax proof is listed for foreigners' purchases [S08 Art. 13, S10] and for emigrant transfers [S14]. PRC IIT is 3%–45% on comprehensive income and 20% on interest, dividends and property transfers [S20 Art. 3].Whether tax was withheld on each source and whether any is outstanding.Ask the employer/payer for withholding records; ask the local tax office for a 完税证明 covering the years concerned.S08S10S14S20
Q03 Are you a 'domestic' or 'foreign' individual under the FX rules?The USD 50,000 annual facilitation amount is framed for domestic individuals; foreigners buy against documents.境内个人 includes foreigners resident in China for one continuous year or more [S09 Art. 52(2)]; foreign permanent-residence card holders get the annual quota [S11]; foreigners buy FX for lawful income with ID and income/tax proof [S08 Art. 13].Your residence history and card type; how your bank classifies you.Check your residence permits and entry/exit record; ask the bank which category its system shows for your ID.S08S09S11
Q04 Were you ever a PRC citizen with a mainland hukou?Only emigrants can use the SAFE emigrant property transfer (M05).Approval by the SAFE office of the original hukou; free; 5 + 20 working days; hukou cancellation and foreign settlement proof required [S12, S14].Your citizenship history.Locate the hukou cancellation record (户口注销证明) and US naturalisation certificate.S12S14
Q05 Which bank holds the money, and will it sell USD and wire for your ID type online?Fees and spreads differ: BOC caps at CNY 410 per wire, HSBC at CNY 620; spreads 0.21% vs 0.50% today.BOC fee and rate [S01, S03]; HSBC fee and rate [S02, S04].Your bank (if neither) and its per-day / per-transaction limits.Ask the bank for its current tariff and online limits for passport holders; compare with S03/S04.S01S02S03S04
Q06 Do you have a US account in your own name to receive the wire (and an HSBC US account for M03)?PRC rules send emigrant funds only to the applicant's own overseas account; Wise pays only to your own account.Own-account rule for emigrant transfers [S13 Art. 11]; Wise own-account rule [S05].Your receiving bank and its incoming-wire fee.Ask your US bank for its incoming international wire fee and SWIFT details.S05S13
Q07 How much, and how fast?Caps make some routes impractical for large sums.ATM cap RMB 100,000/year [S16]; cash exit USD 5,000 without permit [S17]; Wise CNY 300,000 per transfer [S05].Your total and deadline.Use the tier tables on costs.html to see how many years / trips / transfers each route needs.S05S16S17
Q08 Have you filed FBAR (FinCEN 114) and Form 8938 for the years the account was large enough?Missed US reports are the largest potential 'penalty' — not the transfer itself.FBAR above USD 10,000 aggregate at any time [S21]; non-wilful maximum USD 16,536, wilful USD 165,353 [S22]; Form 8938 thresholds and USD 10,000 penalty [S23]; streamlined procedures exist for non-wilful cases [S30].Your filing history and maximum balances by year.Get year-end and peak balances from the PRC bank for each year; check past returns for Form 8938 and BSA E-Filing for FBARs.S21S22S23S30
Q09 At what USD/CNY rate did you acquire the RMB?Under §988(e)(2) personal FX gain is ignored only up to USD 200 per conversion; the RMB has strengthened since 2025 (IRS 2025 average 7.129 vs BOC 6.6886 today).§988(e)(2) USD 200 rule [S27]; IRS yearly averages and no official rate [S07]; today's BOC rate [S01].Your acquisition dates and rates (basis).Pull deposit dates from statements and the rate on each date (bank board or the rate you used consistently [S07]).S01S07S27
Q10 Is any of the money a gift or inheritance from a non-US person?Large foreign gifts trigger Form 3520 even though no tax is due on the gift itself.Form 3520 for more than USD 100,000 from a nonresident alien; USD 20,573 (2026) from foreign corporations/partnerships [S24]; §6039F threshold USD 21,240.31 for 2027 [S26].Whether gifts are involved and from whom.Identify each gift by donor and year.S24S26
Q11 Were you a PRC tax resident (183+ days) in the years the money was earned?Determines what PRC tax applied to worldwide vs China-source income.183-day test [S20 Art. 1].Days present per year.Count days from passport stamps / entry-exit records.S20
Q12 Is part of the money foreign earned income for a year you can use the FEIE or the foreign tax credit?These change the US tax on the income itself (not on moving it).FEIE 2026 USD 132,900 [S25]; 2027 USD 137,300 [S26 §3.41]; no credit for tax on excluded income [S29].Your bona fide residence / physical presence and which years.Check Form 2555 tests for each year with a preparer.S25S26S29
