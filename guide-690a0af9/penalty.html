The “conversion penalty”, layer by layer

There is no single penalty. These are the separate costs and risks people lump together.

LayerSizeWhat the official text saysSources
P1 · Tax on the act of convertingNone foundNone of the official pages read today imposes a tax on exchanging CNY for USD. Current-account payments are not restricted [S09 Art. 5]; what applies is a documentary test [S08 Art. 13, S10].S09S08S10

P5 · PRC income tax on the underlying income3%–45% (comprehensive) · 20% (interest, dividends, property transfer)A tax on how the money was earned, not on moving it [S20 Art. 3]. Outward payments over USD 50,000 for income items need a tax filing; a domestic individual's own funds for personal use are exempt; foreigners follow the personal FX rules [S19].S20S19
P6 · US tax on exchange gainGain above USD 200 per conversion is not excluded§988(e)(2) [S27]. With the RMB stronger than its 2025 IRS average (7.129) [S07] than the BOC sell rate used here [S01], RMB acquired earlier can show a USD gain on conversion. The tax rate on that gain depends on your return — blank (flag F-11).S27S07S01
P7 · US reporting penalties (only if you fail to file)FBAR non-wilful ≤ USD 16,536 · wilful USD 165,353 · Form 8938 USD 10,000–60,000[S22, S23]. Filing is free; the risk is not filing.S21S22S23
P9 · US excise tax on the transfer (new in 2026)1% — but only when the sender pays in cash or a cash-like instrumentIRS: from 1 Jan 2026 a 1% remittance transfer tax applies to remittances sent from the United States when the sender provides cash, a money order, a cashier's check or a similar physical instrument [S31, S32]. The funding source decides it: a transfer paid from a bank account or by card is outside the charge, and a China→US transfer is not a remittance "sent from the United States" in the first place. Applies to you only if you fund a US-side transfer with cash (flag F-19).S31S32
P8 · PRC penalties (only on unlawful routes)Up to 30% of the amount; 30%–100% in serious evasion cases; watch list 3 years[S09 Arts. 39, 45; S15]. Crypto routes are illegal outright [S18].S09S15S18
