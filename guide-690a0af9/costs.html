The one US tax on a transfer amount — and why it should not apply to you

From 1 January 2026 a 1% excise tax applies to remittances sent from the United States when the sender pays in cash, a money order, a cashier's check or a similar physical instrument S31S32. A bank-funded or card-funded transfer is outside the charge, and a China→US repatriation is not a remittance sent from the United States.
