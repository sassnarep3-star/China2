Executive summary

First investigate a documented bank transfer, not a workaround. If the funds are eligible lawful current-account income such as Chinese salary, SAFE says an overseas individual can buy foreign exchange at a bank with valid ID and transaction-amount evidence of the source, including tax documentation. That is a conditional route—not a finding that this particular balance qualifies or that no tax is owed. SAFE Tianjin, 2026

Do not assume that a tax receipt is a new tax on currency conversion, or that furnishing it means all taxes have already been paid. Underlying income, gain on currency, reporting and transaction costs are separate questions. The U.S. foreign tax credit can reduce double taxation where requirements are met, but it is not automatic and cannot be claimed on excluded income. IRS foreign tax credit · IRS currency guidance

How the leading route would work

  1. Classify and document the origin of each amount, holder identity and lawful tax treatment. The bank must be satisfied about authenticity; reconstruct old salary documents if needed. SAFE.
  2. Ask the account-holding branch whether it accepts the current-account income application, which specific documents and authorizations it requires, and whether the USD may be sent to the intended account. SAFE.
  3. Request a live conversion-and-wire quote, including all known sender, intermediary and receiving fees, and expected net proceeds; compare eligible banks without assuming the account can be moved or a reference rate is executable. BOC · ABC.
  4. Review U.S. income, currency-gain and reporting implications with qualified advisers; submit the bank's required paperwork, convert and send only after approval, and retain receipts and wire confirmation. IRS.
