T05Foreign tax creditSource-backedEligible foreign income taxes may reduce U.S. income tax subject to rules and limits; Form 1116 is generally used for individuals. No credit for taxes on FEIE-excluded income. Bank charges are not automatically eligible income taxes.

Benefit: Lawful way to address potential double taxation.

Caution: Not an automatic full offset; categories, timing, source and treaty entitlement matter.
