C10Effective execution drag: analytical formulaAnalysisLet A be total CNY debited, q the simultaneous reference CNY per USD, and N final USD credited. Benchmark USD = A/q. Execution drag = A/q − N; drag % = 100 × (1 − Nq/A). Derived comparison formula, not an official fee or tax rule.

Caution: Requires confirmed A, q and N. Source taxes and later U.S. taxes are separate; avoid double-counting fees already included in A or N.
