How this was verified

Each published research row was compared with the cited official page body or specified PDF sections. The ledger records the authority, relevant passage, review date and limitation. Search results were discovery aids, not final evidence. No current price is inferred from a marketing example.

“Official” includes a bank or provider speaking about its own product; this is primary product evidence, not government endorsement. Review flags distinguish source conflicts, incomplete legal currency checks and unknown private facts. Citation coverage tests are structural checks—not an independent legal audit.

Outbound browsing through the shell is restricted in this environment. Official-page retrieval used the research fetch tool. Local/CI checks validate internal links and citation presence; external site availability is not continuously guaranteed.
