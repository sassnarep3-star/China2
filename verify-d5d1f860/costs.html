Most important first: the five checkpoints as mobile cards, then the complete 680-row ladder. Every published fee is recomputed from the tariff text, not copied; every unpublished component is blank and flagged. Download the ladder (CSV).

What "published fee" means here. It is the transfer/issuance fee a provider prints in a public tariff (for HSBC, 2.2.4.1–2.2.4.2). It is not the all-in cost: the FX spread, correspondent and recipient bank charges, and any tax are separate. USD restatement uses 7.129 CNY/USD (IRS 2025 average). Where a method publishes nothing, the cell stays blank.

Bank used for the fee model: HSBC China, because it is the only institution in the reviewed sources that publishes a complete per-wire schedule. If your account is at ICBC, BOC, CCB, CMB or another bank, its tariff (and its minimum/maximum and cable charge) may differ and must be obtained directly — the arithmetic template here still applies.
