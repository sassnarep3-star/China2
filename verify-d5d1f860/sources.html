Source hierarchy used

  • Primary regulator / statute: SAFE, PBOC, State Council, State Taxation Administration, IRS, CBP, FinCEN/eCFR, GPO. These govern the claim.
  • Provider primary documents: HSBC China tariff, Wise help centre, Western Union China page. These state that provider's fees and limits — not a regulator's rule, and not your account's terms.
  • IRS exchange-rate convention: the 2025 yearly average is used only to restate CNY fees. It is neither a live rate nor the rate your bank will apply.

Not used as authority: news articles, blogs, forums, tax-preparer marketing, or any AI-generated figure. Where the only available statement was secondary, the value was left blank.

Known weaknesses of these sources

  • The eCFR is authoritative but not the official print edition (VS16); the GPO annual CFR is. 26 USC §988 here is the 2024 edition (VS15).
  • The IRS FEIE web page is stale — it still lists 2020–2023 amounts (VS20); the 2026 figure comes from the Form 2555 instructions (VS19).
  • The RMB 20,000 carry limit is supported by a 2004 PBC explanatory Q&A (VS09), not by re-reading Announcement [2004] No. 18 itself. The instrument number is flagged.
  • The HSBC PDF contains an internal Chinese-language header dated 2024 alongside the 31 October 2026 effective date; the tariff is used as published but that inconsistency is flagged.

Scope and limits. Every source on this site was fetched and quoted on 2026-11-10; the quote shown is the text relied on.
