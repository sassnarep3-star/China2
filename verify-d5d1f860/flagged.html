How to close a flag

  1. Provide the bank's written, itemised quote (rate, sender fee, cable, correspondent, recipient) → F02, F03, F08 close.
  2. Provide the PRC tax records for the balance → F01 partially closes and M01 eligibility can be assessed.
  3. Confirm your HSBC segment and account type → F03 closes for your case.
  4. Confirm your category (former PRC resident, inheritor, permanent-resident card holder) → M02/M10/M11 eligibility resolves.

Until then, this site will keep the cells blank.
