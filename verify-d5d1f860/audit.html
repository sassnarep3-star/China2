Repository audit: what the existing sites get right, and where they drift

This repository contains seven prior dashboards built by different sessions. They are preserved, not overwritten. This page records what an independent read of the published claims found on 2026-11-10, and what this dashboard does differently. Nothing below is an accusation of bad faith — several items are already flagged by their own authors.

Scope. Read-only review of the checked-in HTML/data plus the primary sources in the ledger. I did not re-verify every row of every prior site; structural checks in those repos do not establish factual accuracy, and this audit does not claim to be exhaustive.

AU-01Cross-site method-count drift
Sites in this repository state 22, 20, 18, 17 and 13 methods/legal routes for the same question. Each has its own scope note, but a reader landing on the hub cannot tell which count is canonical.
Open / documentedThis dashboard reconciles to the 17-method set in repatriate/data/methods.json, which is the only set with a complete 40-tier cost ladder.
AU-02Discounting the fee illustration's customer segment
The headline "$30.86–$86.97 per wire" is HSBC Premier/Advance. HSBC's own tariff makes telegraphic transfer issuance and cable charges Free for Private Banking individual customers, so the illustration does not apply to that segment.
Corrected hereSegment is stated on every cost row; the Private Banking case is shown separately as $0.00.
AU-03Stale IRS FEIE page cited for a current figure
irs.gov's FEIE page still prints 2020–2023 amounts ($120,000 for 2023). Any 2026 amount derived from it would be wrong.
Flagged2026 amount taken from Form 2555 instructions ($132,900) and the stale page is cited only as evidence of staleness.
AU-04Absence of a cap presented as an entitlement
SAFE's guidance for foreign individuals' documented income states documents, not a dollar ceiling. Several pages summarise this as "no annual cap", which is an inference from silence, not an affirmative unlimited right.
FlaggedRendered as "no numeric cap appears in the cited text; the bank's review decides".
AU-05"$0" published fees for cash routes
Method pages show a published bank fee of zero for buying/carrying cash. Spread, travel and risk are not zero and are not published; a reader can misread $0 as free.
FlaggedMarked "$0.00 †" with the unverified components named in the same row.
AU-06Mutually inconsistent dashboards
docs/, site/, portal/, repatriate/, ladder/, briefing/ and audit-*/ each present overlapping claim sets with different corrections applied. Several earlier pages were superseded rather than withdrawn.
Open / documentedThis site links the repository hub and states its own scope; it does not silently re-endorse other pages.
AU-07The "no conversion tax" headline is true but incomplete
The statement is supported for the act of conversion, but it reads as an all-clear. The real exposure is PRC income tax already owed, FX spread, US reporting penalties and FX-evasion fines.
Corrected hereFive-layer decomposition on the Tax page, each layer separately sourced.
AU-08Unrealistic single-wire assumption at the top of the ladder
The $400,000 row assumes one wire. Banks commonly split large remittances; each extra wire re-applies the ¥100 floor (or ¥500 cap) and ¥120 cable.
FlaggedSingle-wire assumption disclosed; multi-wire totals are not invented.
AU-09Bank tariff cited as if it were the cost of the transfer
An HSBC tariff line is a published charge of one bank for one segment, excluding correspondent, recipient and third-party charges (tariff Important Note 7) and excluding taxes.
Corrected here"Published fee" is a named column, never presented as all-in cost.
AU-10No live link-health check in prior CI
Earlier verifiers checked that a URL string was present and looked official, not that the document still exists or says what is claimed. A 404 or rewritten page would pass.
Improved24 documents fetched and quote-checked by hand on 2026-11-10; this is a point-in-time check, not continuous monitoring.
AU-11Instrument-number precision on the RMB carry limit
The CNY 20,000 limit is attributed to PBOC Announcement [2004] No. 18, but the fetched PBC page is an explanatory Q&A that states the 20,000 figure without reproducing the announcement.
FlaggedMarked "verified · limited" with the limitation on the source row.
AU-12Different exchange-rate conventions across pages
Some pages restate CNY fees at 6.9, others at 7.129. Mixing conventions changes USD figures by several percent and makes cross-page comparison unreliable.
Corrected hereOne rate (7.129, IRS 2025 average) declared once and repeated in every table footnote.

Structural observations

  • Seven dashboards, one question. docs/ · site/ · portal/ · repatriate/ · ladder/ · briefing/ · audit-*/ — each with its own verifier and CI job. Coverage is impressive; coherence is not.
  • Verifier quality varies. The strongest existing checks (row counts, citation presence, local links, arithmetic recomputation) prove structure. None of them can prove that a cited page still says what the row claims — the gap this pass targets.
  • Repeated corrections without retraction. Later sessions corrected earlier claims by adding new pages rather than amending the old ones, so a search engine or a direct link can still land on superseded wording.
  • Deterministic builds are enforced. verify-ladder regenerates output and fails on any diff; this session kept that contract and regenerated the ladder inventory after adding files.

What this dashboard adds

  1. Quote-level verification: 24 documents fetched on 2026-11-10, with the exact sentence shown next to the link.
  2. A single cost ladder (17 × 40 = 680 cells) recomputed independently, with the fee model written out line by line.
  3. Explicit separation of published fee, binding constraint, fine exposure and unverified spread — so a "$0" cannot be read as "free".
  4. Segment-correct HSBC fees (Private Banking free; Premier/Advance 0.1% + cable).
  5. Blank-by-default policy for every value no source publishes, with the reason on the row.

Scope and limits. Every source on this site was fetched and quoted on 2026-11-10; the quote shown is the text relied on. Values a provider or regulator does not publish are left blank and flagged — blank means unverified, not zero. No private account facts were used, no bank was contacted, no funds moved and no return filed. The 24 sources here are a re-verification of load-bearing claims, not a legal opinion or a personalised tax computation.
