Tax and reporting rules

PRC foreign-exchange and tax-recordal rules, and U.S. translation, exclusion and reporting rules — each with the text it was read from.

The distinction that matters Nothing below taxes the conversion. PRC rules govern whether the money may leave and what must be filed first. U.S. rules govern how it is translated, what is excluded and what must be reported. The one provision that touches a currency gain directly — 26 U.S.C. §988(e)(2) — exempts it unless the gain exceeds $200 (S06 ↗).

Notes and limits

  • U01 Functional currency and translation requirement: Payments of U.S. tax must be remitted to the IRS in U.S. dollars.
  • U02 Official U.S. exchange rate: The IRS yearly average for the Chinese yuan is 7.129 for 2025 and 7.189 for 2024.
  • U03 Tax on a personal foreign-currency gain (26 U.S.C. 988(e)): Section 988(e)(1) also disapplies the rest of section 988 to personal transactions. A transaction is not personal to the extent its expenses meet section 162 or 212.
  • U04 FBAR civil penalty ceiling - non-willful: Adjusted by 90 FR 5630, Jan. 17, 2025.
  • U05 FBAR civil penalty ceiling - willful: The statute also permits 50% of the account balance where that is greater.
  • U06 Foreign earned income exclusion - 2026: Reaches EARNED income only. It does not exclude a gain on a property sale or an inheritance.
  • U07 Foreign earned income exclusion - 2027: Rev. Proc. 2026-35 section 3, item .41.
  • U08 U.S.-China treaty cap on PRC tax on dividends: Article 9(2) of the 1984 Convention. The treaty caps PRC withholding; it does not by itself remove U.S. tax on a U.S. citizen.
  • U09 U.S.-China treaty cap on PRC tax on interest: Article 10(2).
  • U10 FBAR filing threshold and Form 8938 thresholds: NOT re-verified from fincen.gov or irs.gov in this session. The penalty ceilings at U04 and U05 were verified; the filing thresholds were not. Left BLANK.
  • U11 FinCEN Form 105 currency reporting threshold: NOT verified in this session - see flag F06. Left BLANK.
  • U12 Form 3520 foreign gift / bequest reporting threshold and section 6039F penalty: NOT verified in this session - see flag F12. Left BLANK.
  • P01 Annual individual foreign-exchange purchase and sale quota: Above the quota the purchase is not prohibited; S02 says examination of the authenticity of transactions exceeding the quota is strengthened.
  • P02 Lump-sum transfer of an emigrant's PRC property: S01 also cancelled the requirement to file with SAFE where the total exceeded RMB 500,000.
  • P04 Outbound-payment tax recordal threshold: Article 3(13) exempts a domestic individual's private-use FX for study, travel or visiting relatives abroad.
  • P05 Recordal frequency and channel: S11 confirms the USD 50,000 threshold itself was not changed.
  • P06 Tax authority review window on the recordal: The review expressly checks whether the outbound item has paid tax as required.
  • P07 PRC individual income tax rate applicable to the balance: NOT VERIFIED in this session - see flag F01. Left BLANK, not inferred.
  • P08 PRC penalty for splitting a transfer across other people's quotas: The monitoring system is verified; the penalty amount is NOT - see flag F11. Left BLANK.
