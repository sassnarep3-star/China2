Methods: every route, with pros, cons and steps

Thirteen routes from mainland CNY to USD, each with its legal standing, trade-offs and a step-by-step playbook.

What makes a route lawful A route is lawful when the FX purchase is either inside the individual's annual USD 50,000 quota (S02 ↗), supported by authenticity documents above that quota, or covered by a SAFE approval under Huifa [2014] No. 2 Section VI (S01 ↗) — and when a single payment above USD 50,000 equivalent has its tax recordal in place (S10 ↗, S11 ↗).
M01 · HSBC China - Private Banking (personal client) - outward SWIFT TT, charges SHA  costed

Route: Buy USD over the counter or in online banking against a CNY balance, then wire it out by SWIFT with charges shared (SHA).

Standing: Documented route. The FX purchase itself is subject to SAFE individual quota and authenticity rules (S02).   Source: S09 ↗

Pros

  • Lowest verified bank charge in this dataset: RMB 0 for both the wire and the cable.
  • Fully documented, leaves a clean audit trail usable for FBAR / Form 8938 and for a future IRS enquiry.
  • No SAFE capital-account approval is needed for a current-account purchase inside the annual quota.

Cons

  • Requires a HSBC China Private Banking relationship, which the tariff ties to a minimum aggregate balance the bank sets and may change (S09 note 11).
  • The tariff expressly excludes receiving-bank and correspondent-bank charges from 'free' (S09 备注), so the true landed cost is higher and is NOT published.
  • The FX spread is not published anywhere in the tariff, so the single largest cost component is unknown.

Step by step

  1. Confirm the source of the CNY balance and hold the contracts, payslips, invoices or sale documents that prove it.
  2. Confirm PRC tax has been settled and obtain the tax record / 纳税记录 for the period that generated the balance.
  3. If the single payment will exceed USD 50,000 equivalent, complete the outbound-payment tax recordal under Announcement [2013] No. 40 first (S10, S11).
  4. Purchase USD against the CNY balance. Inside the annual USD 50,000 quota the bank's document review is simplified; above it, authenticity documents are examined (S02).
  5. Instruct the outward SWIFT TT with charges SHA (S09 2.2.4.1 备注).
  6. Keep the SWIFT MT103 and the CNY-side debit advice; report the account on FinCEN Form 114 if the FBAR threshold was met at any point in the year.

Fee basis: Tariff 2.2.4.1 私人银行业务 / 个人客户 = 免费; 2.2.4.2 电报费 个人客户 = 免费

M02 · HSBC China - Premier Elite / Premier Elite International (卓越理财尊尚 / 尊尚国际通) - outward SWIFT TT, SHA  costed

Route: Same wire as M01 but on a Premier Elite relationship, whose remarks column waives the wire and cable charges that standard Premier pays.

Standing: Documented route. Subject to the same SAFE individual quota and authenticity rules (S02).   Source: S09 ↗

Pros

  • Verified RMB 0 wire and cable charge, same as Private Banking, at a lower relationship threshold.
  • Premier Elite International is designed for customers whose primary relationship is with an overseas HSBC, which suits someone already banking in the U.S.

Cons

  • Eligibility is conditional and the tariff lets the bank withdraw it (S09 note 11).
  • Free covers only HSBC's own tariff lines; correspondent and receiving-bank charges still apply and are unpublished.

Step by step

  1. Confirm Premier Elite or Premier Elite International status is active for the month of the transfer.
  2. Complete the same source-of-funds and tax-record preparation as M01.
  3. Complete the Announcement [2013] No. 40 tax recordal if the single payment exceeds USD 50,000 equivalent (S10, S11).
  4. Purchase USD and instruct the outward TT with charges SHA.
  5. Retain MT103 and file U.S. reporting as applicable.

Fee basis: Tariff 2.2.4.1 and 2.2.4.2 备注: 卓越理财尊尚、卓越理财尊尚国际通客户享受该项服务免费

M03 · HSBC China - Premier (卓越理财) - outward SWIFT TT, SHA  costed

Route: Standard Premier outward wire. This is the fee schedule most retail HSBC China customers actually pay.

Standing: Documented route. Subject to the same SAFE individual quota and authenticity rules (S02).   Source: S09 ↗

Pros

  • Charge is capped: the percentage component cannot exceed RMB 500 no matter how large the wire, so the fee falls as a percentage of the amount as the amount grows.
  • Widely available; no private-banking balance required beyond the Premier minimum.

Cons

  • The RMB 100 minimum makes small wires expensive in percentage terms: 0.3086% at the USD 10,000 tier, falling to 0.0217% at USD 400,000.
  • Cable charge is a flat RMB 120 on top, so splitting a large transfer into many small wires multiplies the fixed cost.

Step by step

  1. Prepare source-of-funds and tax records as for M01.
  2. Complete the Announcement [2013] No. 40 recordal if the single payment exceeds USD 50,000 equivalent (S10, S11).
  3. Purchase USD and instruct the outward TT with charges SHA.
  4. Retain MT103 and file U.S. reporting as applicable.

Fee basis: Tariff 2.2.4.1 卓越理财账户: 按汇款金额的0.1%收取, 每笔最低人民币100元/等值, 每笔最高人民币500元/等值, 另加电报费; 2.2.4.2 电报费 每笔人民币120元/等值

M04 · HSBC China - Advance (运筹理财) - outward SWIFT TT, SHA  costed

Route: Same wire on the Advance relationship tier. The published fee schedule is identical to Premier, so the published cost is the same.

Standing: Documented route. Subject to the same SAFE individual quota and authenticity rules (S02).   Source: S09 ↗

Pros

  • Identical published fee to Premier, so no cost penalty for the lower tier on this line item.
  • Lowest balance requirement of the HSBC tiers in this dataset.

Cons

  • The tariff restricts who may open an Advance account from 2026-03-18 to specific target groups (HSBC group employees, mortgage customers, corporate payroll programme customers, downgraded Premier customers) - S09 note 4.
  • Same unpublished spread and correspondent-charge exposure as every other wire.

Step by step

  1. Confirm Advance account eligibility under S09 note 4.
  2. Prepare source-of-funds and tax records as for M01.
  3. Complete the Announcement [2013] No. 40 recordal if the single payment exceeds USD 50,000 equivalent (S10, S11).
  4. Purchase USD and instruct the outward TT with charges SHA.

Fee basis: Tariff 2.2.4.1 运筹理财账户: identical schedule to Premier - 按汇款金额的0.1%收取, 每笔最低人民币100元/等值, 每笔最高人民币500元/等值, 另加电报费; 2.2.4.2 电报费 每笔人民币120元/等值

M05 · HSBC China - Premier (卓越理财) - outward SWIFT TT, charges OUR (recipient receives the full amount)  costed

Route: Same wire, but instructing OUR so the recipient receives the full amount. HSBC pre-collects RMB 200 as a proxy for overseas correspondent charges and bills any shortfall afterwards.

Standing: Documented route. Subject to the same SAFE individual quota and authenticity rules (S02).   Source: S09 ↗

Pros

  • Guarantees the recipient receives the exact amount instructed, which matters when a fixed USD sum is contractually required.
  • Caps the surprise from correspondent deductions at the time of sending.

Cons

  • The RMB 200 is a pre-charge, not a cap: S09 says the shortfall is collected later on notice from the overseas correspondent, so the final cost is open-ended.
  • Most expensive verified fixed component of any HSBC tier in this dataset at every amount.

Step by step

  1. Prepare source-of-funds and tax records as for M01.
  2. Complete the Announcement [2013] No. 40 recordal if the single payment exceeds USD 50,000 equivalent (S10, S11).
  3. Purchase USD and instruct the outward TT with charges OUR.
  4. Expect and reconcile a later debit if correspondent charges exceed the RMB 200 pre-charge.

Fee basis: Tariff 2.2.4.1 + 2.2.4.2 as M03, plus the 2.2.4.1 备注 pre-charge: 如汇款人要求美元汇出汇款金额划付收款人，我行将预先向汇款人收取人民币200元/等值作为预扣海外代理行费用 (RMB 120 cable + RMB 200 OUR pre-charge = RMB 320 fixed)

M06 · HSBC China - Private Banking (personal) - outward SWIFT TT, charges OUR  costed

Route: The free Private Banking wire combined with an OUR instruction. The only verified charge is the RMB 200 correspondent pre-charge.

Standing: Documented route. Subject to the same SAFE individual quota and authenticity rules (S02).   Source: S09 ↗

Pros

  • Lowest verified cost of any OUR option in this dataset at every amount from USD 10,000 to USD 400,000.
  • Recipient receives the full instructed amount.

Cons

  • RMB 200 is a pre-charge; the shortfall is billed later and is uncapped (S09 备注).
  • Requires the Private Banking relationship.

M07 · HSBC China - foreign-currency cash withdrawal at the counter (tariff 2.2.2.1), then physical transport  costed

Route: Convert CNY to USD in the account, withdraw USD banknotes at the counter, and carry them out of the PRC and into the U.S.

Standing: The withdrawal itself is a published free service, but S09 states it must comply with PRC cash-management law, and amounts of USD 5,000 or more need at least three working days' notice. The PRC customs declaration limit and the U.S. FinCEN Form 105 requirement were NOT re-verified from an official source in this session and are left BLANK.   Source: S09 ↗

Pros

  • Verified bank charge of RMB 0 - the lowest published charge of any method in this dataset.
  • Avoids the wire route entirely, so no correspondent-bank deduction.

Cons

  • Physically impractical above modest sums: USD 400,000 in USD 100 notes is roughly 4,000 notes weighing about 40 kg.
  • The PRC per-trip cash export limit is NOT verified in this session and is left BLANK (flag F05).
  • Cash carried into the United States above the reporting threshold must be declared on FinCEN Form 105; the threshold was NOT re-verified in this session and is left BLANK (flag F06).
  • Physical risk of loss, theft and seizure; depositing large cash sums in the U.S. triggers bank source-of-funds questioning.

Step by step

  1. Book the withdrawal at least three working days ahead if USD 5,000 or more (S09 2.2.2.1 备注).
  2. Convert CNY to USD inside the account first; note that converting account funds to cash and back can attract a cash-to-spot differential, which is not published and is left BLANK.
  3. Obtain the PRC customs cash declaration position before travel - NOT verified in this session, left BLANK.
  4. On arrival in the U.S., declare on FinCEN Form 105 if the threshold is met - threshold NOT verified in this session, left BLANK.

Fee basis: Tariff 2.2.2.1 同城本行柜台提取外币现钞 (USD, HKD, JPY, EUR): 免费 for 私人银行业务 / 卓越理财账户 / 运筹理财账户. 2.2.2.2 现钞兑换 is also 免费.

M08 · SAFE Huifa [2014] No. 2 Section VI - lump-sum transfer of personal property on emigration  no published fee

Route: A person who has emigrated applies to the SAFE branch at their former place of registered permanent residence for approval to convert and remit their PRC property in one lump sum (or in batches) up to the approved limit.

Standing: Expressly provided for by S01. This is the only route in this dataset that lawfully moves a whole estate out at once rather than in annual instalments.   Source: S01 ↗

Pros

  • Explicitly permits a LUMP SUM: S01 cancelled the old rule that an emigrant's property had to be transferred in several instalments.
  • S01 also cancelled the requirement to file with SAFE when the total exceeded RMB 500,000, removing one procedural step.
  • Not limited by the USD 50,000 annual individual purchase quota, because it runs on a SAFE approval rather than the quota.

Cons

  • Eligibility turns on emigration status, which is a personal fact not supplied and not inferred here (question Q02).
  • Approval is by the SAFE branch where the emigrant's original registered permanent residence was, so it is tied to the hukou location.
  • SAFE approval timelines and the document list are not published in S01 and are left BLANK (flag F07).

Step by step

  1. Establish and document emigration status.
  2. Apply to the SAFE branch at the original registered permanent residence (S01 VI(I)).
  3. Obtain the approval document specifying the limit.
  4. Instruct the bank to remit in a lump sum or in batches within that limit (S01 VI(II)).

Fee basis: none published in a document fetched this session

M09 · SAFE Huifa [2014] No. 2 Section VI - lump-sum transfer of inherited property  no published fee

Route: An heir applies to the SAFE branch at the decedent's former place of registered permanent residence for approval to convert and remit inherited property.

Standing: Expressly provided for by S01.   Source: S01 ↗

Pros

  • Provides a lawful channel for inherited PRC assets.
  • S01 VI(III) removed the old requirement that property inherited from different decedents be applied for and remitted separately.

Cons

  • Depends on an inheritance that has not been described (question Q03).
  • Tied to the decedent's original registered permanent residence, which may be a different branch from where the assets sit.

Step by step

  1. Establish the inheritance and obtain the supporting legal documents.
  2. Apply to the SAFE branch at the decedent's original registered permanent residence (S01 VI(I)).
  3. Remit within the approved limit (S01 VI(II)).

Fee basis: none published in a document fetched this session

M10 · Outbound-payment tax recordal under STA / SAFE Announcement [2013] No. 40 (enabling step for a single payment above USD 50,000)  no published fee

Route: Before a bank processes a single outbound payment above USD 50,000 equivalent, the payer files a tax recordal with the competent tax authority. This is a gate, not a route: it unlocks the wire.

Standing: Required by S10 for the listed payment categories above USD 50,000 equivalent per single payment.   Source: S10 ↗

Pros

  • S11 confirms only ONE recordal per contract is needed even for multiple payments, and it can be filed online through the provincial electronic tax bureau.
  • S11 confirms the threshold is unchanged at USD 50,000 equivalent, so payments at or below it need no recordal.
  • S10 Article 3(13) exempts a domestic individual's private-use FX for study, travel or visiting relatives abroad.

Cons

  • The tax authority has 15 working days to review the filing and may demand more documents (S10 Article 8), so this is the main schedule risk on any large single transfer.
  • The review expressly checks whether the outbound item has been taxed as required (S10 Article 8(2)), which is where unpaid PRC tax surfaces.
  • Whether any fee is charged for the recordal is NOT published in the documents fetched and is left BLANK (flag F08).

Step by step

  1. Assemble the stamped contract or transaction voucher and a Chinese translation if it is in a foreign language (S10 Article 2).
  2. File the Recordal Form online through the provincial electronic tax bureau, or at the tax service hall (S11).
  3. Record the system-generated form number and verification code (S11).
  4. Present those to the bank to complete the outbound payment (S11).
  5. For repeat payments under the same contract, do not re-file (S11).

Fee basis: none published in a document fetched this session

M13 · Splitting the transfer across the annual USD 50,000 quotas of several people  not recommended

Route: Having relatives or friends each buy USD against their own quota and remit it for the beneficial owner.

Standing: NOT RECOMMENDED. S02 records that SAFE built the bank-to-SAFE management information system specifically 'to prevent acts of avoiding administration, such as repeated purchases of foreign exchange and foreign exchange sales in installments'. The PRC penalty for doing so was NOT verified from an official source in this session and is left BLANK (flag F11).   Source: S02 ↗

Pros

  • None that survive the compliance analysis.

Cons

  • Directly contradicts the stated purpose of the SAFE monitoring system described in S02.
  • Creates a U.S. problem as well: funds arriving from unrelated third parties are hard to source and can look like unreported gifts, engaging Form 3520 reporting whose threshold was NOT verified in this session (flag F12).
  • Exposes the people who lend their quota, not just the beneficial owner.

Step by step

  1. Do not use this route. Use M08 or M09 for a lump sum, or M01-M06 staged across calendar years.

Fee basis: none published in a document fetched this session
