S01 · Circular of the State Administration of Foreign Exchange on Further Improving and Adjusting Foreign Exchange A

Publisher: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), PRC

Locator: Section VI, paragraphs (I)-(III)

Foreign exchange purchases and payments for property transfers due to immigration shall be examined and approved by the local SAFE branch in the place of the immigrant's original registered permanent residence. ... The requirement that the total amount of property transfer exceeding the equivalent of RMB500,000 shall be reported to the SAFE for the record shall be cancelled. ... The requirement that an immigrant's property can be transferred several times is cancelled. After the applicant goes through the approval procedures ... the bank may remit the relevant funds in a lump sum or in several batches within the limit specified in the approval document.

S06 · 26 U.S.C. 988 - Treatment of certain foreign currency transactions

Publisher: U.S. Government Publishing Office (govinfo) - United States Code, 2024 edition

Locator: Sec. 988(e)(1)-(3)

(e) Application to individuals (1) In general. The preceding provisions of this section shall not apply to any section 988 transaction entered into by an individual which is a personal transaction. (2) Exclusion for certain personal transactions. If- (A) nonfunctional currency is disposed of by an individual in any transaction, and (B) such transaction is a personal transaction, no gain shall be recognized for purposes of this subtitle by reason of changes in exchange rates after such currency was acquired by such individual and before such disposition. The preceding sentence shall not apply if the gain which would otherwise be recognized on the transaction exceeds $200.
