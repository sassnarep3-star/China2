Open questions and discovery actions

What is known, what is missing, why the decision turns on it, and how to find out.

Two facts known, 14 unknown The brief supplied exactly two facts: that the requester is a U.S. citizen, and that a CNY balance sits in a mainland bank account. Nothing else. Every unknown below is left unknown — none has been inferred, because each one changes the answer. 8 are blocking: the route cannot be chosen without them.

Questions that must be answered

Q01 · What generated the CNY balance - salary, business profit, dividends, sale of property, sale of securities, an inheritance, or a gift?   MISSING - blocking

Why it decides the answer: Source of funds affects the PRC tax position and documentary requirements. Different transfer paths may be available; confirm the applicable route with the bank and SAFE.

How to discover it: Pull the bank statements for the account for the full period the balance accumulated, plus the underlying contract, payslips, sale agreement or share-transfer documents. Then pull the matching PRC tax filings and payment receipts from the electronic tax bureau for the same years.

Q02 · Has the requester emigrated from the PRC, and do they still hold a PRC hukou (registered permanent residence)?   MISSING - blocking for the lump-sum routes

Why it decides the answer: S01 Section VI discusses property transfers due to immigration and refers to the SAFE branch at the original registered permanent residence. Eligibility and other routes require confirmation.

How to discover it: Check hukou status with the local public security registration office, and check whether a PRC exit-emigration settlement (注销户口 / 定居国外) has been completed.

Q03 · Is any part of the balance inherited?   MISSING - blocking for M09

Why it decides the answer: S01 Section VI provides a separate inheritance route, approved by the SAFE branch at the decedent's original registered permanent residence, and S01 VI(III) removed the rule requiring separate applications per decedent.

How to discover it: Obtain the notarial inheritance certificate and the decedent's former hukou location.

Q04 · What is the exact CNY balance, and is it one account or several?   MISSING - blocking for tier selection

Why it decides the answer: The actual balance is unknown. HSBC fees include a minimum and cap; S10 recordal applies only to covered categories of payment, subject to exemptions.

How to discover it: Read the current balance from the statement, and decide whether the plan is one payment or several.

Q05 · Has PRC individual income tax already been paid on the income that produced the balance?   MISSING - blocking

Why it decides the answer: Tax on underlying income is a possibility, but the reported charge is not identified. S10 Article 8 discusses review of tax compliance where recordal applies. Confirm with the bank and tax authority whether this case is in scope and what is owed.

How to discover it: Log into the provincial electronic tax bureau and download the individual income tax filing and payment records (个人所得税纳税记录) for every year the balance accumulated. Compare against the bank deposits.

Q06 · Is the requester a PRC tax resident for any year in which the balance was earned - 183 days or more, or the six-year rule applied?   MISSING - blocking

Why it decides the answer: PRC residence and income source can affect the tax position. Residence rules and an applicable rate have not been established for this case (F01).

How to discover it: Count days in the PRC per calendar year from passport entry/exit stamps, and obtain the PRC residence determination from the competent tax authority or a PRC-licensed tax agent.

Q07 · Has the requester filed FinCEN Form 114 (FBAR) and Form 8938 for every year the account existed?   MISSING - blocking for the U.S. side

Why it decides the answer: S05 lists inflation-adjusted FBAR penalty amounts; the willful maximum may be greater if 50% of the balance exceeds the specified amount. Filing and penalty exposure depend on the facts and the year.

How to discover it: Pull the filed returns and the BSA E-Filing acknowledgements for each year. If any year is missing, the IRS Streamlined filing procedures are the route to consider before any transfer.

Q08 · Where is the requester tax resident for U.S. purposes, and do they qualify for the foreign earned income exclusion?   MISSING - material to net cost

Why it decides the answer: The FEIE only excludes EARNED income and is worth USD 132,900 for 2026 (S07) rising to USD 137,300 for 2027 (S08). It does nothing for a balance that came from a property sale or an inheritance. Knowing the source (Q01) and residence (Q08) together decides whether any U.S. relief is available at all.

How to discover it: Apply the Form 2555 tax-home test and either the bona fide residence test or the 330-day physical presence test to the actual travel record.

Q09 · Which bank holds the account, and what relationship tier does the requester have there?   MISSING - blocking for the cost table

Why it decides the answer: The verified cost difference between tiers is the whole of the published cost spread in this dataset: RMB 0 for HSBC China Private Banking and Premier Elite versus RMB 220-620 for Premier and Advance (S09). Only HSBC China's tariff was fetched; every other bank is BLANK (flags F09, F10).

How to discover it: Ask the bank for its current published 账户和服务费率 PDF and its current FX rate board, then re-run this model against them.

Q10 · What FX spread does the holding bank actually apply to a CNY-to-USD purchase at this size?   MISSING - largest unknown by value

Why it decides the answer: This is very likely a bigger cost than any published fee. The HSBC tariff fetched in this session publishes wire charges but publishes NO spread, and S09 states the fee table excludes anything not priced by HSBC. At 0.50% the spread on USD 400,000 would be USD 2,000, which is more than thirty times the largest verified bank fee in this dataset - but 0.50% is an assumption and is therefore NOT published here (flag F02).

How to discover it: Request an indicative dealing rate in writing for the actual amount, and compare it against the same-day PBOC central parity rate to compute the real margin.

Q11 · What do the correspondent bank and the U.S. receiving bank deduct?   MISSING - material

Why it decides the answer: S09 states expressly that HSBC's 'free' covers only HSBC's own tariff lines and does NOT extend to receiving-bank or correspondent-bank charges in the payment path. Those amounts are set by institutions this session did not fetch, so they are BLANK (flag F03).

How to discover it: Ask the U.S. receiving bank for its inbound international wire fee, and ask HSBC for the typical correspondent deduction on the USD corridor. Compare SHA versus OUR on a single small test wire before moving the bulk.

Q12 · Is there any urgency, and is the requester willing to stage the transfer across calendar years?   MISSING - decides strategy

Why it decides the answer: S02 sets the individual annual purchase quota at USD 50,000, so a balance far above that either needs multiple calendar years, an above-quota authenticity review, or the S01 lump-sum approval. Staging across years costs nothing in verified bank fees above the minimum-charge floor but multiplies the fixed RMB 100 minimum and RMB 120 cable per wire.

How to discover it: Decide the earliest acceptable completion date, then compare the staged cost in the cost table against the one-off cost.

Q13 · Has the requester ever been assessed a PRC or U.S. penalty relating to this balance?   MISSING - risk sizing

Why it decides the answer: Prior non-compliance changes the sequencing: it may be necessary to regularise before transferring, because the transfer itself creates the bank and tax-authority interaction described in S10 Article 8.

How to discover it: Check for any PRC tax authority notices and any IRS correspondence, and confirm whether any FBAR was filed late or not at all.

Q14 · Are there PRC exchange controls or account conditions on this specific account (frozen, pledged, restricted-purpose)?   MISSING - operational

Why it decides the answer: An account restriction would block every route regardless of tax position.

How to discover it: Ask the branch in writing whether the account is subject to any hold, pledge or purpose restriction.

Answers established so far

A01. No tax on the act of converting CNY into USD was located in any document fetched this session.

Support: S03 and S04 govern how the IRS wants foreign currency translated and state the IRS has no official exchange rate. Neither imposes a charge. S06 makes a personal foreign-currency gain non-recognised unless the gain exceeds USD 200.

Confidence: Limited to the documents fetched. This is not a conclusion that no PRC charge exists; the PRC statutory rate table was not verified (flag F01).

A02. The largest verified cost component in this dataset is the bank's wire and cable charge, and it is small and capped.

Support: S09: 0.1% with a RMB 100 minimum and RMB 500 maximum, plus a RMB 120 cable. For the SHA charge-sharing options (M03, M04) the verified HSBC charge ranges from RMB 220 to RMB 620 across USD 10,000 to USD 400,000, i.e. about USD 30.86 to USD 86.97 at the S03 rate of 7.129; adding the RMB 200 OUR pre-charge (M05) raises that to RMB 420-820, about USD 58.91 to USD 115.02. It is RMB 0 for Private Banking (M01) and Premier Elite (M02), and RMB 0 for a counter cash withdrawal (M07). The 0.1% percentage component first hits its RMB 500 cap at the USD 80,000 tier.

Confidence: High for HSBC China at the tariff effective 2026-10-31. No other bank verified.

A03. The USD 50,000 figures in SAFE and tax-recordal rules are distinct; application depends on the transaction.

Support: S10 requires a tax recordal for listed categories of single outbound payments above USD 50,000 equivalent, subject to exemptions, including specified personal-use FX under Article 3(13). S11 covers repeat payments under a contract and the filing channel. S02 separately discusses an annual FX purchase quota.

Confidence: The sources state these rules; applicability to this case is not established.

A04. A lump-sum exit does exist and does not require year-by-year staging.

Support: S01 Section VI permits an emigrant's property to be remitted in a lump sum or in batches within the SAFE-approved limit, and cancels the old RMB 500,000 record-filing requirement.

Confidence: High for the rule. Eligibility depends on Q02, which is unanswered.

A05. U.S. reporting and tax obligations must be assessed independently of the transfer.

Support: S05 lists adjusted FBAR penalty amounts, including USD 16,536 non-willful and USD 165,353 willful; the willful maximum can be greater if 50% of the balance exceeds the listed amount. S07 and S08 state FEIE amounts, subject to eligibility.

Confidence: The amounts are source-linked; applicability and current penalty adjustments require review.
