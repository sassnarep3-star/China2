U01Is converting CNY to USD itself a taxable event?No separate conversion tax exists. U.S. tax attaches to income, not to the act of changing currency. All income tax determinations must be made in the functional currency, and the U.S. dollar is the functional currency for all taxpayers except some qualified business units. No conversion tax. Only exchange-rate gain or loss on the currency position is in scope. — fetched S04 · U.S. Internal Revenue Service fetched—
