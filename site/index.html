A verified evidence base for moving money out of China as a U.S. citizen. Nothing here is estimated: unconfirmed values are left blank and flagged.

The central finding. Converting CNY into USD is not a taxable event in either country. Neither SAFE nor the IRS taxes the act of changing currency. The binding constraint is China's USD 50,000 per person per year foreign-exchange facilitation quota and the documentary authenticity review above it — not tax. On the U.S. side the real financial risk is reporting (FBAR / Form 8938), not conversion.
