Unlawful routes

Prohibited mechanisms, with the official basis for the prohibition.

These are the mechanisms most often recommended online for moving money out of China. They are documented here so they can be rejected, not used. Each is tied to the official instrument that prohibits it.

IDRouteMechanismStatusWhy it failsSourceFlag
R13 Virtual currency / cryptocurrency transfer out of China Purchasing cryptocurrency with CNY and selling it abroad for USD. UNLAWFUL
  • All virtual-currency-related business activities are illegal in China and shall be strictly prohibited and cracked down upon in accordance with the law
  • It is an illegal financial activity for overseas virtual-currency exchanges to provide services to mainland residents over the internet
  • Financial institutions and non-bank payment institutions are banned from providing any services for such activity
  • Destroys the documentary trail that every lawful remittance route depends on
  • Creates U.S. reporting exposure on top of Chinese criminal exposure

R14 Splitting / pooling quotas (using multiple relatives' quotas for one beneficiary) Several individuals each use their own USD 50,000 quota and remit to the same overseas beneficiary. UNLAWFUL
  • SAFE's own English text states the annual-quota system exists in part to prevent avoiding administration, including repeated purchases and sales in installments
  • SAFE's card circular separately prohibits borrowing another person's card or lending one's own to evade control
  • SAFE operates a management information system connecting banks to SAFE specifically to detect and verify individual FX purchases and sales
  • The specific sanctions applied to detected splitting were not confirmed against an official SAFE English source in this session and are left unverified

R15 Underground banking / matching domestic and offshore transfers with no cross-border movement CNY is paid to a domestic account while an associate deposits foreign currency offshore. UNLAWFUL
  • No official authorisation exists for this channel
  • The PBOC rules require that assets transferred abroad be lawful and indisputable as to ownership, with proof of lawful source
  • Applicants who swindle foreign exchange with false documents are penalized under the Regulations of the PRC on Foreign Exchange Administration
  • The specific administrative and criminal penalties under those Regulations were not confirmed against an official English text in this session and are left unverified

R16 Fabricated or inflated trade invoicing to access the current account Remittances for trade in goods are processed against trading documents; fabricating or inflating invoices abuses that channel. UNLAWFUL
  • SAFE requires that business under an individual FX settlement account be conducted with VALID trading documents regardless of amount
  • SAFE verifies the authenticity of the materials provided by individuals through its bank-linked system
  • Banks are directed to examine authenticity above the quota
 S05 · State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), People's Republic of China fetched —
R17 Applying for emigrant or inheritor asset transfer on false documents Filing the personal-asset outbound transfer application with false documents, or filing twice on the same assets. UNLAWFUL
  • The PBOC rules state that an applicant found swindling foreign exchange with false documents, or filing applications on the same assets twice, is penalized under the Regulations of the PRC on Foreign Exchange Administration
  • SAFE will not accept applications for assets prohibited from transfer by judicial or supervisory authorities
