S01 Report of Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts (FBAR) U.S. Internal Revenue Service fetched in-session
  • FBAR threshold: aggregate value of foreign financial accounts exceeded $10,000 at any time during the calendar year
  • Who must file: a U.S. person, including a citizen, resident, corporation, partnership, LLC, trust and estate
  • Form used: FinCEN Form 114
  • Due April 15 following the calendar year reported, with an automatic extension to October 15
  • Filed electronically through FinCEN's BSA E-Filing System, not with the federal tax return
  • Records must be kept five years from the FBAR due date
  • Civil FBAR penalty maximums in Title 31 U.S.C. are adjusted annually for inflation (31 CFR 1010.821)
  • Delinquent FBARs should be filed as soon as possible if the IRS has not made contact
S02 Summary of FATCA reporting for U.S. taxpayers U.S. Internal Revenue Service fetched in-session
  • Form 8938 must be attached to the annual income tax return
  • No Form 8938 filing is required if no U.S. income tax return filing is required
  • Living in the U.S., unmarried: more than $50,000 on the last day of the tax year or more than $75,000 at any time
  • Living in the U.S., married filing jointly: more than $100,000 on the last day or more than $150,000 at any time
  • Living abroad, not married filing jointly: more than $200,000 on the last day or more than $300,000 at any time
  • Living abroad, married filing jointly: more than $400,000 on the last day or more than $600,000 at any time
  • Living abroad test: U.S. citizen whose tax home is in a foreign country and present in a foreign country at least 330 days out of a consecutive 12-month period
  • Form 8938 does not relieve filers of FBAR filing requirements

S04 Foreign currency and currency exchange rates U.S. Internal Revenue Service fetched in-session
  • Amounts reported on a U.S. tax return must be expressed in U.S. dollars
  • The U.S. dollar is the functional currency for all taxpayers except some qualified business units (QBUs)
  • All income tax determinations must be made in the functional currency; foreign-currency items must be translated at the exchange rate prevailing when received, paid or accrued

S05 SAFE Spokesman Addresses Press Conference on Relevant Issues Concerning the Detailed Rules on Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange (5 January 2007) State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), People's Republic of China fetched in-session
  • The annual individual foreign-exchange facilitation quota is USD 50,000 (raised from USD 20,000)
  • The quota applies to both individual foreign-exchange purchases and sales, for both domestic and foreign individuals
  • Within the annual quota, formalities are simplified; above the quota, examination of the authenticity of transactions is strengthened
  • SAFE operates a management information system connecting banks to SAFE for individual purchases and sales of foreign exchange; banks verify the authenticity of the materials provided
  • Purchases and sales of foreign exchange under individual foreign-exchange settlement accounts (for individual trade) are NOT subject to the annual quota limits and are conducted with valid trading documents regardless of amount
  • Capital transfers for individual foreign-exchange savings accounts may only be conducted between accounts of the same nature held by the individual or their direct relatives
S06 Circular of the State Administration of Foreign Exchange on Regulating Large-sum Overseas Cash Withdrawals with Bank Cards (Huifa No. 29 [2017]) State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) fetched in-session
  • Overseas cash withdrawals using bank cards in the individual's own name (including supplementary cards) shall not exceed the equivalent of RMB 100,000 in every civil year
  • Exceeding the annual quota bars the individual from overseas cash withdrawal with domestic bank cards in the current and the following year
  • Individuals are prohibited from borrowing others' bank cards or lending their own to evade the management of overseas cash withdrawals
  • Daily overseas cash-withdrawal ceiling per card adjusted from USD 1,000 equivalent to RMB 10,000 equivalent
  • Violators are punished in accordance with the Regulations of the PRC on Foreign Exchange Administration
  • Effective 1 January 2018

S09 The People's Bank of China released the Provisional Administrative Rules on Sales and Payment of Foreign Exchange for Personal Asset Outbound Transfer (PBOC Announcement No. 16 [2004]) People's Bank of China (PBOC) fetched in-session
  • Outbound personal-asset transfer covers two categories only: emigrant asset transfer and inheritor asset transfer
  • Emigrant asset transfer = encashment, exchange and overseas transfer of assets obtained by an emigrant from Mainland China BEFORE obtaining immigration permission from a foreign country or from Hong Kong/Macao SARs
  • Inheritor asset transfer = encashment, exchange and overseas transfer of assets by foreign citizens and residents of Hong Kong or Macao SARs
  • The Rules do not apply to other forms of personal asset transfer
  • Assets applied for transfer must be lawful and indisputable as to ownership; applications are refused where the lawful source is unproven or where civil or criminal litigation is unsettled
  • Applications for value under RMB 500,000 are approved by SAFE branches; RMB 500,000 and above originally required SAFE head-office approval (superseded by the 2014 circular, see S08)
  • Emigrant transfer above RMB 200,000 equivalent had to be remitted in installments (superseded by the 2014 circular, see S08)
  • Designated banks remit directly to the applicant's account in the country of emigration/settlement; drawing foreign-exchange cash inside Mainland China is not allowed
  • Applicants who swindle foreign exchange with false documents are penalized under the Regulations of the PRC on Foreign Exchange Administration
S10 Individual Income Tax Law of the People's Republic of China (7th amendment, 31 August 2018) — official English text State Taxation Administration, People's Republic of China fetched in-session
  • Resident individual: domiciled in China, or not domiciled but resident in China for 183 days or more in a tax year; taxed on income from within China or overseas
  • Non-resident individual: not domiciled and not resident, or resident for fewer than 183 days in a tax year; taxed on China-source income only
  • Comprehensive income (wages/salaries, personal services, author's remuneration, royalties) is taxed at progressive rates of 3% to 45%
  • Income from interest, dividends or bonuses, leasing of assets, transfer of assets, and incidental income is taxed at a flat 20%
  • Resident comprehensive-income taxable amount is computed after deduction of RMB 60,000 plus special and itemized deductions
  • A tax year runs 1 January to 31 December
