Steps
  1. Collect the passport used for the account, the contracts or payslips that show the amounts, and the tax vouchers for the same income.
  2. Ask the branch which papers it accepts as source-of-funds documents for your case, and ask for any refusal in writing.
  3. For a single payment above USD 50,000, ask whether the bank also wants the tax record-filing form.
  4. Buy dollars at the bank’s selling rate on the day; the rate on the board is a guide and the transaction rate governs.
  5. Wire the dollars to an account in your own name and keep the purchase memo and the wire receipt.
  6. In the U.S., report the foreign account for the year and keep the Chinese tax vouchers with your tax records.
