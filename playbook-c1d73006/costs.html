How the figures are made

  1. The margin is the bank’s selling rate less the midpoint of its own buying and selling rates, as a share of the midpoint. For Bank of China that is (6.6912 − 6.67715) ÷ 6.67715 = 0.2104%.
  2. The commission is 0.1% of the RMB paid, held between the bank’s floor and ceiling. The cable charge is added.
  3. RMB fees are turned into dollars at the same bank’s selling rate, because that is the rate at which the fee uses up dollars.
  4. Capped routes are counted, not priced, where the fee is not published: years at the yearly cap, transfers at the per-transfer cap, trips at the cash limit.
  5. Unlawful routes show the fine the regulation allows, which is not a fee and does not include confiscation or criminal liability.
