Penalties

The “conversion penalty” is seven separate things. Only three of them are avoidable.

The original complaint is that converting would cost “a considerable amount of taxes”. Breaking the cost into layers shows why that figure felt large and what each part actually is: a shoppable spread, a capped fee, a free filing, a tax on the income, a usually-trivial U.S. currency gain, two U.S. filing duties with very large statutory maxima, and the statutory penalties for the shortcuts. Layers 1 and 2 are the real cost of converting. Layer 3 is free. Layer 4 is not caused by the conversion at all.

The arithmetic that reframes the question. At USD 400,000 the lawful route costs $901.20 (BOC). The 30% floor in Regulation 532 Art. 39 for evasion is $120,000, and the serious-case ceiling in Art. 40 or 45 is $400,000. If the bank's “considerable tax” is layer 4, then no conversion route avoids it and the choice between routes changes nothing about it; if it is layer 1, it is 0.2% and shoppable; if it is layer 3, it is a free form. The three possibilities differ by four orders of magnitude, and only the bank's written answer (Q04) distinguishes them.

What is not a penalty but gets called one

Phrase used in the corpusWhat the reviewed text actually providesSource
“Conversion tax”No reviewed instrument taxes the exchange. There is a spread, a tariff, a filing step, and tax on the income.S31 S24
“0.5% issuer fee”No instrument reviewed imposes an issuer fee. The only 0.5%-scale figure found is HSBC's published spread of 0.49995%, which is a rate, not a fee.S27 S28
“CNY 30,000 bank fine under 汇发〔2007〕1号 Art. 39”Repealed by 汇发〔2023〕8号; the SAFE republication prints the deletion and the current-rules catalogue repeats it. Use Regulation 532 Arts. 39–48 instead.S03 S04 S01
“USD 50,000 annual limit”An annual facilitation quota, not a ceiling; SAFE says lawful current-account settlement and purchase have no amount limit.S05
“Staged over 8 years via the quota”Only true if you choose the quota route with no other documentation. Route 1 has no such staging, and route 5 is staged over two years by rule, not eight.S05 S11

What this is. A document review. No bank was contacted, no account accessed, no funds moved, no return filed. No personal tax figure is asserted: basis, residency, filing status and the source of the balance are unknown. Every computed figure is arithmetic on a rate or fee the publisher printed, and every published rate board says it is not the executable price — so all totals are lower bounds.
