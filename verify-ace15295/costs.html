Quoted as CNY per 100 units of foreign currency, as published. Half-spread is (sell − buy) ÷ 2 ÷ mid — the cost of crossing the published spread once. HSBC's board is the outlier at roughly 2.4× the published spread of the two Chinese banks on the same days.

Why “free” is not cheapest — except at the very bottom. HSBC publishes a genuinely free telegraphic transfer for its Private Banking individuals and free Global Transfer for Premier S28. Its published spread is 0.5000% against BOC’s 0.2104%, so BOC wins from about $11,820 upward — at USD 400,000 that is $901.20 against $1,989.83. Below that crossover, BOC’s CNY 50 minimum plus CNY 150 cable (about $30) outweighs the spread difference and HSBC Private Banking is marginally cheaper: at USD 10,000, $49.75 against $53.42. Both are shown in the full ladder, and the verifier recomputes the crossover rather than trusting this paragraph.

How each figure is computed

StepFormulaWhere it comes from
1CNY needed = USD amount × bank's published selling rate ÷ 100S24 S26 S27
2Spread cost = USD amount × (selling rate − mid) ÷ selling rateSame board, same row
3Wire fee = rate from the bank's own tariff, floored and capped in CNY, plus cable, then converted at the same selling rateS25 S28
4All-in = step 2 + step 3Arithmetic
—Not included in any cell: the correspondent/intermediary bank's deduction, the receiving U.S. bank's inward fee, and any PRC income tax on the income itselfS28 note 7; S31
The three blanks that matter. (1) China Merchants Bank's wire fee — its rate board was captured, its tariff was not, so no CMB total is shown. (2) ICBC — its public board returned “暂无数据” (no data) to this session and its feed is script-populated, so it is absent entirely rather than copied from a stale snapshot S30. (3) Sending-side totals exclude the receiving side. No sender publishes what the U.S. bank will charge on arrival, and HSBC's tariff says so in terms S28. Add the U.S. bank's incoming-wire fee before comparing with a quote.

What this is. A document review. No bank was contacted, no account accessed, no funds moved, no return filed. No personal tax figure is asserted: basis, residency, filing status and the source of the balance are unknown. Every computed figure is arithmetic on a rate or fee the publisher printed, and every published rate board says it is not the executable price — so all totals are lower bounds.
