S01 State Council (PRC) / gov.cn
中华人民共和国外汇管理条例 (国务院令第532号), 2008
statute · read: full text of the gazette page · retrieved 2026-11-11
  • Art. 12: current-account FX receipts/payments must have a real, lawful transaction basis; banks must reasonably review authenticity of documents and consistency with the FX receipts/payments
  • Art. 39: evading FX (逃汇) — fine up to 30% of the amount; if serious, 30%-up to the equivalent amount
  • Art. 40: illegal arbitrage (非法套汇) incl. obtaining FX from a bank with false/invalid documents — fine up to 30%; serious 30%-100%
  • Art. 42: unlawful carrying of FX across the border — warning, fine up to 20% of the amount
  • Art. 45: private/unlawful buying and selling of FX, or illegally introducing FX trading — warning, confiscation of gains, fine up to 30%; serious 30%-100%; criminal liability
  • Art. 48: failure to submit valid documents, or submitting inauthentic documents — for an individual, fine up to CNY 50,000
  • Art. 52(2): 境内个人 (domestic individual) = 中国公民 and foreigners who have continuously resided in the PRC for a full year, excluding foreign diplomatic personnel and representatives of international organisations

Note: Article 52(2) is why the 'domestic vs non-domestic individual' question matters: a PRC-citizenship test does not answer it alone.
S02 People's Bank of China (English site)
Administrative Rules on Individual's Foreign Exchange — PBOC Decree [2006] No.3
regulation · read: full English text (Articles 1-42) · retrieved 2026-11-11
  • Art. 7: banks and individuals must not evade limit administration by splitting a large amount into small batches
  • Art. 13: a non-domestic individual may convert legitimate RMB income obtained in China under the current account and remit it abroad, on valid ID and relevant documents
  • Art. 37(1): a domestic individual (境内个人) means a Chinese citizen holding a PRC identity certificate, serviceman identity certificate or police identity certificate
  • Art. 37(2): a non-domestic individual (境外个人) means a foreign citizen holding a non-Chinese passport, and Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan compatriots holding the relevant travel permit, and stateless persons
  • Art. 28: domestic fund transfers between a domestic individual's and a non-domestic individual's FX accounts are managed as cross-border transactions

Note: Art. 37(1) is a PRC-citizenship test. A U.S. passport holder is therefore 境外个人 under this rule, which routes them to the documented-commercial-income rule rather than the annual facilitation quota.
S03 SAFE Tianjin branch (republication of 汇发[2007]1号, as amended)
（已修改）国家外汇管理局关于印发《个人外汇管理办法实施细则》的通知
regulation · read: full text incl. the publisher's amendment note · retrieved 2026-11-11
  • Art. 2: annual aggregate management applies to individuals' FX settlement and to domestic individuals' FX purchases; the annual totals are the equivalent of USD 50,000 per person per year
  • Art. 10: for a domestic individual, non-operating current-account settlement above the annual total requires ID plus specific transaction evidence (donations, family support, inheritance, insurance, royalties, services, employee remuneration, overseas investment returns, other)
  • Art. 12: domestic individual purchasing FX above the annual total requires ID and materials evidencing the transaction amount
  • Art. 13(1): FX purchase against legitimate current-account RMB income obtained in China is made on valid ID and transaction-amount materials (including tax documents) — this is the non-domestic individual rule
  • Art. 13(2): re-conversion of unused RMB from an original exchange is made on ID and the original exchange memo; the memo is valid for 24 months; same-day totals of up to USD 500 (and up to USD 1,000 within the port area before departure) need only ID
  • Art. 14: remitting FX abroad from an FX savings account up to the equivalent of USD 50,000/day on ID; above that, documentary evidence of the transaction
  • Art. 30: cash withdrawal of FX up to the equivalent of USD 10,000/day at the bank; above that, prior filing with the local SAFE office
  • Publisher's note: Art. 39 of these Rules (the CNY 30,000 / CNY 1,000 fine provision) has been REPEALED by 汇发[2023]8号

Note: The repeal note is printed on the publisher's own page. Any table citing a CNY 30,000 penalty under this instrument is citing a deleted article.

S05 SAFE Xiamen branch
国家外汇管理局厦门市分局个人外汇业务服务小贴士 (PDF)
official-guidance · read: full PDF text · retrieved 2026-11-11
  • §1 Q1: 境内个人 means a Chinese citizen holding a PRC resident identity card, serviceman identity card or armed-police identity card
  • §1 Q8: 'At present China imposes NO amount limit on FX settlement and purchase under genuine, lawful current-account items and already-opened capital-account items. The USD 50,000 equivalent is an ANNUAL FACILITATION QUOTA, not an individual's annual ceiling on settlement or purchase.'
  • §1 Q9: a domestic individual above/outside the quota settles or purchases on ID plus source-of-funds or purpose materials directly at the bank
  • §1 Q13: generally there is no amount or frequency limit on an individual's outward remittance, BUT an individual must not use splitting to evade regulation
  • §2 Q1: 境外个人 (non-domestic individuals) do NOT complete the 个人购汇申请书 (FX purchase application form)
  • §3 Q4: 近亲属 (close relatives) = spouse, parents, children, siblings, grandparents, grandchildren
  • §4: cash deposit/withdrawal thresholds of USD 10,000/day; over that, customs declaration form or original bank cash-withdrawal document
  • §4 Q4/Q5: carrying FX cash out up to USD 5,000 needs no 携带外汇出境许可证; USD 5,000-10,000 needs the certificate from the bank

Note: Q8 is the clearest official statement that the widely repeated 'USD 50,000 per year limit' is a description of the fast-track quota, not a legal cap.

S09 State Taxation Administration + SAFE
关于服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案有关问题的公告 (公告2013年第40号)
regulation (tax + FX joint) · read: full text as republished by a provincial tax bureau · retrieved 2026-11-11
  • Art. 1: where a domestic entity or individual pays a single outward payment of the equivalent of more than USD 50,000 for listed service-trade / income / current-transfer items, tax recordal (税务备案) with the local tax authority is required unless Art. 3 applies
  • Art. 1(2): includes a non-domestic individual's WORK REMUNERATION in China, and dividends, profit, direct-debt interest, guarantee fees and non-capital donations/compensation received from China
  • Art. 3(13): an individual's PRIVATE-purpose FX use abroad (overseas study, travel, visiting relatives) does NOT require recordal
  • Art. 4: a non-domestic individual's outward payment under service trade, income and current transfer is handled under the individual FX rules
  • Art. 6: if the documents are complete and the form is filled in, the tax authority need not conduct a tax examination on the spot; it assigns a serial number and stamps the form
  • Art. 12: effective 1 September 2013

Note: Art. 6 is decisive: recordal is a filing step, not an assessment. It is the step most often mistaken by payers for 'a tax'.

S11 People's Bank of China / State Council Gazette
个人财产对外转移售付汇管理暂行办法 (中国人民银行公告〔2004〕第16号)
regulation · read: full text in the State Council Gazette · retrieved 2026-11-11
  • Art. 2: covers 移民财产转移 (emigrant property transfer) — a natural person who has moved from the mainland to a foreign country or to Hong Kong/Macao converting and remitting LAWFUL PROPERTY OWNED BEFORE ACQUIRING EMIGRANT STATUS — and 继承财产转移 (inheritance transfer) by a foreign citizen or HK/Macao resident
  • Art. 5: the application goes to the SAFE branch where the emigrant's original household registration was, or where the deceased was registered
  • Art. 6: the emigrant must apply ONCE for the whole amount and remit in stages — first remittance no more than half; after one year from the first remittance, no more than half of the remainder; after two years, the rest; a total of the equivalent of CNY 200,000 or less may be remitted in one go after approval
  • Art. 8(4): for emigration abroad, requires the public-security cancellation-of-household-registration certificate AND a certificate of overseas residence issued or authenticated by a Chinese embassy/consulate
  • Art. 9(5): for inheritance, requires the tax certificate or tax-payment receipt issued by the competent tax authority where the inherited property is located
  • Art. 10: total up to the equivalent of CNY 500,000 is approved by the local SAFE branch; ABOVE that it is first reviewed locally and then approved by SAFE headquarters
  • Art. 11: after selling FX, the bank must remit directly to the applicant's own account in the country of residence and must not hand out FX cash inside China

Note: This is a capital-account route. It requires the applicant to have given up PRC household registration (or to be a foreign heir), and it is staged over two years. It is not a way around documentation.

S13 SAFE + General Administration of Customs (via MOFCOM)

regulation · read: full text incl. Arts. 3-12 · retrieved 2026-11-11

S16 Internal Revenue Service
Report of Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts (FBAR)
U.S. government guidance · read: full page · retrieved 2026-11-11
  • A U.S. person must file an FBAR on FinCEN Form 114 to report financial interest in, or signature authority over, at least one foreign financial account if the aggregate value of those accounts exceeded USD 10,000 at any time during the calendar year
  • Whether the account produced taxable income has no effect on whether it is a foreign financial account for FBAR purposes
  • Due 15 April following the calendar year, with an automatic extension to 15 October
  • Civil penalty maximums in Title 31 are adjusted annually for inflation (S17)

Note: A CNY account in a Chinese bank is a foreign financial account for a U.S. person. This is a filing duty, independent of whether anything is converted.

S18 Internal Revenue Service
Do I need to file Form 8938, Statement of Specified Foreign Financial Assets?
U.S. government guidance · read: full page · retrieved 2026-11-11
  • Specified individuals holding specified foreign financial assets above the applicable threshold must file Form 8938 with the annual return
  • Unmarried taxpayer living in the U.S.: more than USD 50,000 on the last day of the year OR more than USD 75,000 at any time during the year
  • Married filing jointly, living in the U.S.: more than USD 100,000 on the last day OR more than USD 150,000 at any time
  • Taxpayer living abroad, non-joint return: more than USD 200,000 on the last day OR more than USD 300,000 at any time
  • Taxpayer living abroad, joint return: more than USD 400,000 on the last day OR more than USD 600,000 at any time

Note: The married-filing-jointly threshold of USD 100,000 last-day / USD 150,000 any-time is the one most likely to be tripped by a balance in this range.

S20 U.S. Code (Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School)
26 U.S.C. § 988 — Treatment of certain foreign currency transactions
statute (U.S.) · read: subsections (a)-(e) · retrieved 2026-11-11
  • (a)(1)(A): any foreign currency gain or loss attributable to a section 988 transaction is computed separately and treated as ORDINARY income or loss
  • (c)(1)(C)(i): a disposition of nonfunctional currency is treated as a section 988 transaction and the gain or loss is foreign currency gain or loss
  • (e)(2): for an individual's personal transaction, no gain is recognised by reason of exchange-rate changes between acquisition and disposition — 'The preceding sentence shall not apply if the gain which would otherwise be recognized on the transaction exceeds $200.'
  • (e)(3): 'personal transaction' excludes transactions whose allocable expenses meet section 162 or 212 requirements

Note: Ordinary treatment under (a)(1)(A) versus the $200 de-minimis personal-transaction exclusion in (e)(2) is the difference between a capital-gain line and nothing; it turns on whether the transaction is a personal transaction and on the size of the gain.

  • Art. 3(1): comprehensive income is taxed at progressive rates from 3% to 45%
  • Art. 3(2): business income at progressive rates from 5% to 35%

Note: This is a tax on the INCOME, assessed when the income arises. It does not attach to the act of exchanging CNY for USD. Any figure in this project labelled 'PRC tax before conversion' refers to this tax, not to a conversion charge.
