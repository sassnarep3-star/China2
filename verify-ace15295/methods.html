Methods

Ten route types — not twenty-three

Prior sections in this repository count 15 to 23 “methods” by counting each bank, card type and wallet as its own method. They are not: the choice of bank is a parameter (it changes the price, not the legal route), and a card that buys goods abroad is not a way to repatriate a balance. The reviewed instruments support the ten route types below. Each carries its eligibility, its legal basis, the exact steps, and its published pros and cons. Route 10 is unlawful and is listed only so its statutory price is visible — no steps are given.

How to read the tags. lawful means a reviewed instrument describes the route and does not prohibit it. capital means it sits under the capital account and needs SAFE approval, with the staging or documentation that implies. unlawful means a reviewed instrument attaches a penalty. None of these tags is an opinion about your file — a branch officer decides that.

The routes

1 · Documented current-account conversion at a bank, then one wire  lawful, documented

Who can use it: A non-domestic individual with legitimate China-source current-account income: salary, labour remuneration, rent, royalties, services.  ·  Basis: S06 S03

Exact steps

  1. Obtain the identity documents the account is held under; confirm with the bank whether it treats you as 境内个人 or 境外个人.
  2. Assemble source-of-funds evidence for the whole amount: employment contract and payslips, or service contract and invoices, plus the PRC personal income tax record (个人所得税纳税记录).
  3. Ask the bank for a written all-in quote: applied CNY/USD rate, sender fee, cable, correspondent deduction, and the receiving bank's charge.
  4. If a single outward payment will exceed the equivalent of USD 50,000, complete tax recordal (税务备案) first: log in to the provincial e-tax bureau, submit the form and the contract, keep the form number and verification code.
  5. Present the documents and the recordal reference at the branch or through the bank's channel and instruct the transfer to your own U.S. account.
  6. Keep the exchange memo, the SWIFT confirmation and the tax record. They are the audit trail at both ends.

Pros

  • No USD 50,000 ceiling: SAFE states genuinely lawful current-account settlement and purchase have no amount limit.
  • One transfer instead of a staged programme.
  • Cheapest published path in this review: 0.225% at USD 400,000.
  • Repeat conversions at the same bank are streamlined if the salary is continuing (汇发〔2021〕13号 Art. 5).

Cons

  • The bank decides what evidence is enough, and its review is a real gate.
  • Requires the income to be documented and, in practice, the PRC tax position to be on record.
  • The source-of-funds requirement is per tranche; a mixed balance needs every component evidenced.

Sources: S06 S05 S12 S09

2 · Same rule as an domestic individual (PRC citizen route)  lawful, documented

Who can use it: A PRC citizen holding a PRC identity card.  ·  Basis: S03 S05

Exact steps

  1. Within the annual quota: present valid ID only.
  2. Above the quota: present valid ID plus source materials for the settlement, per 汇发〔2007〕1号 Art. 10 and 12, or the purpose materials for a purchase.
  3. Remit from the FX savings account: up to the equivalent of USD 50,000 per day on ID, above that with documentary evidence of the transaction.
  4. Where the onward payment exceeds USD 50,000, complete tax recordal as in route 1.

Pros

  • Simplest document set inside the quota.
  • No fee or tax attaches to the quota itself.

Cons

  • Not available to a person whose only identity document is a foreign passport.
  • The quota resets each 1 January and unused quota does not carry over.

Sources: S03 S05 S02

3 · Annual facilitation quota using a PRC Foreign Permanent Resident ID  lawful, documented

Who can use it: A non-domestic individual who holds a PRC Foreign Permanent Resident ID ('Chinese green card').  ·  Basis: S07

Exact steps

  1. Confirm you hold the Foreign Permanent Resident ID.
  2. Convert within the annual USD 50,000 facilitation quota on ID.
  3. For anything above it, fall back to route 1's documented procedure.

Pros

  • Avoids the full documentation pack for amounts up to the quota each year.

Cons

  • Requires an immigration status most U.S. citizens do not hold.
  • Quota-only tier, so it cannot move USD 400,000 in one year.
  • Splitting a larger amount across years or people to stay inside it is the unlawful route R9.

Sources: S07 S03

4 · Re-conversion of unused renminbi on the original exchange memo  lawful

Who can use it: Anyone who previously converted foreign currency into RMB and has the original exchange memo.  ·  Basis: S08 S03

Exact steps

  1. Find the original exchange memo (兑换水单) for the conversion that produced the RMB.
  2. Present valid ID and the memo at the bank within 24 months of the exchange date.
  3. Same-day amounts up to USD 500 — or up to USD 1,000 at port-area outlets before departure — need ID only.

Pros

  • Fast and document-light where a memo exists.
  • Reverses a conversion rather than creating a new one.

Cons

  • Hard 24-month limit from the exchange date.
  • Only reverses what was converted in, so it cannot move income earned in RMB.

Sources: S08 S03

5 · Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移)  lawful, capital account, SAFE approval

Who can use it: A natural person who has moved from the mainland to a foreign country and is transferring lawful property owned before acquiring that status.  ·  Basis: S11

Exact steps

  1. Cancel PRC household registration and obtain the public-security cancellation certificate.
  2. Obtain a certificate of overseas residence issued or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate.
  3. Assemble property-rights documents and, where required, notarised translations.
  4. Apply once, to the SAFE branch where your household registration was, for the whole amount.
  5. The first remittance may not exceed half; after one year, up to half the remainder; after two years, the rest. The equivalent of CNY 200,000 or less may go in one approved transfer.
  6. Up to the equivalent of CNY 500,000 is approved locally; above that it goes to SAFE headquarters.

Pros

  • Expressly designed for moving a lifetime's lawful property abroad.
  • Not dependent on a current income stream.
  • The bank must remit directly to your own overseas account.

Cons

  • Requires giving up PRC household registration — a serious, irreversible step.
  • Staged over two years by rule, not by choice.
  • Above CNY 500,000 needs SAFE headquarters approval, so the timeline is not in your control.
  • Requires the tax certificate or tax-payment receipt for the property's location.

Sources: S11 S04

6 · Inheritance transfer (继承财产转移)  lawful, capital account, SAFE approval

Who can use it: A foreign citizen, or a Hong Kong/Macao resident, inheriting mainland property.  ·  Basis: S11

Exact steps

  1. Apply to the SAFE branch where the deceased was registered.
  2. Provide the inheritance documents, the relationship documents and the tax certificate or tax-payment receipt from the competent tax authority where the property is located.
  3. Apply once for all property from the same deceased person; it may be remitted in one or several transfers.
  4. Property inherited from different deceased persons requires separate applications.

Pros

  • Purpose-built for inherited funds.
  • One application per deceased person.

Cons

  • Only for inherited property, not for earned income.
  • Requires notarised inheritance documents and a PRC tax certificate.

Sources: S11

7 · Individual foreign-trade operator's FX settlement account  lawful, business account

Who can use it: A registered individual foreign-trade operator or sole trader conducting import/export.  ·  Basis: S05 S03

Exact steps

  1. Register as a foreign-trade operator and open an FX settlement account, managed as a corporate account.
  2. Settle and purchase against the genuine trade documents — the annual quota does not apply to this account.
  3. Keep customs and logistics documents as the authenticity base.

Pros

  • Outside the annual quota entirely.
  • Scales with real trade.

Cons

  • Only for genuine trade — it is not a personal-balance route.
  • Requires business registration and trade documentation.

Sources: S05 S03

8 · Carrying foreign-currency cash out  lawful within limits

Who can use it: Anyone; limited by amount and by what the branch will issue in cash.  ·  Basis: S13 S14 S15

Exact steps

  1. Up to the equivalent of USD 5,000: no certificate needed.
  2. USD 5,000 to USD 10,000: obtain a 携带外汇出境许可证 from the designated FX bank; customs releases against the bank's stamp.
  3. Above USD 10,000: a certificate from the local SAFE branch is required and is 'in principle' not permitted.
  4. Renminbi cash is capped at CNY 20,000 per person per trip.
  5. On entering the U.S. with more than USD 10,000, file FinCEN Form 105 — the threshold applies to the group total, not per person.

Pros

  • No wire fee, no correspondent deduction.
  • Immediate.

Cons

  • USD 10,000 per trip is 2.5% of USD 400,000 — 40 trips.
  • Cash is the widest-spread instrument: the cash buying rate is the worst published rate.
  • Physical risk plus a U.S. reporting duty the moment it exceeds USD 10,000.
  • Repeat trips to stay under the limits can itself attract scrutiny.

Sources: S13 S14 S15

9 · Card and mobile-wallet spending abroad (genuine consumption)  lawful for genuine consumption

Who can use it: Anyone spending on genuine personal consumption abroad.  ·  Basis: S05

Exact steps

  1. Spend directly on a mainland-issued card or wallet for real purchases.
  2. Note that genuine current-account consumption does not consume the annual settlement quota.

Pros

  • No settlement quota consumed for genuine consumption.
  • No wire fee.

Cons

  • Buys goods and services, not dollars in your account.
  • Merchant and card-network limits apply; it cannot repatriate a balance.

Sources: S05

10 · Conversions that are unlawful — listed for their penalties only  unlawful

Who can use it: Not available. Listed so the cost of the alternatives is visible.  ·  Basis: S01 S02

Exact steps

  1. No steps are given. The unlawful conduct is: splitting a large amount across relatives' or friends' annual quotas to evade limit management (PBOC Decree [2006] No.3 Art. 7); using false or invalid documents to obtain FX (Regulation 532 Art. 40); private or disguised buying and selling of FX, including through an underground bank (Art. 45); unlawful carriage of cash across the border (Art. 42).

Pros

  • None.

Cons

  • Evasion (逃汇): fine up to 30% of the amount; if serious, 30% up to the full amount. Regulation 532 Art. 39.
  • Illegal arbitrage (非法套汇) with false documents: up to 30%; serious 30%–100%. Art. 40.
  • Private or disguised FX dealing: warning, confiscation, up to 30%; serious 30%–100%, plus criminal liability. Art. 45.
  • Unlawful cash carriage: up to 20%. Art. 42. Invalid documents: up to CNY 50,000 for an individual. Art. 48.
  • Being placed on SAFE's 关注名单 removes the facilitation quota for the year of listing and the two following years.
  • At USD 400,000 a 30% floor is $120,000 and a 100% exposure is $400,000 — against $901.20 for the lawful route.

Sources: S01 S02 S05
