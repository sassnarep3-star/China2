Questions

The 14 questions that have to be answered before any decision is reasonable

This is the register the brief asked for: what needs to be addressed, what we already know, what we do not, why the gap blocks a decision, and the specific step that would close it. Three of them block everything. None of the three can be answered from anything in this repository — each needs a document only you or your bank holds.

The three that block everything. Q01 — are you a 境内个人 or a 境外个人? PBOC Decree [2006] No.3 Art. 37 splits the entire rule set on this, and Regulation 532 Art. 52(2) uses a different test again. Q02 — what is the documented source of the balance? Regulation 532 Art. 12 makes a real, lawful transaction basis mandatory. Q03 — has PRC tax been assessed on that income, and can you obtain the record? Without it, the “considerable tax” cannot be identified as a tax at all. S02 S01 S31

Priority 1 — nothing can be decided until these are answered

Q01 · Are you still a PRC citizen with a PRC resident identity card, or only a U.S. passport holder (with or without a PRC Foreign Permanent Resident ID)?

Why it blocks a decision: PBOC Decree [2006] No.3 Art. 37 splits all individual FX business into 境内个人 (PRC citizens holding a PRC ID/serviceman/police certificate) and 境外个人 (non-Chinese passports, HK/Macao/Taiwan travel-permit holders, stateless persons). The annual USD 50,000 purchase quota is written for 境内个人; a 境外个人 gets a quota only with a Foreign Permanent Resident ID. State Council Regulation 532 Art. 52(2) adds a second, different test — Chinese citizen OR a foreigner continuously resident in the PRC for one full year. The two tests are not the same, and which one the bank applies changes the documents you must produce.

What we already haveThe instruction 'as a US citizen'. That is not enough: dual status is common and the two definitions diverge.
If it stays unknownCannot choose between routes 1 and 3. Every cost table here is computed for BOTH statuses, and the eligibility column says which is available.
How to discover itRead the identity-document field the bank used when it opened the account (the account category is recorded per PBOC Decree Art. 28). Ask the bank in writing: 'Is this account held as 境内个人 or 境外个人, and under which article?' Check whether a Foreign Permanent Resident ID exists.
Official basisS02 S01 S07
Q02 · What is the documented source of this CNY balance — salary, business income, sale of property, inheritance, gift, or accumulated savings — and is there a paper trail for each tranche?

Why it blocks a decision: Regulation 532 Art. 12 requires a real, lawful transaction basis for every current-account FX receipt and payment, and the bank must reasonably review the documents. 汇发[2007]1号 Art. 10 and 13 require named source evidence (employment contract and income proof, tax record, inheritance documents, property-sale documents). Without a source you cannot use a current-account route at all, and no fee comparison matters.

What we already haveNothing. The balance's composition is unknown.
If it stays unknownRoute 1/2 unproven. The only realistic routes become route 5 (emigrant transfer, if household registration is cancelled) or route 6 (inheritance). Both are capital-account routes with SAFE approval, staged over two years (route 5).
How to discover itRequest from the bank: 12 months of statements plus the historical credit entries; from the tax authority: the personal income tax record (个人所得税纳税记录) via the provincial e-tax bureau or app; from any employer: contracts, payslips, withholding statements.

Q03 · Has PRC individual income tax been assessed and paid on the income that produced this balance — and can you obtain the 完税证明 or 个人所得税纳税记录?

Why it blocks a decision: PRC IIT Law Art. 3 taxes the income (3%-45% comprehensive; 20% on interest, dividends, rent and property transfer). The FX rules do not add a tax on conversion; they ask for evidence of the source, and Announcement 2013 No. 40 Art. 1 requires tax recordal for single outward payments above USD 50,000. Whether any tax is due depends on the income, not on the transfer.

What we already haveUnknown. Prior sections wrote '0% conversion tax' and, in places, '0% extra PRC tax if IIT was already withheld' — an assumption, not a fact.
If it stays unknownCannot tell whether the 'considerable tax' the bank mentioned is (a) tax already owed on the income, (b) a recordal step, or (c) a fee. This is exactly the ambiguity in the original question.
How to discover itLog in to the provincial e-tax bureau (自然人电子税务局) and download the personal tax record; or ask the withholding employer/agent for the withholding statement. Compare the record's total income with the balance.

Priority 2 — these decide the route and the U.S. exposure

Q04 · What did the bank actually say, in writing — which document did it request, and under which rule did it refuse?

Why it blocks a decision: 'A requested tax certificate', 'an actual tax assessment', 'an FX eligibility question' and 'a transfer fee' have completely different fixes. Announcement 2013 No. 40 Art. 6 says the tax authority need not examine the tax position at recordal. If the bank is asking for a 备案表 number, that is a free online filing, not a tax.

What we already haveA paraphrase ('without paying a considerable amount of taxes'). No document reference.
If it stays unknownThe cost model is being applied to a problem that may be a paperwork problem, not a money problem.
How to discover itAsk for the refusal in writing, naming: the tax type (if any), taxpayer, period, base, amount and legal basis; or the exact document list. Send the question through the bank's complaint channel so there is a record.
Official basisS09 S10 S06
Q05 · What is your U.S. tax basis in the CNY — the USD value when each tranche was received — and is your other U.S. taxable income in 2026 known?

Why it blocks a decision: 26 U.S.C. § 988(c)(1)(C) treats disposing of foreign currency as a section 988 transaction. For a personal transaction, § 988(e)(2) excludes the gain unless it exceeds USD 200; above that, § 988(a)(1)(A) makes it ordinary income unless an election applies. The size of the gain is (proceeds − basis), and the rate depends on your other income (Rev. Proc. 2025-32).

What we already haveNothing. No basis, no other income, no filing status.
If it stays unknownThe U.S. layer is shown as a labelled illustration across assumptions, never as your liability.
How to discover itReconstruct the USD value of each deposit at the spot rate on the day received (IRS accepts any consistently applied posted rate). Keep the statements; then compare with the conversion date.

Q06 · Have you filed FBAR (FinCEN 114) and, if thresholds are met, Form 8938 for the years this account existed?

Why it blocks a decision: A U.S. person must file an FBAR where aggregate foreign accounts exceeded USD 10,000 at any time in the year, and 8938 at USD 50,000/75,000 (single, in the U.S.) or USD 100,000/150,000 (MFJ, in the U.S.). Non-willful FBAR maxima are USD 16,536 per violation and willful maxima USD 165,353 (penalties assessed on or after 17 Jan 2025).

What we already haveNothing. Prior sessions flagged this as unknown too.
If it stays unknownA transfer that creates a visible USD inflow without the filings in place is the scenario in which the largest numbers in this project apply. Fees of 0.2% are irrelevant next to a USD 165,353 maximum.
How to discover itCheck prior returns and the FinCEN BSA e-filing history. If unfiled, get advice on delinquent filing or the Streamlined procedures before moving money — the answer changes the sequencing, not just the cost.

Priority 3 — these decide timing and price

Q07 · Where is your tax home and residency in 2026 — the U.S., mainland China, or split — and are you claiming the foreign earned income exclusion or foreign tax credit?

Why it blocks a decision: The 1984 U.S.-PRC treaty and the § 911 exclusion depend on residency and tax home; § 988(a)(3)(B) sets the source of currency gain by reference to the taxpayer's tax home. Filing status also changes the 8938 thresholds and the bracket the gain lands in.

What we already haveUnknown.
If it stays unknownTreaty and exclusion results are not asserted anywhere in this section; the illustrations show the U.S. statutory layer only.
How to discover itDetermine days present and tax home; if claiming § 911, the Form 2555 qualification must be documented. Compare with the treaty's saving clause before assuming treaty relief.

Q08 · Do you need the whole amount in one movement, and what is the actual deadline?

Why it blocks a decision: Route 5 (emigrant transfer) is expressly staged: half first, then up to half of the remainder after one year, the rest after two years (Announcement 2004 No. 16 Art. 6). The annual facilitation quota resets on 1 January and unused quota does not carry over.

What we already haveAn amount range (USD 10,000-400,000) but no deadline or liquidity need.
If it stays unknownCannot compare a one-off documented conversion against a two-year capital-account programme on a like-for-like basis.
How to discover itState the cash need and date. If a bank offers staging, get the schedule in writing.

Q09 · What is the bank's executable all-in quote today: applied CNY/USD rate, sender fee, cable, correspondent deduction and receiving-bank charge?

Why it blocks a decision: Every rate board reviewed carries a disclaimer that it is for reference and the transaction rate prevails. BOC's board says so explicitly; HSBC's says rates may change without notice. HSBC's tariff note 7 excludes other institutions' charges.

What we already haveReference boards only (BOC 6.6630/6.6911; CMB 6.66295/6.69199; HSBC 6.6411822/6.7079204) and published fee schedules.
If it stays unknownEvery total in this project is a lower bound on a reference rate, labelled as such.
How to discover itAsk for a written quote for the exact amount and date, plus the intermediary/correspondent bank's deduction and the receiving bank's inward fee.

Q10 · Do you have a lawful need and a compliant account in Hong Kong or elsewhere that could receive the funds, and would that actually reduce the cost?

Why it blocks a decision: Moving money to a third jurisdiction does not remove the PRC documentation requirement at the point of purchase, and it adds a second FX conversion and a second set of receiving fees. Nothing in the reviewed sources offers a lower-tax path for the same balance.

What we already haveNothing. No third-country account is known to exist.
If it stays unknownThird-country routing is listed in the methods table with its extra layers and left unpriced.

Priority 4 — these close the remaining gaps

Q11 · Is any part of this balance realistically available as FX cash, and do you want to carry it?

Why it blocks a decision: Carrying FX cash out is capped in practice: up to USD 5,000 needs no certificate, USD 5,000-10,000 needs a bank certificate, above USD 10,000 needs a SAFE certificate and is 'in principle' not allowed. RMB cash is capped at CNY 20,000 per person per trip. Entering the U.S. with more than USD 10,000 requires a FinCEN 105 report.

What we already haveNo information about whether the balance can be withdrawn as cash at all.
If it stays unknownCash is listed, costed at the cash spread where published, and marked impractical for the amounts in question.
How to discover itAsk the bank whether FX cash is available at the branch and in what denominations; confirm the reporting duty at the U.S. end before travelling.

Q12 · Is the shortfall the bank describes a currency-conversion loss (spread), a tax on income, or a compliance cost — and has anyone put a number on each?

Why it blocks a decision: The seven layers are separable and only some are avoidable: spread (shoppable), wire/cable/correspondent fees (partly shoppable), tax recordal (free), PRC income tax (fixed by the income, not the transfer), U.S. currency gain (usually under USD 200), U.S. reporting (a filing duty), and penalties for unlawful routes (avoidable only by not using them).

What we already haveNo breakdown was provided.
If it stays unknownThe cost tables still give the number for every layer that can be computed from published inputs.
How to discover itUse the Penalties page: it prices each layer at each USD 10,000 step and marks which are avoidable.
