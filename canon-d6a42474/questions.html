Decision register · 16 questions

What has to be answered before any decision

Each question below is gating: the route or the cost changes depending on the answer. None of them is answered by this repository, because every one depends on private facts. Each has a stated way to find out.

How to use this page. Answer Q01–Q05 first. Until Q01 (origin) and Q02 (PRC tax status) are answered, no route can be selected and no tax figure can be computed. Q05 is the largest quantified risk in the file: unfiled U.S. reports carry penalties far larger than any bank fee.

The register

Q01 · Where did the RMB come from, tranche by tranche?gating

What we know

Nothing in this repo establishes it. The brief says only that the money is 'stuck'.

How to find out

Classify every large credit as salary, business profit, property sale, inheritance, pre-emigration property, dividend/interest, gift or something else. Pull five years of statements.

Why it decides something

The origin decides the route (M01 vs M07 vs M08) and the PRC tax rate (S17 Art. 3). Nothing can be computed without it.

Source: S17

Q02 · Has PRC tax already been paid on the income, and do you hold the tax record?gating

What we know

Not known. The bank's gate under S02 Art. 13(1) and S06 is a source-of-funds file that includes tax vouchers.

How to find out

Download the 个人所得税 App payment records for each earning year; request 完税证明 at the tax hall.

Why it decides something

This is the actual gate on the cheap route. Without it no bank will run M01.

Source: S02 S06

Q03 · Are you a 境内个人 or a 境外个人 for FX purposes?gating

What we know

Unknown. Two official texts define 'domestic individual' differently (F12).

How to find out

Ask the bank in writing which category it uses for your account, and whether a Foreign Permanent Resident ID changes the answer.

Why it decides something

It decides whether the USD 50,000 annual total (M06) applies to your purchase at all.

Source: S01 S02

Q04 · When was each tranche acquired, and at what USD basis?gating

What we know

Unknown. IRS reference averages are published: 7.129 (2025), 7.189 (2024), 7.075 (2023), 6.730 (2022), 6.452 (2021) (S13).

How to find out

Export bank credits by date and convert each with a consistently applied posted rate.

Why it decides something

It decides whether there is an exchange gain above the $200 personal-transaction line (S12).

Source: S12 S13

Q05 · What is your U.S. offshore reporting history?gating

What we know

Unknown. FBAR is required above $10,000 aggregate at any time (S10); maximum civil penalties are $16,536 non-willful and $165,353 willful (S11).

How to find out

Pull the peak balance for each calendar year and compare with the FBAR/8938/3520 filings actually made.

Why it decides something

Unfiled reports are the largest quantified risk in the whole file — larger than any bank fee.

Source: S10 S11

Q06 · What will the bank actually charge and at what rate?gating

What we know

Only published tariffs are known: HSBC 0.1% min CNY 100 max CNY 500 + CNY 120 cable (S09); BOC 0.1% min CNY 50 max CNY 260 + CNY 150 cable (S08); board 668.86 per 100 USD at 15:30:04 on 2026-11-11 (S07).

How to find out

Request a written quote showing the applied rate, commission, cable, correspondent and receiving-bank charges.

Why it decides something

The published spread and fees are a floor; the executed quote is the number that binds.

Source: S07 S08 S09

Q07 · What do the correspondent and receiving banks charge?gating

What we know

Not published by either Chinese bank (F13).

How to find out

Ask both banks for the intermediary and beneficiary-bank charge, or use an OUR/'Pay Exact' instruction (CNY 200 at HSBC, S09) and check the credit.

Why it decides something

It is the only part of the wire cost that no official page in this repo quantifies.

Source: S09

Q08 · Can someone in China run the formalities if you are abroad?gating

What we know

The rules allow an agent for the annual-total operations and for the property-transfer application (S02 Art. 4; S03 Art. 7), with ID, authorisation and, for relatives, proof of relationship.

How to find out

Ask the bank and the SAFE branch what authorisation they will accept; the notarisation requirement for property documents was removed (S04 VI(4)).

Why it decides something

It determines whether you must travel or can execute remotely.

Source: S02 S03 S04

Q09 · Is your PRC household registration still active?gating

What we know

Unknown.

How to find out

Check the hukou status. The emigrant property route (M07) requires cancellation and a pre-departure tax settlement (S03 Art. 8; S17 Art. 10(5)).

Why it decides something

It rules the emigrant route in or out.

Source: S03 S17

Q10 · Is any part of the balance an inheritance?gating

What we know

Unknown.

How to find out

Identify the deceased, the will/probate documents and the PRC tax certificate for the estate.

Why it decides something

Inheritance has its own route and its own documents (M08) and triggers Form 3520 above $100,000 (S15).

Source: S03 S15

Q11 · What exactly is the 'considerable tax' you were quoted?gating

What we know

Not identified anywhere in this repo. No reviewed text taxes the act of converting CNY to USD (F01).

How to find out

Ask for the assessment or quote in writing and identify whether it is IIT on the income, a penalty, a withholding, or a bank charge.

Why it decides something

If it is IIT on the origin, no conversion route avoids it; if it is a penalty, the facts and dates decide the exposure.

Source: S01 S17

Q12 · Which U.S. account will receive the USD, and in whose name?gating

What we know

Unknown.

How to find out

Confirm the receiving bank's incoming-wire details, the name match, and whether it charges.

Why it decides something

A third-party name on the receiving end breaks the 'own account' logic of every lawful route here.

Source: S02

Q13 · Why is the money 'stuck' today?gating

What we know

Unknown: bank refusal, missing documents, quota, a freeze, or a compliance review are different problems.

How to find out

Ask the bank for the refusal in writing, with the rule it relies on.

Why it decides something

It decides whether this is a documentation problem or a legal one.

Source: S02

Q14 · How much is in the account, and in what currency?gating

What we know

Unknown. Only the brief's range of USD 10,000 to USD 400,000 is used for the cost tables.

How to find out

State the balance and currency composition.

Why it decides something

The fixed fee stops mattering above roughly USD 74,754, while the margin grows linearly — the mix changes with size.

Source: S07 S09

Q15 · How urgent is the transfer?gating

What we know

Unknown.

How to find out

Set a deadline and a rate tolerance.

Why it decides something

Waiting is free of fees but keeps full currency exposure (M12).

Source: S07

Q16 · Who is authorised to advise you in each country?gating

What we know

Unknown. This repo is research, not advice.

How to find out

Engage a PRC tax adviser and a U.S. cross-border CPA; give each the facts from Q01–Q05.

Why it decides something

The two tax systems interact; the answer needs both.
