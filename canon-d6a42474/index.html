The tax is rarely the problem. The evidence is.

For a U.S. citizen with RMB in a mainland bank, the cheapest lawful route that moves the whole balance in one instruction is one documented conversion and one wire: about $900.30 on $400,000 at Bank of China's published e-banking fee and board, or $931.70 at HSBC Premier/Advance. Almost all of it is the bank's exchange margin. Carrying cash is marginally cheaper in fees but needs 40 declared trips; a balance already in USD would need only the wire. No tax on the conversion itself appears in any text read here — what the bank gates is proof of where the money came from.

Answer first

If the RMB is already-taxed salary or business income

One documented bank conversion plus one wire is the route to assess first (M01 / M03). The statute does not restrict current-account payments S01, and SAFE's 2026 guidance confirms an overseas individual converts legitimate current-account RMB income on ID plus source-of-funds documents including tax vouchers S06. The gate is the tax record, not a tax.

If the money is pre-emigration property or an inheritance

Use the property-transfer route (M07 / M08): one application to SAFE where the household was registered, with the tax certificate and the ownership documents S03. The staged remittance and the above-RMB-500,000 head-office filing were abolished in 2014 S04 — pages that still describe staging are out of date (F10).

If the income was never taxed in China

No route removes the PRC tax on the income. Rates are 3%–45% comprehensive, 5%–35% business and 20% flat on property, interest, dividends and royalties S17. Settle or document it first; a bank will ask for the tax record anyway S02.

What not to do

Splitting across relatives' annual totals, underground swaps and crypto bridges are unlawful. The FX statute prices evasion at up to 30% of the amount, or 30%–100% in serious cases S01; virtual-currency exchange is an illegal financial activity and the civil act is void S05. They are listed, without instructions, so they are not re-discovered as ideas.

The U.S. side is reporting, not a transfer tax. Moving your own principal is not income. Exchange gain on a personal transaction is ignored unless it exceeds $200 S12; the 2026 foreign earned income exclusion is $132,900 for qualifying earned income S14. The expensive mistakes are unreported accounts and unfiled forms: FBAR above $10,000 aggregate, with maximum civil penalties of $16,536 (non-willful) and $165,353 (willful) S10 S11.

What it costs to move the money

Published sender fees converted at HSBC's and BOC's own renminbi tariffs, plus the full one-way exchange margin from the Bank of China board of 2026-11-11 15:30:04 (668.86 CNY per USD). The margin is measured against the board midpoint of 667.46. Intermediary, receiving-bank and U.S. adviser costs are not included; they are not published by these banks (F13).
