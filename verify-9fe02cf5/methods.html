M01
Onshore bank conversion of documented current-account income, then outward SWIFT wire (SHA)		LAWFULNo USD 50,000 cap on the document route; the bank reviews the transaction evidence.$92.66 at $400,000
HSBC China Premier/Advance outward TT, sender pays own bank charges (SHA)
  • Expressly contemplated by the FX rules: identity plus transaction/source evidence, including tax documents where required.
  • No statutory limit on when the old income is converted.
  • Can be entrusted to another person with authorisation and documents if the holder is abroad.
  • At the tariff read here the sender-side fee is capped (CNY 500 + CNY 120 cable), so it does not scale with the amount.
  • The bank's due-diligence decision is discretionary; missing contemporaneous proof of the original income is the usual failure point.
  • Tax vouchers may have to be obtained first (a PRC tax filing, not a conversion fee).
  • FX spread and any correspondent/intermediary deductions are not published in the tariff and remain unverified.
  • Nothing here establishes that any particular balance qualifies.
Step by step
  1. Obtain the original evidence of the RMB income: contract, payroll records, invoices, tax payment certificates.
  2. If PRC individual income tax was not withheld, settle the PRC filing at the competent tax authority and keep the certificate.
  3. Ask the bank in writing which category it will book the conversion under and what document list it requires.
  4. Complete the tax recordal only if the bank/payee structure requires it for a service-trade payment.
  5. Instruct the conversion and the outward wire on the same visit; request the applied CNY/USD rate and an itemised fee slip.
  6. Keep the exchange memo, the wire confirmation and the rate evidence: they are needed for US basis and reporting.

M02
Same wire with 'Pay Exact' (OUR) so the receiving bank charges are absorbed		LAWFULNo amount cap in the tariff; CNY 200 per instruction at the bank read here.$122.55 at $400,000
HSBC China Premier/Advance outward TT with Pay Exact (OUR) at CNY 200
  • The published tariff states the beneficiary receives the full USD proceeds, with any shortfall collected later.
  • Removes uncertainty about receiving-bank deductions for the principal.
  • Same document route as M01, so no additional eligibility test.
  • Adds a fixed CNY 200 per instruction.
  • Any shortfall in overseas bank charges is still collected from the remitter afterwards.
  • Does not remove the FX spread, which is the larger cost at high amounts.
Step by step
  1. Complete the M01 document and conversion steps.
  2. At instruction, tick the 'Pay Exact' / OUR option.
  3. Confirm on the receipt that OUR was recorded and that the fee slip shows the CNY 200 item.
  4. Retain the confirmation showing the exact USD amount the beneficiary received.

M03
Outward wire through a Private Banking individual account		LAWFULNo amount cap in the tariff.$0.00 at $400,000
HSBC China Private Banking individual outward TT and cable (both published free)
  • The tariff read here publishes the telegraphic-transfer issuance fee and the cable charge as free for individual Private Banking customers.
  • Same eligibility and document route as M01.
  • Requires Private Banking status, which the holder may not have.
  • Free sender fees do not mean free conversion: the spread is not published.
  • Third-party and correspondent charges are expressly excluded from the tariff.
Step by step
  1. Confirm the account is booked as a Private Banking individual account.
  2. Obtain the same document set as M01.
  3. Instruct the wire and obtain the fee slip showing zero commission and zero cable.
  4. Record the applied rate, since the fee saving is trivial next to the spread.

M06
Annual USD 50,000 facilitation quota, in yearly tranches		CONDITIONALUSD 50,000 equivalent per person per year.— Bank fee for quota-based purchase not read in this session
  • Simple identity-based route inside the quota, without a transaction-by-transaction source review.
  • Available to a domestic individual or a PR-ID holder.
  • A foreign individual without a PR ID is outside this route.
  • Splitting a balance across people or years to defeat the quota is a separate unlawful act.
  • Takes 8 calendar years for USD 400,000 even if everything else is perfect.
Step by step
  1. Establish which category the holder falls into, in writing, before relying on this route.
  2. Convert up to the quota each calendar year and wire it out.
  3. Keep every conversion memo so the tranches can be traced.
  4. Do not use family members' quotas to accelerate the schedule.

M07
Overseas ATM cash withdrawals on a mainland debit card		LAWFULRMB 100,000 equivalent per person per civil year; RMB 10,000 equivalent per card per day.— ATM operator and card-issuer charges not read in this session
  • Uses an existing card; no documentary review at the counter.
  • Useful for spending needs rather than moving a balance.
  • Hard annual ceiling, and exceeding it blocks overseas cash withdrawal for the current and following year.
  • Requires many withdrawals and many years for large sums.
  • Cash still has to be deposited in the US and is subject to the USD 10,000 border-reporting rule if carried.
  • Card and ATM fees are not published in the documents read here.
Step by step
  1. Check the card's own overseas withdrawal limit and fee with the issuer.
  2. Withdraw within RMB 10,000 equivalent per card per day.
  3. Track the cumulative civil-year total against RMB 100,000.
  4. Deposit the cash in a US account and keep the withdrawal slips.

M08
Reconversion of unspent RMB using the original exchange memo		CONDITIONALOriginal memo valid 24 months; ID-only handling up to USD 500/day (USD 1,000 at port facilities).— Fee not read in this session
  • A clear rule with a defined document: the original exchange memo.
  • Small same-day amounts need only ID.
  • Does not apply to RMB earned in China, which is the usual case.
  • The 24-month validity has usually expired for a long-stuck balance.
  • Small daily limits make it irrelevant for USD 400,000.
Step by step
  1. Locate the original exchange memo and check its date.
  2. Present ID and the memo at the bank within 24 months of the exchange.
  3. For larger amounts, expect the bank to apply the general document route instead.

M09
Capital-account transfer: emigration, inheritance or property proceeds		CONDITIONALGoverned by the separate provisional rules; not re-read in this session.— Fee not read in this session
  • A recognised route with its own rulebook rather than the current-account document route.
  • Can be the only route for genuinely capital-account money.
  • Heavy documentation and approval steps; the specific rulebook was not verified here.
  • Timeline and acceptance vary by locality.
  • Estate and property paperwork is usually the real bottleneck.
Step by step
  1. Identify whether the balance is capital-account money at all (property sale, inheritance, pre-emigration assets).
  2. Obtain the SAFE local-branch document list for that category in writing.
  3. Assemble the title, tax-clearance and identity documents.
  4. Obtain approval before instructing any conversion.

Third-party remittance apps or agents (Wise, Western Union, 'agents')		CONDITIONALProvider-specific and mostly undocumented in official sources.— Provider fee and availability not verified in this session
  • May be cheaper for small amounts if genuinely available.
  • Convenient interface.

REJECTED: cryptocurrency / USDT / stablecoin bridge		REJECTEDProhibited.— Not applicable
  • Advertised as fast and outside the banking system.

  1. Do not use this route.
