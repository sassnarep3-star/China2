Questions that must be answered before any decision

Each answer selects which methods are even available and therefore which rows of the cost table apply. Nothing here is assumed on the holder's behalf.

Q01 What is the lawful origin of the RMB balance?

Origin selects the entire route: salary/labour income (current account), property sale, inheritance, pre-emigration assets (capital account), or business proceeds.

What we know

PRC rules split current-account and capital-account money and require a genuine, lawful transaction basis (C01, C02).

What we do not know

The origin of this balance.

How to discover it

  1. Obtain the bank statement history and the original credit advices.
  2. Match each credit to its underlying contract, payroll record, sale contract or estate document.
  3. Classify each tranche as current account or capital account before choosing a route.

Q02 Was PRC individual income tax withheld and paid on that income, and is there a tax certificate?

For a foreign individual, the conversion requires source-of-funds materials including tax documents (C06). Unpaid PRC tax is the layer that actually costs 3%-45% or a flat 20%.

What we know

The rule requires tax documents; the IIT rates are 3%-45% / 5%-35% / 20% (C06, C14).

What we do not know

Whether tax was withheld, whether certificates exist, and whether any period remains unfiled.

How to discover it

  1. Request the individual income tax payment records from the competent tax authority.
  2. Ask the former employer for withholding certificates.
  3. If nothing was withheld, obtain advice on the filing position before approaching the bank.

Q03 How does SAFE classify the holder: domestic individual (境内个人) or foreign individual (境外个人), and is there a Foreign Permanent Resident ID?

The USD 50,000 annual purchase quota applies to domestic individuals and only to foreign individuals holding a PR ID (C04, C07).

What we know

The quota wording and the PR-ID condition (C04, C07).

What we do not know

The holder's SAFE category and PR-ID status.

How to discover it

  1. Check residence days and domicile against the definition in the FX regulations.
  2. Ask the bank, in writing, which category it will book the account under.
  3. If a PR ID exists, confirm it is accepted for quota purposes at that branch.

Q04 When was each CNY tranche acquired, and what is its USD basis?

US tax on any exchange gain depends on the acquisition-date rate against the conversion-date rate, and the USD 200 personal-transaction threshold (C18).

What we know

Section 988(e) excludes personal transactions and only excludes gain of USD 200 or less; translation is at the applicable spot rate (C17, C18).

What we do not know

Acquisition dates and rates, and therefore the size of any gain.

How to discover it

  1. Build a tranche table: date, CNY amount, spot or consistently posted rate that day, USD basis.
  2. Keep the source for each rate used.
  3. Do not assume a gain is zero because the money 'just sat there'.

Q05 What is the US reporting history for this account?

An account above USD 10,000 aggregate is already an FBAR item, and Form 8938 thresholds can be crossed while the money is still in China (C20).

What we know

FBAR threshold and 8938 thresholds and penalties (C20, C21).

What we do not know

Whether prior FBAR/8938 filings exist, and whether any foreign gift or trust reporting applies.

How to discover it

  1. Check filed returns for FinCEN 114 and Form 8938 for each year the account existed.
  2. If anything is missing, consider the IRS compliance procedures before moving money.
  3. Keep a written chronology.

Q06 What is the bank's executable all-in quote today, itemised?

The spread is the dominant cost at large amounts and it is not published; only an executable quote settles it (F12).

What we know

The published tariff items that can be read (C23) and a dated published board benchmark (C25).

What we do not know

The applied rate, the spread, and any correspondent or receiving-bank deduction for this account.

How to discover it

  1. Ask for a written quote stating the applied CNY/USD rate, commission, cable, intermediary and receiving charges.
  2. Compare the quoted rate with the published board on the same timestamp.
  3. Ask what the beneficiary will actually receive in USD.

Q07 If the money is capital-account money, are the title, tax-clearance and approval documents in hand?

Property, inheritance and pre-emigration transfers follow a separate rulebook with approval before conversion (C02, C11).

What we know

Such transfers are governed by separate provisional rules (C11).

What we do not know

The local document list, the approval status, and whether the specific rulebook applies.

How to discover it

  1. Request the SAFE local-branch document list in writing.
  2. Assemble title documents, tax clearance and identity documents.
  3. Obtain approval before instructing conversion.

Q08 Is the holder physically in China, or abroad and needing a proxy?

The remittance formality may be entrusted to another person with authorisation and the full document set (C09).

What we know

Proxy is expressly permitted for beyond-quota and foreign-individual purchases (C09).

What we do not know

The branch's practice on notarised authorisations and whether it will accept a company finance officer as agent.

How to discover it

  1. Ask the branch for its authorisation template and notarisation requirement.
  2. Prepare the authorisation, both IDs and the full document set.
  3. Confirm in writing that the agent can sign the conversion and wire.

Q09 Is the goal liquid USD in a US bank, or spending capacity?

Spending capacity has different ceilings (card/POS, ATM RMB 100,000 a year) from a lump-sum conversion (C12).

What we know

The ATM ceiling and the daily card ceiling (C12).

What we do not know

The actual need and timing.

How to discover it

  1. Quantify how much must be liquid versus spent over time.
  2. If liquidity is required, only the document or capital-account routes can deliver it at scale.

Q10 Which bank holds the money, and what is its published tariff?

Fees, spreads and acceptance practice are bank-specific; only one bank's tariff was read here (C23, F03).

What we know

One bank's published outward-remittance tariff (C23) and one published rate board (C25).

What we do not know

The holding bank's tariff and spread.

How to discover it

  1. Obtain the holding bank's current tariff PDF and its rate board.
  2. Compare the two with the numbers here before relying on any fee figure.

Q11 Has any 'fast' solution been offered - pooling relatives' quotas, a crypto/USDT bridge, or an underground swap?

Each of those is separately prohibited, with penalties of up to 30% or more of the amount (C16, C26).

What we know

The prohibitions and penalties (C16, C26).

What we do not know

What has been proposed to the holder.

How to discover it

  1. Write down the proposal and refuse it.
  2. Record who proposed it; if money has already moved, get advice before any further step.

Q12 What is the plan for the US side after the money arrives?

A credit is only usable if the PRC tax was paid and documented, and FEIE-excluded income cannot also generate a credit (C19).

What we know

Form 1116 mechanics, the one-year-back/ten-year-forward carry, and the FEIE/credit conflict (C19).

What we do not know

Whether the holder uses FEIE, and whether any PRC tax will be creditable at the treaty rate.

How to discover it

  1. Decide FEIE versus credit before the year closes.
  2. Keep PRC tax certificates and the conversion rate evidence.
  3. Report the account on FBAR/8938 as applicable.
