F01 The 5%-per-month / 25% cap figure for Form 3520 was not re-verifiable in this session

Earlier pages in this repository state 'Form 3520 gift/bequest: 5%/mo up to 25%'. This session re-read IRC §6677 (foreign trust information) and found the penalty there is the greater of USD 10,000 or 35% of the gross reportable amount, with 5% substituted for §6048(b) returns and USD 10,000 per 30 days after notice. The gift/bequest 5%-per-month figure belongs to a different provision (IRC §6039F) that this session could not retrieve at the govinfo and OLRC URLs tried. The figure is therefore neither confirmed nor contradicted here.

UNVERIFIED · Affects: transfer-guide/taxes.html, transfer-guide/penalties.html, study-8f529c94/taxes.html (not edited by this session) · Sources: S13

F02 Characterisation of a personal exchange gain above USD 200 is not stated in §988

Section 988(e)(1) removes personal transactions from §988, and §988(e)(2) excludes recognition only where the gain does not exceed USD 200. The statute does not, in that text, say whether a larger personal exchange gain is capital or ordinary. Earlier pages describe it as a capital gain. That characterisation was not verified here and is not used in any computed row.

UNVERIFIED · Affects: transfer-guide/data/penalties_and_taxes.json (TX-07), estimate-facf2fe9/tax.html · Sources: S12

F03 The FX spread for any actual account is unknown

No PRC rule and no reviewed tariff publishes the spread a bank applies to a customer conversion. The cost table therefore uses a dated, published benchmark band derived from Bank of China's own buying and selling rates (0.420% on 2026-11-11) and labels it as a benchmark, never as the holder's cost. The account-specific spread stays blank.

F10 The PRC tax position of the balance is unknown

If the money is untaxed PRC-source income, the cost is the PRC income tax (3%-45%, 5%-35% or flat 20%), not any conversion charge. If it was already taxed and documented, the conversion can cost nothing extra in PRC tax. The facts are not available.

F12 The Bank of China board is a benchmark, not the holder's quote

The 0.420% band is derived from two prices one bank published at one timestamp. It is not the spread of the bank that holds the balance, and it is not an executable rate.

The eCFR table read on 2026-11-11 is headed 'penalties assessed on or after January 17, 2025' and its last amendment note is 90 FR 5630 (17 January 2025). No later amendment appears in the rendering read. The figures are quoted with that date attached rather than presented as timeless.

F14 Absence of a conversion tax in the reviewed instruments is not proof that no tax applies

The FX regulations and implementing rules regulate eligibility, documents and penalties. Not finding a conversion tax in them is an absence-of-evidence result. It says nothing about PRC income tax on the underlying money or about US tax.
