Two corrections to earlier pages in this repository

Foreign trust vs foreign gift. The foreign-trust information penalty in IRC §6677 is the greater of $10,000 or 35% (5% substituted for §6048(b) returns) plus $10,000 per 30 days after notice (S13). The widely quoted '5% per month up to 25%' belongs to a different provision that could not be retrieved here; it is left unverified on F01 rather than repeated.

Characterisation above $200. Section 988(e) removes personal transactions from §988 and excludes recognition only up to $200. It does not say the excess is capital gain, so that description is not used anywhere in this site (F02).
