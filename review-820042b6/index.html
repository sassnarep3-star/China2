Executive summary

First distinguish tax owed on the income from documentary requirements and exchange/transfer fees. If the funds are documented lawful current-account income of an overseas individual, ask the account bank whether it will process direct conversion and remittance on that basis. SAFE Tianjin says identity and source materials showing transaction amounts, including tax vouchers, are required; that does not establish a particular tax bill or guarantee bank acceptance. SAFE Tianjin, 2026 FAQ.

If the funds are documented wages, SAFE Henan says no regulatory time limit is specified for conversion/remittance of lawful RMB income, subject to evidence and bank authenticity review. It also says the individual may entrust others to handle remittance; confirm execution, identity and authorization requirements with the bank. SAFE Henan, Q5 · SAFE Henan, Q3.

U.S. citizens generally report worldwide taxable income, and foreign-currency gain may have U.S. tax consequences. Foreign tax credits can reduce U.S. tax when applicable but are subject to limits; they do not guarantee elimination of double taxation. Check past FBAR and other filings with a qualified cross-border adviser. IRS worldwide income · IRS Publication 525, Foreign currency transactions · IRS foreign tax credit.
