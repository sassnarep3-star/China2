Source links provide rule/reference context. They do not verify the blank cost cells. A real estimate requires an eligible route, executable quote, exact debit/credit, all fees, and lot-level tax basis.

How to complete a real estimate

  1. Identify the source of each CNY deposit and whether the account is personal, business or investment.
  2. For each lot disposed of, record acquisition date, CNY amount, USD basis and the exchange rate used.
  3. Record the disposition date, executed CNY/USD rate, gross USD proceeds and all sender/intermediary/receiver fees.
  4. Classify the gain under section 988 and the personal-transaction exception; if capital, determine holding period and rate bracket.
  5. Compute tax only after tax year, filing status, taxable income, foreign taxes and credit limitations are known.
