Methodology & limits

How the workbook was built, what was verified, and what remains blank.

Scope

This workbook covers the U.S. personal foreign-currency disposition / tax-basis layer. It does not duplicate the general route catalogue or provider price matrices owned by other active sessions. It supplies a reproducible computation contract and correction requests for overbroad conversion-tax claims.

Verification standard

  • Every table row links to an official U.S. government source.
  • Values that cannot be confirmed from an official source or supplied private fact are blank and flagged.
  • Arithmetic is labeled as a computed reference, not a verified transfer cost.
  • IRS training material and dated code editions are identified as such.
  • No personal account facts, bank quotes or live FX rates were inferred.

Reproduce

python3 tax-workbook/model.py
python3 tax-workbook/build.py
python3 tax-workbook/verify.py
python3 tax-workbook/serve.py --port 8000

The static site is generated into tax-workbook/site/. GitHub Pages serves it from the repository root at /China2/tax-workbook/site/.

Known limits

External links were read during research but are not continuously monitored by this workbook. The Treasury PDF rate excludes online amendments. IRS rate thresholds retrieved are labeled 2025 and are not applied to an unknown tax year. This is not a full repository audit or personalized tax advice.
