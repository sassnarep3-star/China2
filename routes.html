Transfer routes.

Options depend on the legal source of funds. A payment method is not an exemption from exchange controls or tax.

Scroll horizontally on smaller screens to view the complete table. Source links describe the rule or transaction, not a guarantee of individual eligibility.

Foreign investment proceedsForeign-investment law implementation guidance lists capital contributions, profits, capital gains, asset-disposal earnings and liquidation proceeds as remittable in RMB or foreign currency.Potential route for qualifying foreign investment.Not a blanket personal-account exemption; ownership, investment registration, tax and bank review matter.Official source ↗
Inheritance / personal property transferThe amended SAFE detailed rules identify outward transfer of domestic inheritance lawfully inherited by foreign citizens as governed by personal-property transfer rules.Possible dedicated route for a genuine inheritance.Applicability, documents, taxes and approval process need confirmation; not a general channel for wages.Official source ↗
Property sale proceedsSAFE guidance discusses exchange and remittance of lawful domestic property transfer income of overseas individuals.Possible source-specific bank route.Requires verifying applicable current rules, ownership, sale and tax documents with bank; no rate established here.Official source ↗
Overseas ATM withdrawal using a Chinese cardSAFE describes overseas cash withdrawals with domestic bank cards.Useful for modest travel cash if the card and network work.Cash rather than a US bank transfer; SAFE states annual RMB 100,000 per person and RMB 10,000 per card per day; bank fees and FX rates unknown.Official source ↗
Spend overseas with a domestic cardSAFE distinguishes overseas card purchases from ATM withdrawals.May pay genuine expenses directly.Does not transfer a balance to a US account; merchant acceptance, limits and pricing vary.Official source ↗
Carry RMB cash and exchange abroadChina’s government guide states a per-trip RMB cash carrying limit for visitors.Can cover small legitimate travel needs.The guide states RMB 20,000 per trip; US reporting and local exchange availability apply; theft and unfavorable spreads are risks.Official source ↗

Cross-border RMB then offshore conversionForeign-investment implementation guidance mentions remittance in RMB as well as foreign currency for qualifying foreign investors.Allows comparison of onshore and offshore quotes if bank offers the permitted transaction.Not verified for an ordinary personal savings transfer; receiving-bank acceptance, compliance and costs unknown.Official source ↗
