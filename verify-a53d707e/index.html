What this pass means for the answer

  1. The "$50,000 quota" is not available to a U.S. citizen without a Chinese permanent-resident ID. SAFE's own 2024 policy answer says a foreign individual's lawful current-account RMB income is purchased at a bank against ID and transaction/source evidence — and that the annual facilitation quota applies to foreign individuals only if they hold a PRC Foreign Permanent Resident ID card. SAFE Tianjin, 2024 Q&A case (9)
  2. None of the PRC instruments re-read here imposes a separate "conversion tax". The PRC instruments re-read here impose documentation and authenticity duties on the bank, and the PRC income-tax statute taxes income by category. Whether tax is due depends on what created the balance and whether it was already taxed — and that is unknown, so it stays blank. SAFE 2026 FAQ (08) · PRC IIT Law
  3. The published bank fee is small; the unpublished FX spread is not. On HSBC China's tariff effective 31 October 2026 a Premier/Advance outward telegraphic transfer is 0.1% of the amount, minimum CNY 100, maximum CNY 500, plus a CNY 120 cable charge; Private Banking individuals are shown as Free for both items. That caps the published sender fee at CNY 620 (about $86.97 at the IRS 2025 average rate) regardless of size. The spread — the actual price of the conversion — is not published anywhere and is left blank. HSBC China tariff, items 2.2.4.1–2.2.4.2 · IRS yearly averages
  4. The U.S. side is a reporting risk, not a conversion tax. A personal foreign-currency transaction is outside §988, and no gain is recognised on it unless the gain exceeds $200; above that a gain is recognised under the general rules. Separately, FBAR and Form 8938 carry fixed civil penalties that dwarf the bank fee. 26 U.S.C. §988(e) · 31 CFR 1010.821

I01 The briefing's headline stat card reads "30–100%" as the "PRC fine band for FX evasion / illegal conversion routes". Under Decree 532 the ordinary ceiling is 30% or less (Arts. 39, 40, 45); 30%–100% applies only "where the circumstances are serious"; a border cash-carry breach is capped at 20% (Art. 42). The same page's detail table states this correctly, so the card overstates the base case. Not edited — briefing/** is inside the scope of open PR #41. Logged as a cross-scope correction request in COORDINATION.md. Already flagged as "penalties are not a fee" on audit-456e7542. Decree 532, Arts. 39/40/42/45
I02 The Form 8938 sentence "potential max $60,000" without saying what the cap applies to. §6038D(d)(2) caps only the post-notice additional penalty at $50,000; the $10,000 in (d)(1) is separate. $60,000 is the correct total, but the composition should be stated, and 31 U.S.C. 5321(a)(5) has no equivalent total cap for FBAR. Recorded here; wording note only. No value changed. 26 U.S.C. §6038D

Layout defect found and fixed in this pass

Rendering the published pages in Chromium at a 390 px viewport showed the repository's "start here" page forcing a horizontal scroll: briefing/index.html laid out at 865 px and briefing/costs.html at 597 px, against a 390 px viewport. The cause was layout only — stacked table cells used a flex line whose text could not shrink below its min-content width, and a method <select> kept its max-content width. The fix is confined to briefing/assets/style.css (stack the cells, allow word wrapping, cap the select at 100%): both pages now measure 390 px with no element extending past the viewport. No figure, label or source link was changed, and the same check across the other key pages at 390 px reports no overflow.

4 · Method, and what this record does not establish

Method

  • Each source was opened and read during this session; the rowwise locator is the article, item number or table cell that carries the value. Chinese-language instruments are quoted in the original where the wording matters, with a working translation marked as such.
  • Arithmetic was recomputed from confirmed inputs rather than copied: (500 + 120) ÷ 7.129 = 86.97; CNY 500 ÷ 0.1% = CNY 500,000 = $70,136 at 7.129.
  • Where the repository and the source disagreed, the source wins and the difference is listed above instead of being silently corrected in another session's file.

What this record does not establish

  • It is not a tax opinion or legal advice. No personal tax liability is computed, and no route is certified as available to any particular person.
  • It does not certify link health. The links were live when opened on 10 November 2026. A bank or regulator may change a page afterwards.
  • It does not adopt other dashboards' figures. Numbers were taken from the official sources, not from sibling pages, so agreement between this record and another page is evidence only if that page cites the same locator.
  • It is not a whole-repository audit. It covers the load-bearing claims behind the cost tables, route list and tax page. Earlier sessions' broader audits and their open flags remain published and are not superseded.
  • No bank was contacted, no funds were moved, and no return was filed.
