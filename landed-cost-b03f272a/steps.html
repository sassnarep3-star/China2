Terms used on this site
  • Yuan, CNY, RMB, ¥: the same currency. $ and USD: U.S. dollars.
  • SAFE: State Administration of Foreign Exchange, China's currency regulator. PBOC: People's Bank of China, the central bank.
  • Non-domestic individual (境外个人): the exchange rules' term for a foreign individual. Under the Regulations a foreigner who has lived in China continuously for a year counts as a domestic individual; see flag F07.
  • Exchange-rate margin: how much worse the bank's price is than the mid-point between its own buying and selling prices.
  • FBAR: the yearly U.S. report of foreign accounts (FinCEN Form 114). Form 8938: statement of foreign financial assets attached to the tax return. Form 3520: report of large foreign gifts or bequests.
  • Streamlined procedures: the IRS route for fixing non-willful failures to report foreign accounts and income.
