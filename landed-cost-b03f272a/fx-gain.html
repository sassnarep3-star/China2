RuleWhat it saysOfficial sources
What countsConverting yuan, including a yuan bank deposit, into dollars is a disposal of foreign currency.S30
How the gain is measuredDollars received, minus the dollar value of the same yuan at the rate when you received it. The IRS accepts any posted rate used consistently.S22S30F15
The $200 ruleOn a personal transaction no gain is recognised unless it exceeds $200. Above $200 the whole gain counts; $200 is a threshold, not an allowance.S30S31
Rate if it is a personal transactionReported as a capital gain. Held more than a year: 0%, 15% or 20% depending on taxable income (0% up to $48,350 single in 2025). Held a year or less: ordinary rates.S31S32
Rate if it is not personalA deposit held for investment or business falls under section 988: ordinary income, 10% to 37%. F16S30S33
Extra 3.8%The net investment income tax can apply to net gains above the income thresholds ($200,000 single, $250,000 joint). Not included in the columns. F18S34
LossesNo U.S. tax arises on a loss. Whether the loss can be deducted is not confirmed. F17S32S30
Two official 2025 averagesIRS: 7.129. Federal Reserve: 7.1875. This page uses the Federal Reserve series for every year. F15S22S23

Terms used on this site
  • Yuan, CNY, RMB, ¥: the same currency. $ and USD: U.S. dollars.
  • SAFE: State Administration of Foreign Exchange, China's currency regulator. PBOC: People's Bank of China, the central bank.
  • Non-domestic individual (境外个人): the exchange rules' term for a foreign individual. Under the Regulations a foreigner who has lived in China continuously for a year counts as a domestic individual; see flag F07.
  • Exchange-rate margin: how much worse the bank's price is than the mid-point between its own buying and selling prices.
  • FBAR: the yearly U.S. report of foreign accounts (FinCEN Form 114). Form 8938: statement of foreign financial assets attached to the tax return. Form 3520: report of large foreign gifts or bequests.
  • Streamlined procedures: the IRS route for fixing non-willful failures to report foreign accounts and income.
