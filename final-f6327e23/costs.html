Cost to convert and transfer, $10,000 to $400,000

Forty amounts in $10,000 steps. Costs are the sum of a published bank fee, the measured FX markup, and a published US receiving fee. Tax is never folded into these numbers.

Read this before using the numbers. These are estimates built from public price lists, not quotes. Three components: (1) a bank fee that is published (S14), (2) an FX markup measured from one dated official quotation (S16), (3) a US receiving fee published by one bank (S15). A commercial FX spread is not regulated and is not published as a rule; your bank's executable quote supersedes this page. No tax is included — see Tax & penalties for the conditional layer.

3 · Where the money goes at each scale

$10,000

Fixed costs dominate: the CNY 220 floor-plus-cable is about $32.91 regardless of size, so the all-in is $69.90 (0.70%).

$100,000

Spread overtakes the fee: $209.86 of spread vs $92.74 of bank fee; all-in $318.60 (0.32%).

$400,000

The fee is capped at CNY 500 + CNY 120, so it is nearly irrelevant; the spread is $839.44. All-in $948.18 (0.24%).

The one lever that matters

Negotiating the FX markup moves the answer far more than any fee. Each 0.10 percentage point of spread costs $100 per $100,000. That is why the brief's "conversion penalty" is mostly a pricing question, not a tax question.

4 · Assumptions, in full

Every departure from a published figure is listed here rather than buried in the table.

A01 The USD amount is the amount to be received in the US. The CNY converted is USD x the BOC spot selling rate on…

The USD amount is the amount to be received in the US. The CNY converted is USD x the BOC spot selling rate on 2026-11-10 (S16).

Why: Fees are charged in CNY; a rate is needed to express them in USD.
Effect if wrong: Moves the fixed-fee USD figure by <0.3% for a +/-2% rate change.
Status: model assumption

A02 FX markup on the CNY->USD leg is 0.2098%, the half-spread between BOC's published spot selling rate (6.6851) a…

FX markup on the CNY->USD leg is 0.2098%, the half-spread between BOC's published spot selling rate (6.6851) and the midpoint of its published bid/ask (6.6711).

Why: No regulator publishes a spread; this is arithmetic on one dated official quotation.
Effect if wrong: Directly scales with amount: USD 20.98 per USD 10,000. A bank quoting 1% would cost about 5x this line.
Status: measured snapshot, not a quote

A03 The HSBC China Advance/Premier telegraphic-transfer tariff applies (0.1%, min CNY 100, max CNY 500, plus CNY 1…

The HSBC China Advance/Premier telegraphic-transfer tariff applies (0.1%, min CNY 100, max CNY 500, plus CNY 120 cable).

Why: It is the only dated, published per-transfer fee schedule verified in this session.
Effect if wrong: Fixed CNY 220 at the low end; capped at CNY 620 above ~CNY 500,000 of principal.
Status: one bank only

A04 The receiving US bank charges USD 16 per incoming international wire (BofA Interest Checking, S15). No interme…

The receiving US bank charges USD 16 per incoming international wire (BofA Interest Checking, S15). No intermediary-bank deduction is included.

Why: Intermediary deductions are not published anywhere I could verify.
Effect if wrong: Understates the true deduction if a correspondent bank takes a fee.
Status: one bank, one account type; intermediary fees excluded

A05 No tax is included in the all-in cost columns.…

No tax is included in the all-in cost columns.

Why: A conversion tax depends on acquisition rates and filing history that are private facts.
Effect if wrong: See taxes.html for the conditional section 988 layer; it is shown separately, never merged.
Status: deliberate exclusion

A06 Statutory penalty ceilings (30% of amount) are shown only for unlawful routes, as the legal maximum, never as …

Statutory penalty ceilings (30% of amount) are shown only for unlawful routes, as the legal maximum, never as an expected cost.

Why: The statutes set a range up to 30% (30%-100% if serious); no authority publishes an average.
Effect if wrong: A ceiling, not a forecast.
Status: legal maximum
