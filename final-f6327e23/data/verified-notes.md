### US-T1 · 26 U.S.C. §988 (govinfo, US Code 2024 ed.)
URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm
- (c)(1)(C)(i): "In the case of any disposition of any nonfunctional currency— (I) such disposition shall be treated as a section 988 transaction".
- (c)(1)(C)(ii): nonfunctional currency "includes coin or currency, and nonfunctional currency denominated demand or time deposits or similar instruments issued by a bank".
- (e)(1): section does not apply to a §988 transaction by an individual that is a personal transaction.
- (e)(2): for personal transaction, "no gain shall be recognized ... by reason of changes in exchange rates after such currency was acquired ... before such disposition. The preceding sentence shall not apply if the gain which would otherwise be recognized on the transaction exceeds $200."
- (e)(3): "personal transaction" excludes transactions to the extent allocable expenses meet §162 (other than travel) or §212 (other than taxes).
=> Personal conversion of a personal CNY balance: exchange gain not recognised if <= USD200; above USD200 the whole gain is ordinary income.

### US-T3 · IRS FBAR page
URL: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/report-of-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts-fbar
- Must file if US person has financial interest/signature authority in >=1 foreign account AND aggregate value exceeded $10,000 at any time in the calendar year.
- Due April 15, automatic extension to Oct 15; file electronically via FinCEN BSA E-Filing (FinCEN Form 114); not filed with the return.
- Records: keep 5 years from FBAR due date.
