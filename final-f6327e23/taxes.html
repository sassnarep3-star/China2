Tax and penalties: what is actually taxed

A conversion is not the taxable event people fear. This page separates the currency trade, the income that funded it, the reporting duties, and the penalties for getting the route itself wrong.

US §988: the $200 line

The statute says that where an individual disposes of nonfunctional currency in a personal transaction, "no gain shall be recognized … by reason of changes in exchange rates", and then: "The preceding sentence shall not apply if the gain which would otherwise be recognized on the transaction exceeds $200." S08 A "personal transaction" excludes anything whose allocable expenses meet §162 (other than travel) or §212 (other than taxes) — so business use changes the analysis S08.

Technical caveat, flagged as F08: §988(e)(1) says the section does not apply to an individual's personal transactions, while (e)(2) sets the $200 rule. How the two sentences interact when the gain exceeds $200 is a question for a professional; this page reports the text, not a conclusion about your return.
