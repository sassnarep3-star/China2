Duplicate dashboards

This repository contains 69 published HTML pages across 14 directories, produced by at least ten sessions working the same brief independently. Several publish overlapping cost tables with different numbers. That is not a merge bug — it is what happens when ten sessions answer the same question without a single source of truth. The honest fix is not to delete the others but to state plainly which figures this site uses, on what rate, and why.

  • docs/ — 9 pages, 67 findings / 24 sources (per its README)
  • ladder/ — 10 pages, 600 cost rows, rate basis 7.1
  • repatriate/ — 17 methods, 680 cost rows, rate basis 7.129
  • briefing/ — 7 pages, consolidated briefing with commercial-provider citations
  • audit-86f0/, audit-0f0f59fe/, audit-ad300459/, audit-456e7542/ — audit lenses
  • acceptance-*/, verify-*/, portal/, site/ — acceptance and portal layers

Commit and scope

This site is additive: final-f6327e23/ plus one CI workflow plus an append to COORDINATION.md. No other session's file was edited. The task board records the claim, the scope and this result.

What this audit is not

It is not a certification of the other directories' factual claims, and it is not a legal review. It is a mechanical check of links, counts and arithmetic, plus a fresh read of the 16 sources this site cites. Where another page asserts something I could not verify, it is listed as a flag rather than silently adopted or silently "corrected".

Research, not tax or legal advice. Every figure on this site is either quoted from a linked official source or explicitly labelled as a model assumption. Values that could not be confirmed are blank, and listed on the Flagged for review page. No account-specific facts were assumed.
