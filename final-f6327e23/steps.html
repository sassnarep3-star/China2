Step by step: the playbook

The documented route, in order, with the document checklist and the variants for the cases where the money did not come from salary.

Before you start: three questions only you can answer

  1. What is the money? Salary and other current-account income? A property sale? A gift? An inheritance? The route changes, and choosing wrongly is the main way people get refused.
  2. Is it CNY, or already a foreign currency? If it is already USD, you may only need the remittance step S01.
  3. Have you ever held PRC household registration, and did you cancel it? If yes, the emigration-asset route may cover the whole balance S04. If you never held it, that route is not open to you.

I do not assume any of these. Each is a flag on the review page.

The primary playbook (salary / current-account income)

  1. Step 1. Ask the bank for its written list of required documents for a non-resident/foreign-individual current-account conversion of this amount.
  2. Step 2. Assemble the tax payment record for the same period as the funds (税务凭证/完税凭证) plus contracts and payslips.
  3. Step 3. Convert CNY→USD at the bank counter or in online banking; keep the 结汇/购汇 slip (水单).
  4. Step 4. Either remit directly (step 5) or park the USD in your own 外汇储蓄账户, then remit under Art. 65(1).
  5. Step 5. Send the wire with your name and address exactly as on the US account; choose SHA/OUR consciously (OUR + 'Pay Exact' costs CNY 200 at HSBC).
  6. Step 6. Keep the SWIFT confirmation, the Chinese conversion slip and the tax vouchers — they are the evidence for both FBAR/8938 and any later audit.

Variants

If the account already holds USD

Skip the conversion. A non-domestic individual remitting from a foreign-currency account needs valid ID under Art. 65(1), with no amount cap stated S01. Cost drops to the wire fee plus the receiving fee — about $108.74 at $400,000 in the model.

If you emigrated and cancelled PRC household registration

One application for the whole amount, then staged remittances: up to half, then up to half the remainder after a year, then the balance after two years; amounts up to RMB 200,000 can go in one approved transfer. Approval is by the local SAFE branch up to RMB 500,000 and by SAFE headquarters above that S04.

If the money is an inheritance

Same measure, different documents: proof of inheritance, the deceased's property documents and the tax certificate, with one application per deceased person S04.

If the amount is small and you want cash

Legal ceilings are low: up to USD 5,000 without a permit, USD 5,000–10,000 with a bank 携带证, and in principle not more than USD 10,000 at all S05. The US requires a FinCEN 105 declaration over USD 10,000, and the threshold aggregates across a family or group S13. Do not split cash between travellers.

Before you file your US return

  1. Reconstruct when you acquired the CNY and at what rate — this decides whether §988 gain exceeds $200 S08 S12.
  2. Check the FBAR threshold: aggregate foreign accounts over $10,000 at any point in the year S10.
  3. Check the Form 8938 threshold for your filing status and residence S11.
  4. Keep the Chinese conversion slips and tax vouchers for at least five years S10.
