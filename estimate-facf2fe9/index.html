Summary

  • No Chinese tax on converting or wiring was found. What stops a conversion is missing proof that tax on the income was paid. A foreign passport holder may buy dollars with lawful renminbi income on showing ID and source papers that include tax vouchers (SAFE FAQ 08). That tax is owed whether or not the money leaves. F20
  • The cheapest lawful route is a documented wire from the bank that holds the money. At the rates and fees the banks published on 10 November 2026, $400,000 costs $881.92 at Agricultural Bank (0.22%), $967.38 at Bank of China (0.24%), $1,173.07 at China Merchants Bank (0.29%) and $2,025.80 at HSBC China Premier (0.51%). Almost all of it is the exchange-rate margin, not the fee.
  • The larger number is on the US side. The yuan now trades near 6.6851 per dollar; the IRS yearly averages for 2023-2025 were 7.075-7.189. Of every $10,000 received today, about $551-$701 would be exchange gain on renminbi acquired in those years — $22,044-$28,037 on $400,000. How it is taxed depends on your facts (26 USC 988 · IRS rates). F21
  • Chinese income tax already paid is not lost. It can be credited against US tax on the same income, within limits (IRS Pub. 514). Interest on Chinese bank savings is currently exempt from Chinese income tax, but it is still US income (Cai Shui 132 · IRS manual).
  • Unlawful channels cost more than any tax. The fine is up to 30% of the amount, and 30%-100% in serious cases — up to $120,000 and $400,000 on $400,000 — with possible criminal liability (FX Regulations).
  • Report the account in the US. FBAR above $10,000 in total; Form 8938 above its thresholds; Form 3520 for a foreign bequest above $100,000. The FBAR penalty table runs to $16,536 per non-willful violation (IRS FBAR · 31 CFR 1010.821 · IRS Form 3520).
  • Capped routes do not scale. ATM withdrawals stop at ¥100,000 a year (27 years for $400,000); cash stops at $10,000 a trip with a bank permit (40 trips); the $50,000 yearly quota is only for holders of a Chinese Foreign Permanent Resident ID (8 years).

How the estimates are made

Cost in dollars = amount × (the bank's dollar selling rate ÷ reference mid − 1) + (commission + cable fee in renminbi) ÷ reference mid. The reference mid, 6.669994 yuan per dollar, is the average of the mid-points of the five mainland boards read; it is arithmetic, not a published rate. Charges by intermediary banks and by your US bank are not included because the sending banks do not publish them.

General information, not tax or legal advice. Rates and fees were read from the publishers' own pages on 10 November 2026 and will change. Nothing here is a quote from a bank. A value that could not be confirmed from an official page is left blank and listed under Flagged for Review.
