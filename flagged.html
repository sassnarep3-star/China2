What is unverified, unknown or irregular

Nothing on this page blocks the rest of the site; everything on it limits how far a number can be trusted. Each item says what is missing, why, and what would resolve it.

Blank means blank

Where an official source does not state a value, the cell is empty. It is never inferred from a commercial page, a forum, or another country's rule.

Personal facts

Five of these items depend on facts only you hold — whether tax was paid, what the money came from, whether earlier filings were made. They are unanswerable from public sources.

The two correction notes

§988 gain is ordinary, not capital

An earlier data file stated that a personal foreign-currency gain above $200 is reported as capital gain. Section 988(a)(1)(A) treats foreign currency gain or loss as ordinary, and §988(e)(2) is the personal-transaction exclusion, not a rate conversion. The earlier statement is wrong on its face and should not be relied on.

S06

The 35% trust penalty is not a foreign-gift penalty

An earlier portal attributed "the greater of $10,000 or 35%" to Form 3520 in general. That is the §6677 penalty for foreign trusts. Receipt of a foreign gift is reported in Part IV and penalised under §6039F(c), which is a different provision with a different measure. Gift reporting thresholds are verified; the gift penalty percentage is not stated here (F13).
