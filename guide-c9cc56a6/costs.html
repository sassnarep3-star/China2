What each route costs, $10,000 to $400,000

Published bank fees are exact and linked. The FX margin is an assumption and is labelled as one on every row. Unlawful routes are priced as statutory fine ranges, not fees.

Read this before the tables. Two different things are shown in every row:

  1. Published bank fee — an exact number from a published tariff (G08). This is verifiable and it is small.
  2. FX margin — the gap between the rate the bank deals on and the market. No bank publishes this, so it is shown as a labelled band (0.30%-1.50% for wires), not as a fact. See flag F01.

The "all-in estimate" adds the two. Treat it as an order of magnitude with a known bottom edge, not a quote.
