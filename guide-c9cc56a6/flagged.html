F01 The FX conversion margin is not published - every all-in cost cell carries an assumption

unverified

No bank publishes the customer CNY/USD bid and offer it will actually deal on, so the FX margin cannot be sourced. The cost ladder models a band of 0.30%-1.50% for telegraphic routes, 0.80%-3.00% for cash, 0.00%-3.00% for card routes and 0.20%-1.00% for the offshore route. Those bands are labelled assumptions on every table and are NOT official figures. The published fee column is separate and exact.

To resolve: Replace the band with the bank's own executable quote before acting. The published fee is the small part of the all-in cost.

F02 The repo's existing ladder uses a superseded HSBC tariff

irregularity

ladder/data/sources.json (S06) cites the tariff effective 1 January 2021, and ladder/data/costs.csv therefore charges USD 7.00 for a Global Transfer (route R02). The tariff effective 31 October 2026 (G08, item 2.2.6.1) states: free for Private Banking and Premier, and for Advance a CNY 145 indication charge whose actual charge is USD 20 per transaction. This guide uses the 2026 figures. The ladder files were not edited - they are outside this session's scope.

To resolve: Ladder owner should refresh S06 to the 31 October 2026 tariff.

unverified

Bank of China, ICBC, China Construction Bank and Bank of Communications tariffs were not retrieved this session. The fixed-fee column is therefore an HSBC China tariff, and other banks will publish different numbers - some price a wire at a flat fee, some at 0.1% uncapped. G08 · HSBC Bank

F04 Section 988 character once the USD 200 line is crossed: capital or ordinary?

needs-review

26 U.S.C. 988(e)(1)-(3) is verified verbatim. What it does not state is the character of the recognised gain above USD 200. One reading applies section 1001 and treats it as gain on a capital asset; another reads section 988(a)(1)(A) as still governing. IRS Publication 525 is widely quoted as saying such a gain is reported as a capital gain, but this session retrieved only the publication's table of contents and could not verify the sentence, so no character is chosen anywhere on this site and no cell depends on one.

To resolve: Ask a preparer, or read Publication 525's foreign-currency paragraph and note 26 CFR 1.988-1(a)(8).

F06 The emigration route requires proof that your PRC household registration is cancelled

needs-review

The staging rules for C07 and C08 were re-verified directly on 2026-11-10 (G07, Arts. 6, 8, 10, 11): one application for the whole amount, no more than half first, no more than half of the remainder after a year, the balance after two years, and amounts up to RMB 200,000 in one go after approval. What is not resolved is eligibility: Art. 8(4) requires the police-issued cancellation of PRC household registration plus a certificate of overseas residence issued or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate, and Art. 8(6) requires a tax certificate. A US citizen who has never held PRC household registration, or who has not cancelled it, cannot assemble that file. This site therefore describes C07 as conditional rather than recommending it.

To resolve: Confirm with the local SAFE branch whether you can satisfy Art. 8(4) before relying on this route.

F07 Who the USD 50,000 quota actually binds

needs-review

The quota figure itself is verified (G02). Its scope rests on an inherited source (G04 Art. 2), which applies the annual aggregate to individuals' FX sales and to domestic individuals' FX purchases. A foreign national resident in China for one year or more is a 'domestic individual' under Decree 532 Art. 52 - so a long-staying US citizen may be inside the quota regime while a new arrival is not. The distinction between quota purchases and documented income conversion is what makes C01 uncapped and C21 capped.

To resolve: Confirm your own classification with the bank before choosing C01 or C21.

F08 PRC tax on the source income is not computed anywhere on this site

unverified

The tax depends on which of the nine categories the income falls into, what was withheld in China, what deductions apply and which years are open. No figure is filled. G20 · US Customs and Border Protection

F12 The 7.10 CNY/USD rate is a conversion convenience, not a quote

assumption

Bank tariffs are denominated in CNY. Only the CNY 100 / 500 / 120 amounts are affected. At USD 400,000 the whole swing across a 6.80-7.40 band is under USD 15 on a USD 87 fee. The ladder tiers are dollar amounts, so no exchange-rate forecast is embedded in any table.

To resolve: None - but do not read 7.10 as a market view.
