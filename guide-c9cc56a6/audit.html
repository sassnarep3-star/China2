What verify.py enforces on every build

  1. Every source id used by a row, a card or a table exists in data/sources.json and has an https URL.
  2. Every local link and every in-page anchor resolves to a file or an id that exists.
  3. Every cost row carries a non-empty data-src attribute - a row without a source link fails the build.
  4. A blank cost cell must carry a data-flag attribute, and the flag id must exist on the Flagged page. Blank without a flag fails.
  5. The published bank fee is recomputed from the 2026 tariff formula and compared with the rendered value at all 40 tiers.
  6. Tier coverage: every costed route must have exactly 40 rows, from USD 10,000 to USD 400,000 in USD 10,000 steps.
  7. The section 988 gain table is recomputed from the IRS yearly averages and compared cell by cell.
  8. No rendered page may contain 'None', 'nan' or an unformatted float.
  9. The count of channels, lawful routes and flagged items is asserted so a silent deletion is caught.
