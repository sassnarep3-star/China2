Unpublished commercial values are blank, never estimated. * = modelled spread band, unverified.

Read first: there is no CNY→USD conversion tax in the PRC or the US. The real costs are (1) bank spread, (2) published tariff fees, (3) penalties for unlawful methods, (4) US reporting-penalty exposure. The single largest component — the spread — is not published; get the bank's executable quote before acting. Fee floors below assume one wire (or one tranche per calendar year for M02).
