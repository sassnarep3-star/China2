China money: the least-tax, lawful way to move CNY → USD

As a U.S. citizen: there is no such thing as a CNY→USD “conversion tax”. What people call the conversion penalty is a stack of four separate things — PRC income tax on the source of the balance, the bank's spread and wire fees, PRC fines for unlawful routes, and U.S. reporting penalties. This page is the executive summary; every claim links its official source.

2 · The least-tax path, in decision order

  1. Prove the source of the balance (contract/invoices + bank statements + tax vouchers). Nothing moves lawfully without this.
  2. Settle any unpaid PRC tax on that income — this is the step that stalls large balances.
  3. Move it via M01 (bank conversion + same-name SWIFT wire — published fee caps at $86.97 regardless of size) or M03 (Global Transfer, $0 published fee for PB/Premier) — see cost tables.
  4. US side: FBAR + Form 8938 on time; Form 1116 for the PRC tax; if past years were missed, use Streamlined procedures before the IRS finds you.
  5. Never use M14–M17 (crypto/smurfing/underground banks/false docs): fines 30–100% + criminal exposure.
Cheapest published-cost path for a documented after-tax balance: one wire from China → own US account. At $400,000: published fees $86.97 (HSBC Premier/Advance) or $0 (Private Banking individual / Global Transfer PB-Premier), plus the unpublished spread (model 0.30–1.50% = $1,200–$6,000, unverified). At $10,000: same routes, fee $30.86, spread $30–$150. The fee is trivial; the spread and the paperwork are the game.

3 · The bank-first checklist (take this to your branch)

  1. Ask for the refusal/approval in writing; note the tax type, taxpayer, period, base, amount and legal basis — distinguish a tax payment from a tax certificate or a bank fee.
  2. Ask which FX category applies to you (documented foreign-individual income vs annual quota vs settlement account) and what evidence satisfies it (contract, invoices, tax vouchers, permits).
  3. Get the executable all-in quote: applied CNY→USD rate, fee and cable, Pay Exact surcharge, intermediary/receiving charges, and the delivery date.
  4. Confirm your PRC tax settlement for the years the income arose (annual 汇算清缴) and get the 完税证明.
  5. US filings: FBAR by Apr 15 (auto-extension to Oct 15), Form 8938 with the return, Form 1116 for the credit.
  6. Past years unreported? Ask a US tax professional about Streamlined Filing Compliance Procedures now, not after an IRS letter.
  7. Carrying > $10,000 cash into the US? File FinCEN 105. China: USD 5,000/trip no permit; USD 5,000–10,000 with bank permit.
