The one gap that matters most

The retail CNY/USD spread — the margin a bank takes between the rate it quotes and the rate it gives you — is the largest real cost of converting, and no reviewed bank publishes it. HSBC's tariff lists transaction fees only. Because this site will not estimate, the spread is blank in every table and every total is a floor. A user comparing routes on price alone is comparing the smallest component of the cost.

Irregularity
I-01
 The retail CNY/USD spread is not published by any bank reviewed.
HSBC's published tariff (C01) lists transaction fees but no exchange-rate markup, and no other reviewed source publishes an executable customer rate. Consequence: every dollar total on this site is a floor. Deliberately left blank rather than estimated.
 C01 does not block
Irregularity
I-02
 SAFE's Tianjin 2024 guidance and the 2006 Decree appear to differ on whether a 境外个人 gets an annual purchase quota.
Decree [2006] No.3 (P01) imposes the annual total on 境内个人, while SAFE Tianjin's 2024 Q&A (P08) states 境外个人 have a settlement quota but no purchase quota EXCEPT holders of a Foreign Permanent Resident ID. Resolution used here: quote P08 for the 境外个人 case and flag that bank practice governs. Not resolved by this document — flagged for the user to confirm with their bank.
