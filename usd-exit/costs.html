How to read these numbers

ColumnWhat it isWhat it is not
FeeThe published bank tariff for the wires the route requiresNot the spread, not tax, not professional fees
YearsHow long the route takes at its published ceilingNot a promise — approval times are discretionary
TransfersHow many separate wires are neededNot a suggestion to split; each is lawful and disclosed
RecommendedThe route that avoids the worst trade-off at that sizeNot advice — it ignores your tax facts, which dominate

Roadmap by amount

$10k – $50k

One wire under the annual quota. Published bank fee US$32.74 – US$67.86. No approval, no irreversible step. P02 C01

$60k – $150k

Two to three calendar years at the quota, or the documented above-quota route, or the emigration transfer. This is the band where the trade-off first bites. P02 P06

$160k – $400k

Four to eight calendar years at the quota. Past roughly $100,000 the emigration route becomes both cheaper and faster — at the price of cancelling 户口, which is irreversible. P06 P11
