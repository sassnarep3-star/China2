Live verification · 11 November 2026

This page is the dated record of what was re-checked live on 2026-11-11, what the full test-suite re-run showed, and what the landing-page integration changed. It is not a new cost model, a legal opinion, or a personal tax determination. Canonical analysis lives in the executive briefing, the re-verification dashboard and the cost ladder; this page links to them rather than duplicating them.

Most important, first

  1. There is no separate PRC tax merely for exchanging CNY→USD in the instruments reviewed — but that is not a conclusion that nothing is taxable. The balance’s source, any underpaid PRC tax on the underlying income, U.S. exchange gain/loss (26 U.S.C. § 988, ordinary income/loss), and U.S. reporting (FBAR, Form 8938, Form 3520) are separate questions that no published source can settle without your facts.
  2. The lawful route is documentation-first. SAFE guidance re-read today requires identity plus source-of-funds evidence (including tax documents) for current-account conversions of overseas individuals’ lawful RMB income: SAFE Tianjin, 2026-07-13, item 08.
  3. “Conversion penalty” ≈ five layers, none of which is a single official number: (a) PRC tax on the income that created the balance; (b) tax-certificate/recordal gate before the bank wires; (c) bank FX spread — unpublished by any bank reviewed (stays blank, flagged); (d) published wire/transfer fees (HSBC tariff effective 2026-10-31: 0.1% min CNY 100 / max CNY 500 + cable for Premier/Advance; Private Banking free); (e) U.S. reporting exposure (FBAR non-willful max $16,536 / willful max $165,353 — 31 CFR 1010.821, table live as of 11/09/2026).
  4. Every cost ladder in this repository is an estimate built on published components. The unpublished spread and any intermediary/receiver deductions are left blank or explicitly modelled as assumptions — never presented as an executable quote. Do not act on a total without a current written quote from the bank.
  5. All $10,000–$400,000 tables are on the linked pages in this repository (see the coverage map below). Unknown cells are blank by design; blank means unverified, not zero.

6 · Method & limitations

  • Retrieval date for §2 rows is 2026-11-11 (UTC). Retrieval success is recorded as shown; nothing was copied from memory without a live read on this date. Where the live read did not cover a value, the value is labelled as a prior-pass read or omitted.
  • This pass did not re-derive the arithmetic of the 920/680/600-row ladders; it re-ran the suites that do (they exit 0). Structural checks are not legal verification.
  • No personal account facts were used; no bank or authority was contacted; nothing was filed or transferred. This repository is public research. The exact cost and tax position for a specific balance requires the bank’s written quote and a qualified China/U.S. adviser.

Bottom line: start with source-of-funds and tax history; get the bank’s written reason if it refuses; compare current written quotes across eligible routes; settle U.S. reporting separately. Everything in this repository — and on this page — is orientation, not execution.
