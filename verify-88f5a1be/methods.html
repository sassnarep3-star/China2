Methods & step-by-step — 17 routes

13 lawful-conditional routes and 4 rejected-unlawful routes (documented because the brief asks for as many ways as possible — they are not recommendations). Pros/cons/steps summarize the cited sources; analysis is labeled.

M01
lawful-conditional		Bank SWIFT wire of documented after-tax China income (foreign individual)
US citizen with lawful current-account RMB income earned in China + tax vouchers. The key route for money stuck in a China account.		✓ No annual numeric cap stated for documented lawful income (S02/S03); the $50k quota is a different channel.
✓ Bank handles conversion + remittance in one documented process.
✓ Published fee caps at CNY 620/wire (CNY 500 + CNY 120) regardless of size (S20) — large wires are cheap in fixed-fee terms.
✓ Current transfers not restricted in principle (S05 Art. 5).		✗ Requires ID + source-of-funds materials including tax vouchers (S02); undocumented balances cannot use it.
✗ Unpaid PRC tax on source income must be resolved first — income tax, not conversion tax (S08).
✗ FX spread + correspondent charges unpublished (F02) — all-in cost cannot be verified in advance.
✗ Bank authenticity review; bank may request more documents or split the wire (F09).		1. Assemble passport/ID, employment contract or income proof, and PRC tax records covering the amount.
2. If tax on source income is unpaid, resolve it first and obtain tax records (S08).
3. At your China bank, apply to purchase USD with the documented RMB income (S02).
4. Instruct outward SWIFT remittance to your own US account (SWIFT/BIC, account, address).
5. Pay published tariff (0.1% min CNY 100 max CNY 500 + CNY 120 cable; optional Pay Exact CNY 200) (S20).
6. Keep receipts + tax records for US reporting (FBAR/8938 if thresholds met) (S11/S15).		S01;S02;S20
M02
lawful-conditional		Bank wire within the USD 50,000/year facilitation quota (PRC ID or Foreign Permanent Resident ID holders)
PRC domestic individuals; foreign individuals ONLY with PRC Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card (S03). Most US citizens use M01 instead.		✓ ID-only formalities within quota — no transaction documents (S01 Art. 2).
✓ Same bank infrastructure as M01; published tariff applies.
✓ Spreading your OWN funds over calendar years is lawful (one person, one quota).		✗ USD 50,000/person/year (S01 Art. 2): USD 400,000 needs 8 calendar years on this route alone.
✗ Not available to most foreign passport holders (S03).
✗ Using other people's quotas for one beneficiary is prohibited splitting (S04 Art. 7; see M15).
✗ Spread still applies and is unpublished (F02).		1. Confirm you hold a PRC ID or Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card (S03).
2. Purchase USD within remaining annual quota with ID (S01 Art. 2).
3. Remit abroad: domestic <=USD 50,000/day from FX savings account with ID; above with authenticity documents (S01 Art. 14).
4. Repeat in later calendar years for amounts above the quota, or use document-based routes for the excess.
5. Keep receipts; comply with US reporting (S11/S15).		S01;S03;S20
M03
lawful-conditional		HSBC Global Transfer to your own overseas HSBC account
Customers with HSBC China + own-name HSBC accounts in the destination country. CNY→USD conversion still needs a lawful basis (M01/M02).		✓ Published fee: Free for Private Banking and Premier; Advance USD 20/transaction (S20 2.2.6.1).
✓ Own-name transfer; simple audit trail.
✓ No correspondent-fee uncertainty for the HSBC-internal leg (Note 7 still excludes other institutions/government charges).		✗ Requires HSBC accounts in both countries; Premier eligibility (e.g., TRB >=CNY 500,000) applies (S20 Note 3).
✗ Does not bypass SAFE conversion rules — purchase still needs quota or documents (S01/S02).
✗ FX spread unpublished (F02); per-transaction limits not in tariff (F02).		1. Open/confirm own-name HSBC accounts in China and the US (eligibility per S20).
2. Convert CNY to USD inside HSBC China on a lawful basis (M01 documents or M02 quota).
3. Send via Global Transfer to your own US HSBC account (S20 2.2.6.1).
4. Keep records; US reporting applies (S11/S15).		S20;S01;S02
M04
lawful-conditional		Wise (from-CNY) to your own foreign account
Non-mainland-Chinese individuals with PRC tax records (tax paid within last 3 years); personal accounts; own-account transfers only.		✓ Provider states overall limit = documented after-China-tax salary, does not expire — no $50k/year staging within that limit.
✓ Single transfer up to CNY 300,000 via bank-app pay-in.
✓ Regulated partner pay-in flow; transparent in-app quote before sending.		✗ Only your own accounts as recipient; no third-party payments.
✗ Requires recent tax records; undocumented balances do not qualify.
✗ Fees shown only at setup — not statically published, so no verified cost table is possible (F03).
✗ Mainland Chinese nationals cannot use from-CNY sending.		1. Create a Wise personal account; complete first-time verification.
2. Upload PRC tax records; Wise assigns an overall limit from them.
3. Set up CNY→USD transfer to your own US account; review the in-app fee and rate quote.
4. Pay from your own Chinese bank app to the regulated partner account (<=CNY 300,000/transfer).
5. Wise converts and pays out; keep records for US reporting.		S02 (provider terms not re-fetched this session — see F03)
M05
lawful-conditional		Western Union from China (partner-bank online or in-person, cash pickup abroad)
Residents of China sending via WU partner banks. Passport accepted; bank account may be required.		✓ Cash pickup abroad in minutes; large location network.
✓ Online sending via several partner-bank apps after registration.
✓ US is on PSBC's whitelist.		✗ Transfers to recipient bank accounts were 'temporarily unavailable' at retrieval — cash pickup only (F03).
✗ Fees and per-transfer limits not statically published (F03).
✗ Agents are banks subject to local FX rules — SAFE conversion basis still applies.
✗ Cash pickup >USD 10,000 then deposited in the US may trigger bank reporting; carrying cash across the US border needs FinCEN 105 (S18).		1. Register a WU profile with a partner bank (online) or visit a partner-bank branch with passport.
2. Provide receiver details and purpose; pay from debit card/account plus quoted fee.
3. Receive MTCN; share only with your intended receiver.
4. Receiver picks up cash with ID at a WU location.
5. Keep records; US reporting applies to the underlying account/income (S11/S15).		S01 (provider terms not re-fetched this session — see F03)
M06
lawful-conditional		Overseas ATM cash withdrawals with a China-issued bank card
Holders of domestic (mainland-issued) bank cards withdrawing cash abroad. Parallel cash channel, separate from the $50k quota.		✓ No tax documents needed at the ATM; card network converts automatically.
✓ Useful for small amounts; RMB 10,000/card/day ceiling (S06).		✗ Hard cap RMB 100,000/person/calendar year across ALL cards (~USD 14,027 at reference rate) (S06).
✗ Exceeding it suspends overseas withdrawals current AND following year (S06).
✗ ATM operator + issuer fees and card-network FX margin unpublished (F02).
✗ USD 400,000 would take ~29 calendar years — impractical (computed).
✗ Borrowing others' cards to evade is prohibited (S06).		1. Use your own mainland-issued card at an overseas ATM.
2. Track cumulative withdrawals across all cards: <=RMB 100,000/year, <=RMB 10,000/card/day (S06).
3. Deposit withdrawn cash into your US account; keep records.
4. For physical carry into the US >USD 10,000 at one time, file FinCEN 105 (S18).		S06;S18
M07
lawful-conditional		Buy USD cash in China and carry it out (within customs permit rules)
Travelers exiting China. <=USD 5,000 no permit; USD 5,000-10,000 with bank permit; >USD 10,000 in principle not allowed (S07).		✓ No permit needed <=USD 5,000/trip (S07).
✓ HSBC counter banknote withdrawal published Free (S20 2.2.2.1).
✓ US entry with <=USD 10,000 needs no FinCEN 105; above is legal WITH reporting (S18).		✗ USD 400,000 = 80 permit-free trips (computed) — travel costs and risk dominate; repeat-traveler rules cut limits to USD 500/1,000 (S07 Art. 6).
✗ Notes buy/sell board rates apply (spread unpublished, F02); >=USD 5,000 withdrawal needs 3 working days' notice at HSBC (S20).
✗ Unlawful carrying: warning + fine up to 20% (S05 Art. 42); US non-declaration risks forfeiture (S18).		1. Purchase USD cash on a lawful basis (M01/M02) at the bank counter.
2. Carry out within per-trip limit; obtain bank carrying permit for USD 5,000-10,000 (S07).
3. Enter the US; file FinCEN 105 if carrying >USD 10,000 (S18).
4. Deposit into your US account; keep records.		S07;S20;S18

✗ Offshore RMB conversion rates/fees unpublished (F02).
✗ Excess RMB is returned by customs; penalties for non-declaration; unlawful carrying fine up to 20% (S05 Art. 42).		1. Withdraw <=CNY 20,000 cash.
2. Exit China within the limit; declare if required.
3. Convert RMB to USD at a bank/exchange abroad (offshore rate).

M09
lawful-conditional		Reconvert unused RMB previously exchanged from foreign currency
Foreign individuals holding RMB previously converted FROM foreign currency and left unused, with the original exchange memo (24-month validity) (S01 Art. 13(2)).		✓ With original memo: reconversion up to memo amount, no tax documents (S01).
✓ ID-only small reconversions: <=USD 500/day onshore, <=USD 1,000/day airside before departure (S01).		✗ ONLY for previously exchanged unused RMB — not a general route for salary/savings (S01).
✗ Memo expires 24 months after original exchange (S01).
✗ USD 400,000 via ID-only USD 500/day = 800 bank days (computed) — impractical; memo route limited to original amount.		1. Locate the original exchange memo from when you converted USD→CNY.
2. Within 24 months, present ID + memo at the bank; reconvert up to the unused memo amount (S01).
3. Without a memo, only ID-only daily small-amount reconversion applies (<=USD 500 onshore / <=USD 1,000 airside).
4. Remit the reconvened FX abroad with ID (S01 Art. 15).		S01
M10
lawful-conditional		Emigrant personal asset transfer (SAFE-approved capital transfer)
Individuals who EMIGRATED FROM Mainland China (assets obtained before immigration permission) — typically former PRC residents, not US-born citizens (S09).		✓ SAFE approval allows lump-sum remittance of the approved amount — no $50k/year staging (per 2014 amendment, NOT re-fetched — F04).
✓ Covers assets beyond salary (property, savings) if lawful, owned and undisputed (S09).		✗ Eligibility is narrow: emigrants from Mainland China with hukou cancellation/immigration proofs (S09); most US citizens born abroad do not qualify.
✗ SAFE application at original registered residence; documentary burden; approval amount limits the transfer (S09).
✗ Tax clearance on underlying assets required; emigration triggers a PRC tax return.
✗ No published fee schedule (F02).		1. Confirm emigrant status (immigration permission + PRC hukou cancellation proofs) (S09).
2. Settle PRC tax on the assets; emigration tax return.
3. Apply to SAFE at the original registered-residence branch with written application, ID, asset-source proofs (S09).
4. After approval, bank purchases FX and remits within the approved limit.
5. US reporting: FBAR/8938 for the China account; the transfer itself is not US-taxable income (moving own funds).		S09;S01

✓ No $50k quota staging for the approved inheritance.		✗ Requires inheritance legal documents/notarization and tax clearance; SAFE approval at decedent's original registered residence (S09).
✗ Only inherited assets — not a route for your own savings.
✗ Process fees/timeline unpublished (F02).		1. Obtain inheritance legal documents or notarial deeds.
2. Settle applicable PRC taxes on the estate/assets.

4. Bank remits the approved amount lump sum or in batches.

✗ Only if you actually own sellable property — not applicable to a plain bank balance.		1. Complete the property sale; pay PRC IIT on the gain and transaction taxes (S08).
2. Obtain tax vouchers and sale documents.
3. At the bank, convert the after-tax RMB proceeds to USD and remit with ID + documents.
4. US: report the sale and any gain on your US return; FTC for PRC tax may apply subject to limits (S17); FBAR/8938 while funds are in China.		S08;S17
