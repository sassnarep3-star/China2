Targeted official-source review, 10 November 2026. Research only, not personal tax or legal advice. The balance's origin, tax history, account status, exchange rate and bank's reasons are unknown. No funds were moved, bank contacted, or return filed.

Bottom line: If this is qualifying lawful China-source current-account income, investigate conversion and a bank transfer with the required source and tax documents. Eligibility and the least expensive route cannot be established without the bank's decision and an executable quote. Documented conversionSAFE describes foreign individuals purchasing foreign currency using lawful current-account RMB income earned in China with identification and transaction-amount evidence including tax vouchers. This does not establish eligibility of an unspecified balance, especially capital assets.SAFE Tianjin FAQ
QuotaThe $50,000 annual facilitation quota is not a universal ban on larger documented transactions. The applicable classification and quota eligibility need individual review; nationality alone does not decide the result.SAFE detailed rules, articles 2 and 13 · SAFE eligibility FAQ
Currency gain and taxDo not assume converting an account has no U.S. tax effect. §988 contains rules on currency gains and a limited exclusion for certain personal transactions with gain no greater than $200. Applicability, basis, character and tax are unverified for this account.26 U.S.C. §988(c), (e) · IRS currency guidance
Example transfer feeHSBC China's published tariff lists 0.1%, minimum CNY 100 and maximum CNY 500, plus CNY 120 cable for certain Premier/Advance outward telegraphic transfers. At the IRS 2025 average of CNY 7.129 per USD, CNY 620 is about $86.97. This is an illustration, not the actual fee in dollars, a conversion price or an all-in cost; eligibility and third-party charges matter.HSBC China tariff, 2.2.4.1–2.2.4.2 and notes · IRS 2025 yearly average
