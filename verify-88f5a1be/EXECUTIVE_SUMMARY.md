## 1. The core finding: there is no "conversion tax"

Neither country taxes the act of converting CNY to USD. SAFE imposes **quotas and authenticity review** ([SAFE Detailed Rules](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0430/2464.html), [PBOC Decree 2006 No. 3](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/english/130733/2025080815071447034/index.html)); the FE Regulations do not restrict current transfers ([Decree 532 Art. 5](https://www.safe.gov.cn/beijing/2018/1221/1010.html)). What is feared as a "conversion penalty" decomposes into seven layers:

| Layer | What it actually is | Verified anchor |
|---|---|---|
| 1. PRC conversion tax | **None exists.** Quota + review, not tax | [SAFE rules](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0430/2464.html) |
| 2. PRC source-income tax | IIT owed when income arose: 3–45% comprehensive / 5–35% business / 20% flat. If unpaid, banks gate conversion | [IIT Law](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html) |
| 3. FX spread | Bank's real conversion margin — **not published; blank + flagged (F02)** | [HSBC tariff](https://www.hsbc.com.cn/content/dam/hsbc/cn/docs/document-download/tariff-of-Accounts-services-for-personal-customers-v2.pdf) |
| 4. Wire fees | Published: HSBC 0.1% (min ¥100 / max ¥500) + ¥120 cable ≈ **$30.86–$86.97 per wire** | [HSBC tariff 2.2.4.1–2](https://www.hsbc.com.cn/content/dam/hsbc/cn/docs/document-download/tariff-of-Accounts-services-for-personal-customers-v2.pdf) |
| 5. US tax on conversion | **$0** unless personal gain > **$200**; above that, capital gain | [26 USC 988(e)(2)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm) |
| 6. US reporting penalties | FBAR (> $10k aggregate), Form 8938, FinCEN 105 (> $10k cash). Avoidable by filing | [FBAR](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/report-of-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts-fbar), [31 CFR 1010.821](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-H/section-1010.821) |
| 7. PRC fines (unlawful routes) | Up to 30%; serious **30–100%** + criminal liability | [Decree 532 Arts. 39–45](https://www.safe.gov.cn/beijing/2018/1221/1010.html) |

Moving your own already-taxed money is not new US income. FBAR non-willful max is **$16,536/report** and willful is the greater of **$165,353 or 50%** of balance (assessed on/after 2025-01-17); Form 8938 max is **$60,000/form**.

## 2. Best route for a stuck balance

**Documented after-tax China income → bank conversion + SWIFT wire (M01).** SAFE lets foreign individuals convert lawful current-account RMB income with ID + income/tax vouchers, **with no annual dollar cap stated** ([SAFE 2026 FAQ](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html)). The famous **$50,000/year quota is a different channel** — and per SAFE, foreign passport holders get it only with a PRC Permanent Resident ID Card ([SAFE case 9](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0621/2529.html)). Practical sequence: assemble tax records → resolve any unpaid IIT → convert + wire at your bank → keep receipts → file FBAR/8938 if thresholds met.

Conditional alternatives: HSBC Global Transfer to your own overseas HSBC (**free** Premier/PB, $20 Advance) ([tariff 2.2.6.1](https://www.hsbc.com.cn/content/dam/hsbc/cn/docs/document-download/tariff-of-Accounts-services-for-personal-customers-v2.pdf)); emigrant/inheritance SAFE-approved transfers (narrow eligibility) ([PBOC 2004 rules](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/english/130721/2025080815062856255/index.html)).

**Slow/cash channels are capped, computed:** overseas ATM ≈ **$14,027/year** ([RMB 100k/yr cap](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2017/1230/1397.html)); cash carry **$5,000/trip** permit-free ([Hui Fa 2003-102](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2004/content_63085.htm)). $400k would take 29 years of ATM withdrawals or 80 cash trips — the 40-row tables make this visible.

**Do not use:** crypto bridges (illegal financial activity, [Yinfa 2026-42](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html)), quota splitting ([PBOC Art. 7](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/english/130733/2025080815071447034/index.html)), underground banks, fake contracts — fines up to 30% of the amount, serious cases **30–100%**, plus confiscation and criminal liability ([Decree 532 Arts. 39–45](https://www.safe.gov.cn/beijing/2018/1221/1010.html)).
