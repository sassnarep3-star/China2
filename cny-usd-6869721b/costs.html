What it costs, $10,000 to $400,000

Converting and wiring through the two banks priced in full costs between 0.22% and 0.53% of the amount. Other methods cost time rather than money: years of ATM withdrawals or dozens of trips.

How each number is made

  • Spread = dollars sent × (selling rate − midpoint) ÷ midpoint, using the bank's own buy and sell rates. R2 R4
  • Bank fee = 0.1% of the yuan paid, held between the tariff's minimum and maximum, plus the cable fee, turned into dollars at the selling rate. F1 F3
  • Cost = spread + bank fee. It leaves out intermediary-bank deductions and your US bank's fee, which are shown separately.
  • Rates are a snapshot from 10 November 2026, about 17:40 to 17:51 Beijing time. The central parity that day was 671.67; banks sold dollars below it. R1
