## The answer

- **Cheapest lawful route found:** convert and wire through a bank against documents. At the rates and tariffs published that day, $400,000 costs **$881.47 (0.22%)** at Agricultural Bank of China or **$900.77 (0.23%)** at Bank of China; $10,000 costs $42.96 or $53.42. [R4](https://ewealth.abchina.com/ForeignExchange/ListPrice/) [F3](https://www.abchina.com/cn/PersonalServices/Forex/) [R2](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/enindex_1619.html) [F1](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202503/P020250331641149290400.pdf)
- **The gate is papers, not price.** A foreign-passport holder has no USD 50,000 yearly quota; the bank sells dollars against passport plus source-of-funds documents including tax vouchers. [L1](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html) [L3](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0621/2529.html)
- **The route follows the source of the money:** pay earned in China, pre-emigration savings (staged over two years above CNY 200,000), inheritance, housing sale, or unused exchanged yuan. [L1](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html) [L8](http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/2004/1116/4133.html) [L9](http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20250915/16a1dd8e54f94d26969b0a9b781106a8.pdf) [L2](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0917/3134.html)
- **China taxes the income behind the money, not the transfer.** Pay 3%-45%, property transfer 20%, savings interest not levied, no inheritance tax. [T1](https://shenzhen.chinatax.gov.cn/sztax/zcwj/zxwj/201901/90e47cf47dfd4294b44ee67512c40c24.shtml) [T2](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5203286/content.html) [T3](http://szs.mof.gov.cn/jytafwgk_8391/2017jytafwgk/2017zxwytafwgk/201710/t20171017_2726094.htm)
- **The largest tax exposure found is in the US.** Exchange-rate gain above $200 is capital gain. On $400,000 the gain is **$28,037** if the yuan dates from 2024 and $24,907 if from 2025. [A2](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p525) [R6](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/yearly-average-currency-exchange-rates)
- **Reporting penalties are larger than any fee:** FBAR up to $16,536 non-willful and $165,353 willful; Form 8938 $10,000 plus up to $50,000; Form 3520 5% a month up to 25%. [A4](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-H/section-1010.821) [A5](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/corporations/summary-of-fatca-reporting-for-us-taxpayers) [A6](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i3520.pdf)
- **Shortcuts are unlawful:** borrowed quotas, underground banks, crypto and false papers carry fines up to 30%, or 30% to 100% in serious cases. [L6](http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/2008/0806/5321.html) [L7](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2015/1231/5442.html) [L15](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html)
