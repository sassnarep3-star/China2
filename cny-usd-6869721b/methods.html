Each route in detail

Unlawful routes are listed so you can recognise them. They carry the penalty provision and no steps.

M1 · Bank conversion and wire of documented China income Lawful, documents needed

FieldDetailSource
Who can use itForeign-passport holders with lawful current-account yuan income earned in ChinaL1 L4 L5 R2 F1
LimitThe documented after-tax amount; no fixed yearly cap in the ruleL1 L4 L5 R2 F1
Published cost10 Nov 2026: spread 0.21% plus CNY 216.85 to CNY 410 per wire at Bank of China, or CNY 146.84 to CNY 280 at Agricultural Bank of ChinaL1 L4 L5 R2 F1
TimeOne branch visit once documents are accepted; wire transit time not publishedL1 L4 L5 R2 F1
For
  • The route the rule itself names for foreign individuals
  • Cheapest priced route found: 0.22% to 0.23% at $400,000
  • One wire can carry the whole documented amount
L1 L4 L5 R2 F1
Against
  • Needs tax vouchers and source documents showing the amount
  • The bank decides whether the documents are enough
  • Intermediary-bank deductions are not published
L1 L4 L5 R2 F1
Steps
  1. Collect your passport, PRC tax payment records for the income, and payroll or contract papers showing the amount.
  2. Ask the branch which papers it accepts; it must check that the purchase matches a real, documented source.
  3. Buy dollars at the bank's selling rate up to the documented after-tax amount.
  4. Wire the dollars to your own US account. Bank of China charges 0.1% (min CNY 50, max CNY 260) plus CNY 150 cable.
  5. Optional: pay USD 25 for full-amount arrival so intermediary banks do not deduct from the wire.
  6. Keep the exchange memo, wire receipt and tax records, and note the dollar value and date for your US return.
L1 L4 L5 R2 F1

M3 · Emigrant property transfer Lawful, SAFE approval needed

FieldDetailSource
Who can use itPeople who moved from mainland China and obtained foreign permanent residence or citizenship, for assets owned before thatL8 L10 L16
LimitThe approved amount; staged above CNY 200,000L8 L10 L16
Published costEach stage is a wire priced like M1; approval fee not publishedL8 L10 L16
TimeTwo years from the first remittance to the last, under Art. 6L8 L10 L16
For
  • The route written for former PRC citizens with pre-emigration assets
  • Covers savings, sale proceeds and other lawful property in one application
L8 L10 L16
Against
  • Requires proof that the household registration was cancelled
  • Requires SAFE approval and a tax certificate
  • Art. 6 releases half first, half of the rest after one year, the balance after two
L8 L10 L16
Steps
  1. Confirm the assets were yours before you obtained the foreign status.
  2. Gather the application and form, identity documents, proof of household-registration cancellation, proof of income source and title, and the tax certificate.
  3. Apply once, for the whole amount, to the SAFE branch where your household was registered.
  4. After approval the bank sells foreign exchange and remits in the stages set by Art. 6; a total of CNY 200,000 or less may go at once.
  5. Price each stage as a separate wire.

M4 · Inheritance transfer Lawful, SAFE approval needed

FieldDetailSource
Who can use itForeign citizens who inherit property in ChinaL8 T3 A6
LimitThe approved amount; one application per decedentL8 T3 A6
Published costA wire priced like M1; approval fee not publishedL8 T3 A6
TimeApproval time not published; may be remitted in one or several paymentsL8 T3 A6
For
  • May be remitted in a single payment after approval
  • China levies no inheritance tax per the 2017 Ministry of Finance statement
L8 T3 A6
Against
  • Requires notarised inheritance documents and SAFE approval
  • Bequests above $100,000 in a year trigger US Form 3520
L8 T3 A6
Steps
  1. Obtain notarised proof of inheritance and the decedent's title documents.
  2. Turn the estate assets into yuan and obtain the tax certificate.
  3. Apply once per decedent to the SAFE branch where the decedent's household was registered.
  4. After approval the bank sells foreign exchange and remits in one or several payments.
  5. File Form 3520 with your US return if the year's bequests from the foreign estate exceed $100,000.
