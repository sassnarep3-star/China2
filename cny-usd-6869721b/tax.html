Tax and penalties

China taxes the income behind the money, not the transfer. The US taxes exchange-rate gain above $200: $28,037 of gain on $400,000 if the yuan dates from 2024.

Exchange-rate gain, $10,000 to $400,000

Gain = dollars received × (1 − 6.6851 ÷ the rate when the yuan was acquired). The IRS yearly average stands in for the acquisition rate; your own figure is the spot rate on the day you received each amount. Yuan from 2024 shows a gain of 7.01%, from 2025 6.23%; yuan from 2021 shows a loss of 3.61%. Negative numbers are losses. No average exists yet for 2026.
