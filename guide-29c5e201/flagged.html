F04 · Quota wording nuance for foreign individuals needs review

SAFE's 2007 English press conference describes the sales quota for domestic and foreign individuals; the 2024 Tianjin FAQ says only Foreign Permanent Resident ID holders enjoy the purchase facilitation quota; the FX Regulations Art. 52 definition includes foreigners resident 1 full year as 'domestic individuals'. Purchase-side treatment of long-resident foreign passport holders should be confirmed with the bank/SAFE before relying on M02.

Source review: S02 · SAFE (safe.gov.cn)S01 · SAFE Tianjin (safe.gov.cn)S05 · SAFE Tianjin (safe.gov.cn)S13 · SAFE Beijing (safe.gov.cn)

F05 · Emigrant transfer eligibility is narrow (analysis) needs review

移民财产转移 covers emigrants FROM Mainland China (assets acquired before immigration permission, hukou cancellation proofs). A US-born citizen who never held PRC residency generally does not fit this definition. Confirm with SAFE if you believe you qualify.

Source review: S09 · People's Bank of China (pbc.gov.cn)S08 · SAFE (safe.gov.cn)

F14 · Reference exchange rate is for comparison only needs review

All CNY->USD fee conversions on this site use the IRS 2025 yearly average (7.129). The IRS states it has no official exchange rate. Actual conversion happens at the bank/provider's live rate, which is unpublished (F03). Computed USD fee figures are reference conversions, not quotes.
