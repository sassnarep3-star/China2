U.S. filing reminder

IRS explains FBAR is electronically filed with FinCEN, not attached to the U.S. income tax return. FBAR and Form 8938 have different thresholds and can both apply. Consult current IRS forms/instructions for filing year; the thresholds above are not a calculation of your liability.

What counts as source tax vs transfer “penalty”?

  • Income tax generally relates to the underlying source/event (wages, services, rent, sale gain, dividends), not the transfer of already-owned bank principal. Classification and tax basis must be determined from actual records.
  • A tariff or exchange spread is a financial cost, not a tax. Only a live exact-amount provider quote can establish the executed rate and fees.
  • FBAR, Form 8938 and cash reporting are disclosure duties with potential penalties for noncompliance; the filing trigger is not a tax charge on conversion.
