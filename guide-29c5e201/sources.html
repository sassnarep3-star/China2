S01 · SAFE Detailed Rules on Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange (Huifa [2007] No. 1, amended republication 2024-04-30)

Publisher: SAFE Tianjin (safe.gov.cn) · Type: regulator · Repository status: fetched

Locator: Arts. 2, 10-15, 20, 28, 30-31, 35, 38; amendment notes (Huifa [2016] 13, [2023] 8)

Source register synopsis: Art. 2: annual facilitation quota USD 50,000 equivalent per person for settlement and domestic-individual purchases; within quota ID-only at bank. Art. 13(1): foreign individuals purchase FX for lawful current-account RMB income earned in China with ID + transaction-amount materials including tax vouchers. Art. 13(2): reconversion of unused previously exchanged RMB with ID + original exchange memo (valid 24 months); <=USD 500/day (<=USD 1,000 airside before departure) ID-only. Art. 14: domestic individual outward remittance from FX savings account <=USD 50,000/day ID-only, above with authenticity documents. Art. 15: foreign individual outward remittance from FX savings account with ID. Art. 20: emigrant/inheritor property transfer per separate rules. Art. 30: foreign-cash withdrawal >USD 10,000/day requires advance SAFE filing. Art. 39 penalty clause repealed by Huifa [2023] 8.

S15 · Individual Income Tax Law of the PRC (7th amendment 2018-08-31, official English)

Publisher: State Taxation Administration法规库 (fgk.chinatax.gov.cn) · Type: statute · Repository status: fetched

Locator: Arts. 1-3, 6-11

Source register synopsis: Resident individual: domiciled in China or >=183 days in tax year; residents taxed on China and overseas income; non-residents on China-source income. Comprehensive income progressive 3%-45%; business income 5%-35%; interest/dividends/property lease/property transfer/incidental flat 20%. Resident comprehensive taxable income after RMB 60,000 + special/itemized deductions; non-resident wages after RMB 5,000/month. Asset-transfer taxable income = proceeds minus original value and reasonable expenses. Emigration with hukou cancellation triggers a tax return (Art. 10(5)).
