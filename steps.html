Exactly how each route works

Nothing here has been executed. No account was accessed, no bank contacted, no return filed. The steps describe the sequence an official source implies; they cannot promise that a particular branch will follow it on a particular day.

M01 — Documented income conversion and wire

The primary lawful route for money that came from documented, taxed PRC income. No annual USD ceiling is stated for a non-domestic individual; the bank's document review is the gate.

  1. Assemble the identity file

    Passport and the bank's customer records. The bank must verify the validity of the identity document and the authenticity of the other materials.

    S01S02

  2. Assemble the source file

    The contract, payroll records, award letter, lease or sale contract that produced the RMB, plus the PRC tax payment certificate or the employer's withholding records. Art. 13(1) expressly lists tax vouchers among the documents.

    S02

  3. Ask the bank to confirm your category in writing

    Ask whether you will be recorded as 境外个人 (non-domestic individual) (non-domestic) or 境内个人 (domestic individual) (domestic) for this transaction. Decree 532 Art. 52(2) counts a foreigner resident in China for one year as 境内个人 (domestic individual), which changes the applicable rule set. Record the answer.

    S11S02

  4. Request the all-in quote before signing anything

    Ask for: the spread against the PBOC central parity, the outgoing wire fee, the intermediary lift, and the receiving US bank's fee. No official source publishes these, so the number must come from the bank in writing.

    S01

  5. Convert and remit in one instruction where possible

    A conversion followed by a separate remittance day creates a second FX exposure and can trigger a second fee. Art. 13 covers conversion; Art. 15(1) covers remittance from an FX savings account with valid ID.

    S01S02

  6. Keep the paper trail for both jurisdictions

    Retain the conversion slip, the wire confirmation and the tax certificate. These are the evidence that the money was already taxed and that no gain needs recognising under §988(e).

    S06S08

  7. File the US information returns

    FBAR if the aggregate account balance exceeded USD 10,000 at any time; Form 8938 if the published thresholds were met; no income is reported merely because the money arrived.

    S08S09S06

M02 — Re-conversion against the original exchange memo

The only route that can avoid tax documents entirely, but only for RMB that came from an earlier conversion of foreign currency, and only for 24 months.

  1. Find the original exchange memo (原兑换水单 (original exchange memo))

    Without the memo this route does not exist. The 24-month clock runs from the date of the original conversion.

    S02

  2. Compare the memo amount with the balance

    Only the amount covered by the memo can be re-converted this way; any excess falls back to the M01 documentation route.

    S02

  3. Present ID and the memo at the counter

    Art. 13(2) requires valid ID plus the original exchange memo.

    S02

  4. Watch the 24-month expiry

    If the memo is older, the bank can refuse; plan the conversion before the window closes.

    S02

  5. Report on the US side as a non-event

    Returning your own previously converted principal is not income; a personal-transaction FX movement is outside §988 unless the gain would exceed $200.

    S06

M03 — Small-amount walk-in conversion with ID only

A safety valve for small sums; it cannot move a large balance in any reasonable time.

  1. Confirm the same-day ceiling

    USD 500 equivalent per day, or USD 1,000 at pre-departure port outlets, with ID only.

    S02

  2. Keep each day below the ceiling

    The rule is drawn per same-day cumulative amount, not per transaction.

    S02

  3. Do not recruit other people

    Splitting across people or cards to evade limits is expressly prohibited and carries the penalty scale in Art. 39-45 of Decree 532.

    S01S11

M04 — Remit an existing FX savings-account balance

The cheapest route when it is available: no conversion is needed, so no spread is paid.

  1. Check the currency of the balance

    This route applies only if the money is already in foreign currency inside the account, not RMB.

    S02

  2. Present ID and request the outward remittance

    Art. 15(1) requires valid ID for a non-domestic individual's outward remittance from an FX savings account.

    S02

  3. Confirm whether the bank applies the domestic-individual daily rule

    Art. 14 sets a USD 50,000 per day ID-only threshold for domestic individuals; banks sometimes apply the stricter rule to everyone. Ask which applies to you.

    S02

  4. File the US information returns

    FBAR and Form 8938 thresholds are unchanged by the transfer itself.

M05 — Overseas ATM withdrawals

Fast, small, and hard-capped. Useful for spending money, not for repatriating a balance.

  1. Confirm the card is enabled for overseas use

    The issuer must have the cross-border switch on.

    S03

  2. Stay inside RMB 10,000 per card per day

    The per-card daily ceiling was adjusted from USD 1,000 to RMB 10,000 equivalent.

    S03

  3. Track the annual ceiling

    RMB 100,000 equivalent per individual per civil year across all cards. Exceeding it suspends overseas cash withdrawal for the current and the following year.

    S03

  4. Do not borrow or lend cards

    Art. I expressly prohibits borrowing or lending cards to evade the rule.

    S03

  5. Declare cash if it crosses the US border

    More than USD 10,000 in currency at one time must be reported on FinCEN Form 105, aggregated across a family or group.

    S14

M08 — Sale of PRC property, then remit the after-tax proceeds

The largest and slowest of the personal capital routes, and the one where the tax base matters most.

  1. Establish the acquisition cost and date

    PRC IIT on an asset transfer is charged on the gain, and the USD basis drives the §988 analysis.

    S04S06

  2. Settle PRC tax on the transfer

    The flat rate for income from transfer of assets is 20% of the taxable gain after the original value and reasonable expenses.

    S04

  3. Obtain the tax payment certificate

    This is the document the bank will want when the proceeds are converted.

    Property purchases by non-domestic individuals are governed by the separate property-market FX rules referenced in Art. 21 of the implementing rules.

    S02

  5. Convert and remit, keeping the sale file

    Sale contract, tax certificate, conversion slip and wire confirmation belong together.

M11 — The USD 50,000 annual quota (domestic individuals)

The rule most often quoted online, and the one most often misapplied to US passport holders.

  1. Establish which category the bank will use

    Art. 9 of Decree 2006 No. 3 ties the annual limit to an individual's FX sales and to domestic individuals' purchases. Art. 52(2) of Decree 532 counts a foreigner resident in China for one year as 境内个人 (domestic individual). Ask the bank in writing.

    S01S11

  2. Use it only for your own money

    Borrowing other people's quotas is the prohibited M18 route.

    S01S11

  3. Expect the system record

    Every quota transaction is recorded in the SAFE personal FX system, so the ceiling is enforced automatically.

    S02

M12 — Transfers between immediate relatives' FX accounts

Lawful for genuine family support; unlawful the moment it is used to split one person's money.

  1. Establish the relationship

    Parents, children and spouse, with a household register, marriage certificate or notarised proof.

    S02

  2. Keep it domestic

    A transfer between a domestic and a non-domestic individual's account is treated as a cross-border transaction, not a family transfer.

    S02

  3. Do not use it to evade the annual limit

    Art. 7 of Decree 2006 No. 3 prohibits splitting to evade limits.

Before you start: the document set that decides everything

  • Identity — passport and the bank's customer file (S01 Art. 5; S02 Art. 26).
  • Source — the contract, payroll, award, lease or sale document behind the RMB (S02 Art. 13(1)).
  • Tax — the 完税证明 (tax payment certificate) or withholding records; without them the bank's Art. 13 review has nothing to approve (S02, S04).
  • US filing history — FBAR and Form 8938 for every year the thresholds were met; a missed year changes the plan more than any fee (S08, S09).
