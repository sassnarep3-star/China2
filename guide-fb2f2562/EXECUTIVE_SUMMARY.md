# China money — executive summary (guide-fb2f2562)

Official sources read 2026-11-11. Site: `guide-fb2f2562/index.html`. Not tax or legal advice.

1. There is no tax on converting CNY to USD in any of the 30 official pages read today. The real "conversion penalty" has four parts: the bank's exchange spread (0.21% at Bank of China, 0.50% at HSBC China to the board midpoint today), a capped wire fee (at most CNY 410 at BOC, CNY 620 at HSBC), PRC income tax that should already have been paid on how the money was earned, and US reporting duties. ([S09](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2008/0806/5321.html), [S01](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/), [S02](https://www.services.cn-banking.hsbc.com.cn/PublicContent/common/rate/zh/exchange-rates.html), [S03](https://www.boc.cn/ebanking/service/cs1/200905/t20090506_990712.html), [S04](https://www.hsbc.com.cn/content/dam/hsbc/cn/docs/document-download/tariff-of-Accounts-services-for-personal-customers-v2.pdf), [S20](https://shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfw/zcfgk/grsds/201809/t441789.html), [S21](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/report-of-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts-fbar))
2. The lawful path for a US citizen is a documented purchase of USD at the bank holding the money, followed by a wire to a US account in your own name. The bank's gate is paperwork: passport plus proof of where the RMB came from and that PRC tax was paid (SAFE 汇发[2007]1号 Art. 13; SAFE Tianjin 2026 Q&A). ([S08](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2007/0105/5320.html), [S10](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html), [S13](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2005/content_64294.htm))
3. Cheapest published route for most amounts: Bank of China online purchase + online cross-border wire (an existing HSBC Premier customer pays less with Global Transfer at $10,000, before the CNY 300 monthly Premier fee). Known cost (fee + spread to the board midpoint) is $53.36 at USD 10,000 and $898.54 at USD 400,000 (0.225%). The same transfer through HSBC China costs $2,083.13 at USD 400,000. Correspondent and receiving fees are not published and are left blank. ([S01](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/), [S03](https://www.boc.cn/ebanking/service/cs1/200905/t20090506_990712.html), [S02](https://www.services.cn-banking.hsbc.com.cn/PublicContent/common/rate/zh/exchange-rates.html), [S04](https://www.hsbc.com.cn/content/dam/hsbc/cn/docs/document-download/tariff-of-Accounts-services-for-personal-customers-v2.pdf))
4. If you emigrated from China and the money is pre-emigration property, use SAFE's emigrant property transfer: free, 25 working days at most, and staged remittance was abolished in 2014. ([S12](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2014/0124/5476.html), [S14](https://www.safe.gov.cn/hebei/file/file/20240816/86fd609207224d71b23cfda39ebe4bbb.pdf))
5. Avoid: splitting across other people's quotas (watch list for 3 years), underground swaps (fine up to 30%), disguised transfers (30%–100%), and any crypto/stablecoin bridge (illegal since 银发〔2026〕42号). ([S15](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2015/1231/5442.html), [S09](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2008/0806/5321.html), [S18](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html))
6. US side: none of the IRS, FinCEN or CBP pages read taxes the wire itself; what can be taxable is FX gain, which is not excluded above USD 200 per conversion (26 USC 988(e)(2)) — RMB bought at the 2025 IRS average of 7.129 and sold at 6.6886 shows about a 6.18% USD gain. File FBAR if your foreign accounts exceeded USD 10,000 at any time, and check Form 8938. FEIE is USD 132,900 for 2026 and USD 137,300 for 2027 (Rev. Proc. 2026-35 §3.41, verified in the official PDF today). ([S27](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm), [S07](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/yearly-average-currency-exchange-rates), [S01](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/), [S21](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/report-of-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts-fbar), [S23](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/comparison-of-form-8938-and-fbar-requirements), [S25](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i2555), [S26](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-26-35.pdf))
7. This repository's earlier sections still contain lines that conflict with the official text — emigrant transfers described as staged, a "dollar-for-dollar" foreign tax credit — and crypto mentioned in places without its illegal status (to check). Every line is listed with file and line number on review.html.
