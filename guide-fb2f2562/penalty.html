The “conversion penalty”, layer by layer

There is no single penalty. These are the separate costs and risks people lump together.

LayerSizeWhat the official text saysSources
P1 · Tax on the act of convertingNone foundNone of the 30 official pages read today imposes a tax on exchanging CNY for USD. Current-account payments are not restricted [S09 Art. 5]; what applies is a documentary test [S08 Art. 13, S10].S09S08S10

Outward payments over USD 50,000 for income items need a tax filing; a domestic individual's own funds for personal use are exempt; foreigners follow the personal FX rules [S19].S20S19
P6 · US tax on exchange gainGain above USD 200 per conversion is not excluded§988(e)(2) [S27]. With the RMB stronger than its 2025 IRS average (7.129) [S07] against 6.6886 today [S01], RMB acquired earlier can show a USD gain on conversion. The tax rate on that gain depends on your return — blank (flag F-11).S27S07S01

P8 · PRC penalties (only on unlawful routes)Up to 30% of the amount; 30%–100% in serious evasion cases; watch list 3 years[S09 Arts. 39, 45; S15]. Crypto routes are illegal outright [S18].S09S15S18
