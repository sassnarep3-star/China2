U.S. reporting

IRS / FinCEN sources
Foreign-account reporting and income tax are different obligations.

An account can create reporting duties even when no money is moved. A transfer does not cure unreported income or missed account filings. This is a checklist, not a determination that a form is required in your circumstances.

LOWER DOUBLE-TAX RISK

Keep a clean evidence file

A

Income and ownership

Account statements, account-holder/beneficial-owner records, source contracts, payroll statements, property or inheritance papers, and dates/amounts of deposits.

B

China tax evidence

Tax filing receipts, withholding certificates, payment vouchers and bank confirmations. Ask whether the bank needs a tax clearance or other document for this transaction type.

C

Conversion records

CNY amount and date, exchange rate, USD proceeds, wire confirmations, bank fee schedule, intermediary deductions and the U.S.-dollar conversion calculation.

!
Foreign tax credit is limited—not an automatic refund.

IRS Form 1116 instructions govern eligibility, source/category matching, and the limitation. A U.S. CPA experienced in international individuals can review prior-year compliance and any correction options.
