Bottom line for a U.S. citizen with CNY in China: the lawful path is documentation-first — prove the source of the balance, settle any PRC tax on the income that created it, then convert and wire through the bank. There is no separate PRC tax on the act of exchanging currency in the SAFE guidance retrieved, but that is not a guarantee of no taxable income or currency gain on the U.S. side. Published HSBC wire fees are small (see Findings A05); the unpublished spread and the paperwork are the real costs.
