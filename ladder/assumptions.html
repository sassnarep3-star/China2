What is published, and what is modelled

This is the honesty page. Anything below is an assumption used to produce an estimate. None of it is a regulator's figure, a bank quote, or a fact about your account.

The rule applied throughout. If an official source states a value, it is printed with a link. If no official source states it, the value is either left blank and flagged, or presented here as a labelled assumption with a range. It is never presented as a verified fact.

Published values that the model actually uses

  • Wire issuance (Premier/Advance): 0.1% of remittance amount, minimum CNY 100, maximum CNY 500 per transaction, plus CNY 120 per cable; Private Banking individuals are exempt. S21 · hsbc.com.cn
  • Same-name HSBC Global Transfer to self-name overseas HSBC accounts: Free for Private Banking and Premier; Advance pays CNY 145 indicative per transaction (actual USD 20). Available only where Global Transfer to Self is offered — tariff remark lists Canada, US, UK, Australia and Hong Kong SAR. S21 · hsbc.com.cn
  • Foreign-currency notes withdrawal over the counter: Free for Private Banking, Premier and Advance; USD/HKD of at least USD 5,000 equivalent needs at least three working days' advance notice. CNY↔notes conversion uses notes buy/sell board rates (the modelled cash spread), not a fixed fee. S21 · hsbc.com.cn
  • International Education Payment (platform-specific): USD 7 actual for Advance (CNY 50 indicative) — listed only to prevent the earlier confusion with a general same-name Global Transfer fee. S21 · hsbc.com.cn
  • CNY 100,000 per person per calendar year of overseas ATM cash; CNY 10,000 per card per day. S03 · safe.gov.cn
  • USD 500 per day onshore / USD 1,000 per day port-side unused-RMB reconversion exemption (24-month original memo rule for larger amounts). S17 · safe.gov.cn
  • USD 5,000 / 10,000 cash carrying thresholds. S15 · safe.gov.cn
  • Emigration and inheritance transfers: local SAFE branch approves; SAFE Circular Huifa [2014] No. 2 §VI cancelled the >RMB 500,000 head-office filing and cancelled mandatory multi-stage installments so banks may remit in a lump sum or in batches within the approved limit. S05 · pbc.gov.cn

What this is. A source-linked model of what it costs to convert and move RMB out of mainland China into USD, tier by tier from $10,000 to $400,000. Every published figure is linked to the authority that publishes it; every figure that is not published is labelled as a model assumption and kept separate.

What this is not. Not individualized tax, legal or investment advice, and not a quote. The bank's executable rate on the day is the only real spread. No transfers were made and no returns filed.
