F02 Residence, not citizenship, decides which individual category applies

severity: high

State Council Decree 532 Art. 52(2) defines “domestic individual” to include a foreign national who has resided continuously in China for one year or more. A US citizen who lived in China for years may therefore sit in a different category from a US citizen who never did, which changes whether the annual purchase facility applies. Prior dashboards in this repository state the opposite as settled.

S04 · safe.gov.cn S02 · safe.gov.cn

F03 A SAFE notice carries conflicting status across SAFE's own mirrors

severity: medium

Hui Fa [2015] No. 49 appears with a “repealed” label on one SAFE branch page and without it on another. The anti-splitting rule is independently stated in PBOC Decree [2006] No. 3 Art. 7, so the substance is not in doubt, but the citation is not relied on alone.

severity: low

What this is. A source-linked model of what it costs to convert and move RMB out of mainland China into USD, tier by tier from $10,000 to $400,000. Every published figure is linked to the authority that publishes it; every figure that is not published is labelled as a model assumption and kept separate.

What this is not. Not individualized tax, legal or investment advice, and not a quote. The bank's executable rate on the day is the only real spread. No transfers were made and no returns filed.
