Exactly how each route works

Ordered for the two routes that cover most real cases (R01 and R02). Nothing here requires you to guess: every step maps to a document or a rule cited at the foot of the block.

Before you go to a branch. Ask the bank for two things in writing: the exchange memo (水单) and an all-in executable quote showing the rate applied, the commission, the cable fee and any correspondent deduction. The spread is the only large variable and it is never published.

R01 Documented after-tax income: bank FX conversion + SWIFT wire 1-10 business days
  1. 1
    Download your personal IIT payment record from the PRC individual income tax app or request a 完税证明 at the tax service hall for the years the balance was earned.
  2. 2
    Assemble the income file: contract or invoices, payslips, bank statements showing the credits, and the tax record. Amounts must tie to the balance.
  3. 3
    Book a counter appointment at the account-holding branch (foreign-exchange business is not reliably transacted at a machine for this category).
  4. 4
    Ask for an executable all-in quote: the USD selling rate applied, the 0.1% commission with its CNY 100/500 floor and cap, and the CNY 120 cable fee.
  5. 5
    Complete the FX purchase; keep the exchange memo (水单) — it is the document that proves the conversion was authorised and is needed for any later re-conversion.
  6. 6
    Instruct the wire to your own US account. Keep the SWIFT MT103 / remittance advice.
  7. 7
    File FBAR (FinCEN 114) by 15 April and Form 8938 with your return if thresholds are met; claim PRC tax on Form 1116.

Bring: Passport; Employment contract or service invoice/payment proof; PRC IIT payment record / tax certificate (完税证明 / 纳税记录); Bank card and account opening documents; Source-of-funds statement for the receiving US bank

R02 Documented income routed through same-name HSBC Global Transfer 1-5 business days
  1. 1
    Confirm both accounts are in exactly the same name and that Global Transfer is enabled for the China-US corridor.
  2. 2
    Complete the R01 documentation and FX conversion inside HSBC China.
  3. 3
    Use Global Transfer to move the USD to your own HSBC US account: free for Premier, reference price USD 7 per transfer otherwise (S06 item 5.3.1).
  4. 4
    Keep the transfer confirmation as the audit trail; the receiving bank still needs a source-of-funds note.
  5. 5
    File FBAR / Form 8938 / Form 1116 as in R01.

Bring: Everything in R01; HSBC US account in the same name; Premier status confirmed on both sides

R03 Re-conversion of unused RMB bought with foreign currency (original exchange memo) 1-10 business days
  1. 1
    Locate the original exchange memo for each conversion you now want to reverse.
  2. 2
    Check the date: the memo is valid for 24 months from the exchange date.
  3. 3
    Present the passport and memo at the counter and request conversion back to the original currency, then remit.
  4. 4
    Keep the new memo; it is the receipt for the outbound leg.

Bring: Passport; Original exchange memo (原兑换水单), within 24 months; Bank statements showing the original conversion

R04 Passport-only small-amount conversion at a bank counter one banking day per USD 500
  1. 1
    Attend a branch counter with your passport.
  2. 2
    Request conversion of no more than the equivalent of USD 500 in one day.
  3. 3
    Take USD cash or instruct a wire from the resulting FX balance.
  4. 4
    Do not repeat this pattern to move a large balance: that is the conduct S01 Art. 7 and S18 prohibit, and it is the classic trigger for a watch-list referral.

Bring: Passport

R05 Airside pre-departure conversion at a port-side counter one departure per USD 1,000
  1. 1
    Convert no more than the equivalent of USD 1,000 in a single day at a port-side counter before departure.
  2. 2
    Declare the cash to US CBP on FinCEN Form 105 if you arrive with more than USD 10,000 in total (S13).
  3. 3
    Do not treat repeated trips as a transfer mechanism.

Bring: Passport; boarding pass

S17 · safe.gov.cn

R06 Withdraw USD cash in China and carry it out one trip per USD 5,000
  1. 1
    Convert or withdraw USD cash at the counter. On the verified tariff a foreign-cash withdrawal costs 0.25%-0.4% with a CNY 40 minimum on non-Premier accounts and is waived for Premier (S06 item 3.1.1).
  2. 2
    Above USD 5,000 and up to USD 10,000, obtain a bank carrying certificate before departure.
  3. 3
    Above USD 10,000 a SAFE-issued certificate is required and is granted only in listed special cases.
  4. 4
    Declare to CBP on arrival above USD 10,000 in aggregate.
  5. 5
    Deposit in the US and keep the withdrawal and declaration records.

Bring: Passport; Bank withdrawal slip; Carrying certificate for USD 5,000-10,000; FinCEN Form 105 on arrival above USD 10,000

R07 Emigration asset transfer (移民财产转移) up to 2 years of staged remittances
  1. 1
    Apply once, to the SAFE branch where your pre-emigration household registration was, for the whole amount you intend to move.
  2. 2
    Settle the PRC tax position on the realised assets and obtain the tax certificate.
  3. 3
    Obtain approval: up to RMB 500,000 equivalent is decided by the local SAFE branch; above that it goes to SAFE head office.
  4. 4
    Remit in the staged pattern: up to half first, up to half of the remainder after one year, the balance after two years. Amounts up to RMB 200,000 may go in one remittance once approved.
  5. 5
    The bank must pay directly to your own overseas account; cash payment inside China is not permitted for this category.
  6. 6
    Keep every approval and remittance advice for the US side.

Bring: Written application stating the reason and a full account of source and realisation; Approval/record of emigration status; Proof of ownership and realisation of the assets; PRC tax clearance certificate for the assets; Notarised documents where required

R08 Inheritance asset transfer (继承财产转移) 1-6 months
  1. 1
    Obtain and notarise the inheritance documents and the deceased's title documents.
  2. 2
    Obtain the tax certificate from the tax authority where the assets are located.
  3. 3
    File one application at the SAFE branch where the deceased was registered; one application covers the whole estate from that decedent.
  4. 4
    Remit in one go or in instalments once approved.
  5. 5
    Report the bequest in the US on Form 3520 if the threshold is met (not verified in this study - see F07).

Bring: Written application describing the relationship and the estate; Application form signed by you or your agent; Passport plus a certificate of overseas residence issued or authenticated by a Chinese embassy/consulate; Notarised proof of inheritance; Notarised proof of the deceased's title to the assets; Tax certificate or payment proof issued by the tax authority where the assets are; Notarised power of attorney if an agent acts

S05 · m.safe.gov.cn

R09 Sale proceeds of PRC residential property 3-12 months
  1. 1
    Complete the sale and settle PRC IIT: the registration authority will not transfer title without the tax clearance certificate (S07 Art. 15).
  2. 2
    Apply to the bank/SAFE for conversion of the net proceeds under the property rules.
  3. 3
    Convert and remit to your own US account.
  4. 4
    Report the disposition in the US and claim the PRC tax on Form 1116.

Bring: Title certificate; Sale contract and completion documents; PRC tax payment certificate for the transfer (checked at registration under S07 Art. 15); Proof the property was self-use where an exemption is claimed

R10 Dividends from a PRC company you own 3-12 months
  1. 1
    Confirm distributable profits after 25% EIT and statutory reserves.
  2. 2
    Pass the distribution resolution and withhold dividend tax at the treaty rate with the residence certificate in place.
  3. 3
    Obtain the withholding payment certificate.
  4. 4
    Convert and remit through the company's bank with the resolution and tax documents.
  5. 5
    Claim the 10% withholding on Form 1116 in the US.

Bring: Audited financial statements and profit distribution resolution; EIT payment proof; Treaty residence certification for the 10% rate; Tax withholding payment certificate

R11 Overseas ATM withdrawals on a PRC bank card about 14,000 USD per calendar year per person
  1. 1
    Withdraw abroad within RMB 10,000 equivalent per card per day.
  2. 2
    Stay within RMB 100,000 equivalent per person per calendar year.
  3. 3
    Deposit the USD and keep the ATM receipts.

R13 Pay US obligations directly from the China account 1-10 business days
  1. 1
    Obtain the genuine invoice from the US payee.
  2. 2
    Present it at the counter with your passport; current-account payments are convertible against the underlying document (S04 Art. 12, S02 Art. 11).
  3. 3
    Instruct the wire to the payee, not to yourself.
  4. 4
    Keep the invoice and payment advice together.

Bring: Invoice or tuition statement; Proof of the obligation; Passport; The bank's current-account payment form

What this is. A source-linked model of what it costs to convert and move RMB out of mainland China into USD, tier by tier from $10,000 to $400,000. Every published figure is linked to the authority that publishes it; every figure that is not published is labelled as a model assumption and kept separate.

What this is not. Not individualized tax, legal or investment advice, and not a quote. The bank's executable rate on the day is the only real spread. No transfers were made and no returns filed.
