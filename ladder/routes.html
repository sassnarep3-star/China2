R15 Sell RMB privately to someone who wants USD (private FX swap) Unlawful

Unlawful · rejected

Not lawful for anyone.

Why not

  • Confiscation plus a fine up to the value of the amount.
  • Criminal exposure.
  • Counterparty risk with no recourse.

What the rule says

Do not do this. S04 Art. 45 covers private, disguised and brokered FX trading: confiscation of the proceeds plus a fine of up to 30% of the amount, or 30%-100% if serious, with criminal liability for larger cases.

S04 · safe.gov.cn

R16 Underground bank / 'huipiao' (offshore RMB, onshore settlement) Unlawful

Unlawful · rejected

Not lawful for anyone.

Why not

  • Funds are typically seized.
  • Fines of 30%-100% and criminal liability.
  • No legal recourse if the operator disappears.

What the rule says

Do not do this. It is the paradigm case of Art. 39 evasion and Art. 45 illegal FX trading, and is prosecuted as illegal business operation at scale.

S04 · safe.gov.cn

R17 Buy crypto or USDT in China and sell abroad Unlawful

Unlawful · rejected

Not lawful for anyone in China.

Why not

  • Illegal financial activity
  • No recourse, and exchanges are blocked from RMB rails.
  • Creates a US tax and reporting mess on top.

What the rule says

Do not do this. Yin Fa [2026] No. 42 (which replaced Yin Fa [2021] No. 237) states that exchanging legal tender for virtual currency is an illegal financial activity, that institutions may not provide settlement services for it, and that losses are borne by the participant.

S16 · pbc.gov.cn

R18 Borrow a relative's or friend's USD 50,000 quota Unlawful

Unlawful · rejected

Not lawful for anyone.

Why not

  • Watch list and refusal of counter service for years.
  • Both sides exposed, including your relative.
  • The quota may not even apply to you (see F02).

What the rule says

Do not do this. S01 Art. 7 prohibits splitting to evade limits; S18 puts borrowers and lenders on a watch list for the current year plus two years; S04 supplies the fine.
