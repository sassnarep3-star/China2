The “penalty”, broken down

Four different things get called a conversion penalty. Sorting them is the whole problem: one is a tax you may already have paid, one is bank revenue, one is a fine for getting it wrong, and one is a US reporting duty that has nothing to do with conversion.

These are statutory maxima, applied case by case. At $400,000 a 30% fine is $120,000 — which is why no workaround beats the documented route, even before criminal exposure.

The foreign tax credit is what stops the same income being taxed twice. It cannot reduce US tax on US-source income, and it is capped at the US tax on the foreign-source income, so an unusually high PRC rate does not automatically become a US refund.

4 · The one number that is not in any statute

The FX spread. It is the largest component of a lawful conversion and no regulator publishes it, because it is a commercial price. Every table on this site therefore shows a band and labels it as a model assumption (A2–A5). A bank quote replaces it; nothing else can.

5 · If the PRC tax was never paid

Then the amount people quote as a “conversion penalty” is the unpaid PRC tax plus, potentially, the Art. 39 or Art. 40 fine. The lawful sequence is: establish the income character, settle with the tax authority, obtain the certificate, convert under R01, then claim the PRC tax in the US on Form 1116. In most cases where the PRC income tax rate is at or above the U.S. rate on the same foreign-source " "income, a qualifying Form 1116 credit can substantially offset U.S. tax on that income, subject to " "sourcing, income-category, legal-liability and limitation rules (S12; see C05).

What this is. A source-linked model of what it costs to convert and move RMB out of mainland China into USD, tier by tier from $10,000 to $400,000. Every published figure is linked to the authority that publishes it; every figure that is not published is labelled as a model assumption and kept separate.

What this is not. Not individualized tax, legal or investment advice, and not a quote. The bank's executable rate on the day is the only real spread. No transfers were made and no returns filed.
