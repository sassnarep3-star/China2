Cost tables by amount

Same question at six amounts. Within each table the routes are ordered by the top of their cost range, so the cheapest lawful route is first. Every row links to the authority behind its ceiling.

How to read a number. “All-in cost” = published bank fees (only where a tariff was verified) + a modelled FX spread band. It excludes PRC tax on the source income, which is shown separately on Tax & penalties, because it depends on the character of the money and not on the transfer. It also excludes US preparer fees, which were not verified.

The full 600-row model covering all 40 tiers: costs.csv · costs.json · routes.json · sources.json · assumptions.json

What this is. A source-linked model of what it costs to convert and move RMB out of mainland China into USD, tier by tier from $10,000 to $400,000. Every published figure is linked to the authority that publishes it; every figure that is not published is labelled as a model assumption and kept separate.

What this is not. Not individualized tax, legal or investment advice, and not a quote. The bank's executable rate on the day is the only real spread. No transfers were made and no returns filed.
