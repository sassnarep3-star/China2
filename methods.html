M01 · Documented after-tax RMB income → bank conversion → SWIFT wire Lawful

Present valid ID plus documents proving the amount and the lawful RMB source (including PRC tax vouchers) at an authorised bank; the bank converts RMB to USD and remits it to your US account.

Verified limit. No annual USD cap is stated for a non-domestic individual's conversion of lawful current-account income; the bank reviews authenticity. The USD 50,000 annual total in Art. 2 is expressly tied to domestic individuals' purchases and to all individuals' FX sales.

Cost. 0% PRC tax on the conversion itself (S04 Art. 2 lists no such category); the tax is on the underlying income.

Capacity. No annual USD cap is stated for this route; one documented operation can cover the whole amount.

Who. US citizen holding a mainland account   Risk. Low, if the source trail is complete.

This is the route the research points to first for documented, already-taxed money.

M02 · Re-conversion of unused RMB from an earlier FX conversion (original exchange memo) Lawful

Convert the leftover RMB back to the original currency against the original exchange memo (原兑换水单 (original exchange memo)); the memo is valid for 24 months from the conversion date.

Verified limit. Bounded by the amount on the original memo and the 24-month validity window.

Cost. 0% tax: this is a return of your own previously converted principal, not new income.

Capacity. No verified cap, or the cap is a personal fact not published anywhere.

Who. Anyone who previously converted FX into RMB in China   Risk. Low, but only if the memo exists and is within 24 months.

Amounts on the memo are personal facts and are not published anywhere; left unverified.

M03 · Walk-in small-amount re-conversion with ID only Lawful

Same-day conversion up to the equivalent of USD 500 (or USD 1,000 at pre-departure port outlets) needs only valid ID, with no tax or exchange documents.

Verified limit. USD 500 per day; USD 1,000 per day at port outlets; per person.

Cost. 0% tax. Commercial spread/fee unverified.

Capacity. Daily ceiling applies.

Who. Non-domestic individuals   Risk. Impractical for large balances: at USD 500/day, USD 400,000 takes 800 counter visits.

A safety valve, not a repatriation plan.

Step-by-step for M03 →

M04 · Remit an existing FX savings-account balance abroad Lawful

If the money is already USD (or another foreign currency) inside a PRC FX savings account, remit it abroad by presenting valid ID.

Verified limit. Art. 15(1) states no annual amount for a non-domestic individual's outward remittance from an FX savings account. (Domestic individuals are handled under Art. 14: USD 50,000/day with ID, above that with authenticity documents.)

Cost. 0% PRC tax; 0% US tax on moving your own funds.

Capacity. A single documented event; no published annual cap.

Who. Non-domestic individuals   Risk. Low, but requires the balance to already be FX.

The cheapest lawful path when it applies: no conversion spread, because no conversion is needed.

Step-by-step for M04 →

M05 · Overseas ATM cash withdrawals with a PRC bank card Lawful

Withdraw USD cash from ATMs abroad using the PRC card; the issuer converts at its own rate.

Verified limit. RMB 100,000 equivalent per individual per civil year across all cards; RMB 10,000 equivalent per card per day. Exceeding the annual cap suspends overseas cash withdrawal for the current and the following year.

Cost. 0% tax. Commercial: issuer conversion rate, out-of-network ATM surcharges - unverified.

Capacity. Annual ceiling applies.

Who. Holder of a cross-border-enabled PRC debit card   Risk. Medium: hard annual wall, cash handling, US side must be declared if carried over USD 10,000.

Also note the US CMIR rule if physical cash crosses the border (S14).

Step-by-step for M05 →

M06 · Overseas merchant / POS spending with a PRC card Lawful

Pay genuine foreign merchants (travel, tuition, hotels, retail) directly with the PRC card, so the RMB is consumed abroad rather than converted and repatriated.

Verified limit. No published fixed amount; spending must be genuine personal consumption and the card's own limit applies.

Cost. 0% tax. Not a way to obtain USD cash for other purposes.

Capacity. This channel does not turn the balance into USD you can hold.

Who. Cardholder   Risk. Low but it does not produce movable USD; using it to extract cash is prohibited (M22).

Counts as a legitimate consumption channel only.

M07 · Carry physical cash across the border Lawful, conditional

Withdraw RMB or USD cash in China and carry it out within customs limits.

Verified limit. PRC declaration limits: not re-verified in this session (prior sessions cited the GACC passenger guide, S22). US side verified: more than USD 10,000 must be reported on FinCEN Form 105, aggregated across a family or group.

Cost. 0% tax if declared. Seizure risk if not declared (S14).

Capacity. No verified cap, or the cap is a personal fact not published anywhere.

Who. Travellers   Risk. High: physical loss, theft, seizure, and repeated trips are visible to customs.

A last resort for small amounts.

M08 · Sale of PRC real estate by a foreign individual, then remit after-tax proceeds Lawful, conditional

Sell the property, settle PRC taxes on the transfer, then convert and remit the net proceeds with the sale and tax documents.

Verified limit. No fixed cap; documentation-driven. Real-estate FX handling is governed by the separate property rules referenced in S02 Art. 21.

Cost. PRC IIT on the transfer gain at 20% (S04 Art. 3(3)); 0% tax on the act of conversion.

Capacity. A single documented event; no published annual cap.

Who. Owner of lawfully acquired PRC property   Risk. Medium: tax base and cost-basis proof determine the real cost.

The 'conversion penalty' people feel is usually this 20% on the gain, already owed before any conversion.

M09 · Inheritance outbound transfer (继承财产转移 (inheritance asset transfer)) Lawful, conditional

Apply under the separate individual-property transfer rules referenced by S02 Art. 20, then convert and remit the inherited proceeds.

Verified limit. Procedure-based; the amount follows the estate documents. The 2004 notice is cited from a prior session (S17) and not re-fetched here.

Cost. PRC IIT treatment of inherited assets depends on the asset and the estate's tax position; not quantified here.

Capacity. A single documented event; no published annual cap.

Who. US citizen inheriting mainland assets   Risk. Medium-high: multi-agency approval, slow.

Documentation and approvals, not a tax rate, are the binding constraint.

M10 · Emigrant personal-asset transfer (移民财产转移 (emigrant asset transfer)) — former PRC residents only Lawful, conditional

Former PRC residents who have emigrated may transfer pre-emigration domestic assets abroad under the dedicated procedure.

Verified limit. Procedure-based, one-time in principle; tied to assets held before emigration and to the household-registration cancellation.

Cost. PRC taxes on the underlying assets as applicable; 0% on conversion.

Capacity. A single documented event; no published annual cap.

Who. Naturalised US citizens / PRs who emigrated from mainland China   Risk. Medium-high; unavailable if you never held PRC residence.

Not available to a US citizen who never emigrated from mainland China.

M11 · PRC-citizen USD 50,000/year facilitated FX purchase quota Lawful, conditional

Within the annual total, a domestic individual buys FX at a bank with valid ID and no transaction documents; the SAFE system records it.

Verified limit. USD 50,000 equivalent per person per calendar year (S02 Art. 2; S01 Art. 9).

Cost. 0% tax on the purchase itself.

Capacity. Annual ceiling applies.

Who. Domestic individuals (境内个人 (domestic individual))   Risk. Medium: hard annual ceiling, and eligibility for a US passport holder is contested (see F02).

S11 Art. 52(2) defines 境内个人 (domestic individual) as including foreigners resident in China for one full year, which conflicts with the narrower reading used by an earlier session. Flagged, not resolved.

M12 · Transfer between FX savings accounts of immediate relatives Lawful, conditional

Domestic transfer of FX between the account holder and immediate relatives, with both IDs and a relationship certificate.

Verified limit. Domestic FX-account transfers only; a transfer between a domestic and a non-domestic individual's account is treated as a cross-border transaction (S02 Art. 28(3)).

Cost. 0% tax on the transfer; the receiving relative's own quota and tax position then apply.

Capacity. No verified cap, or the cap is a personal fact not published anywhere.

Who. Account holders with parents/spouse/children in China   Risk. Medium: using relatives' quotas to split amounts is expressly prohibited (M18).

Lawful for genuine family support; unlawful if used to evade limits.

M13 · PRC employer pays salary directly to your US account Lawful, conditional

The employer withholds PRC IIT, then remits net salary abroad through its own bank channels.

Verified limit. No personal cap; the employer's remittance rules and the tax record filing apply.

Cost. PRC IIT withheld at source (3%-45% comprehensive, S04); 0% on the remittance.

Capacity. No verified cap, or the cap is a personal fact not published anywhere.

Who. Employees of a PRC entity   Risk. Low for the employee; depends entirely on the employer.

Often the cleanest paper trail.

M14 · Dividends / profit repatriation from a PRC company you own Lawful, conditional

Distribute after-tax profits, settle withholding, then remit the dividend.

Verified limit. No personal cap; corporate tax and withholding must be settled first.

Cost. PRC withholding on dividends at the 20% statutory flat rate (S04 Art. 3(3)); a treaty rate may reduce this but was not re-verified here (F04).

Capacity. A single documented event; no published annual cap.

Who. Foreign shareholder of a PRC enterprise   Risk. Medium: requires a compliant company and audited accounts.

Corporate-level tax is a separate, earlier layer.

M15 · Equity transfer or company liquidation proceeds Lawful, conditional

Transfer the equity or liquidate, settle PRC taxes on the gain, register the change, then remit.

Verified limit. No fixed cap; the taxable gain and approvals drive the timing.

Cost. PRC IIT on the transfer gain at 20% (S04 Art. 3(3)); 0% on conversion.

Capacity. A single documented event; no published annual cap.

Who. Foreign investor exiting   Risk. Medium-high: valuation disputes are the usual delay.

M16 · Genuine cross-border service fees, royalties or IP licensing Lawful, conditional

Invoice a PRC payer for real services; the payer withholds tax, files the outbound-payment tax record where required, and remits.

Verified limit. No cap; arm's-length pricing and genuine delivery are required.

Cost. PRC withholding on royalties at the 20% statutory flat rate (S04); VAT may also apply (not re-verified).

Capacity. A single documented event; no published annual cap.

Who. US individual or entity invoicing PRC clients   Risk. High if the arrangement is not genuine: fabricating contracts is a criminal-adjacent violation (M21).

M18 · Splitting amounts across friends' or relatives' USD 50,000 quotas (smurfing) Prohibited

Using multiple people's annual quotas, or multiple cards, to move one person's money.

Verified limit. Not lawful — no limit applies because the channel itself is prohibited.

Cost. Not a cost: a penalty. Up to 30% of the amount, or 30%-100% if serious, plus confiscation and possible criminal liability (S11 Arts. 39-45).

Capacity. Prohibited. The 'cost' is a penalty of up to 30% of the amount, or 30%-100% if serious.

Who. —   Risk. Do not use.
