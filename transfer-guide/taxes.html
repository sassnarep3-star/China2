Critical U.S. Citizen Trap — Treaty Saving Clause + Compulsory-Tax Rule: Under Protocol 1 Paragraph 2 of the 1984 U.S.–China Income Tax Treaty, the United States taxes its citizens on worldwide income regardless of residence. However, Article 22(2) is expressly excepted from the Saving Clause, guaranteeing U.S. citizens a Foreign Tax Credit for PRC income taxes paid — PROVIDED you claimed any treaty rate reduction you were entitled to (such as the 10% cap on dividends, interest, and royalties under Arts. 9–11; IRS Foreign Tax Credit rules disallow crediting voluntary excess foreign withholding!).
