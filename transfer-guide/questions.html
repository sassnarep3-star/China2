Decision Checklist: 12 Questions You Must Answer Before Moving Any Money

No decision can be made on the facts provided. The table below lists what we know from official sources, what we do not know about your private account, and exact next steps to discover missing information — every row linked to a primary government or bank source.

How to use this checklist: Work top to bottom. Questions Q01–Q06 are gating — answering them determines which of the 23 methods (M01–M23) is even lawful for you and what the true tax is. Questions Q07–Q12 refine timing, cost, and reporting. Bring this table to both your China bank FX counter and your U.S. cross-border CPA/attorney. Never pay a tax or fee you cannot name — demand the bank's refusal in writing with article, taxpayer, period, and amount.

What we cannot determine from the prompt: The origin of funds, whether PRC tax was already withheld, your SAFE classification (PR-ID holder or not), prior U.S. FBAR/8938 history, your bank and live quote, and whether you have been offered gray-market workarounds. Every What We DO NOT Know cell explains the private fact and How to Discover gives a concrete action.

What to Do Next (Suggested Order)

  1. Classify every tranche (Q01→Q02): Pull 5-year statements + IIT records; separate taxed salary from untaxed receipts, property/inheritance from business profit.
  2. Confirm your SAFE category (Q03) and physical location (Q08): Are you PR-ID holder or standard foreign passport? In China or back in U.S. needing proxy (M05)?
  3. Check U.S. reporting cure (Q05) BEFORE wiring: If prior FBAR/8938 were missed and non-willful, evaluate Streamlined Foreign 0% vs Domestic 5% — wiring first raises willfulness risk.
  4. Get the bank's all-in written quote (Q06) and compare to the published HSBC Oct 2026 tariff: Demand separate lines for tax payment vs. tax certificate, and for commission/cable vs. spread.
  5. Choose the cheapest lawful lump-sum route (M01, M09, M10, M11) and execute with records (Q12): Single documented wire is 6x cheaper in sender fees than 8 annual $50k quota wires.
