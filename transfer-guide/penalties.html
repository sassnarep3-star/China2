Why STA/SAFE Outbound Tax Recordal (> USD 50,000) Is Frequently Misunderstood as a "Tax"

Under STA & SAFE Announcement [2013] No. 40 and Supplementary Announcement [2021] No. 19:

  • When a domestic payer (such as a PRC company paying dividends/remuneration or a Chinese buyer paying > USD 50,000 for your PRC apartment) remits > USD 50,000 outbound, it must file a Tax Recordal Form (服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案表).
  • Filing fee = $0.00, processed online via the Electronic Tax Bureau or on the spot at the tax hall without on-the-spot tax audit, and required only once per contract under the 2021 supplement.
  • When a foreign individual converts their own current-account lawful RMB income already sitting in their personal Mainland account, Article 4 directs banks to apply SAFE's individual FX rule (Hui Fa [2020] No. 14 Art. 59): valid passport + contract/income proof + STA tax payment record (个人所得税纳税记录).
