Independent targeted review · 11 November 2026 · This page is not tax or legal advice.

Do not rely on an unconditional zero-tax or dollar-for-dollar credit claim. The balance’s source, prior filings, tax residence, bank quote and eligibility are unknown. A source-linked row or arithmetic test does not verify every assertion in that row.

Material findings

ItemFinding and actionPrimary source
Foreign tax creditThe statement in transfer-guide/EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md that legitimate Chinese income tax is “creditable dollar-for-dollar” is overbroad. Credit is limited, and tax attributable to excluded foreign earned income cannot also be credited. Compare Form 1116 and Form 2555 with a qualified adviser; no personal credit has been calculated.IRS Topic 856; IRS Publication 514
Treaty ratesThe same summary says “Always claim” a 10% treaty cap. Articles 9–11 have conditions including treaty residence, beneficial ownership and permanent establishment or fixed-base exceptions. A U.S. passport alone does not establish eligibility. Obtain treaty-residence evidence and review the specific income and exceptions before claiming.IRS: U.S.–China treaty, Articles 4 and 9–11
Standalone conversion taxThe blanket “neither China nor the U.S.” zero-tax statement in that summary is not established by the cited current-account paperwork rule. That rule describes documents, not all possible taxes, income types, local charges or U.S. exchange gains. Leave any personalized tax amount unverified; investigate the underlying income and currency basis.SAFE Tianjin, Article 59 explanation; IRS Publication 525, foreign currency transactions
Personal FX gainIRS describes a $200 exception for a personal foreign currency transaction and capital-gain reporting above it. Do not apply this mechanically to all business or investment transactions; classify the transaction and reconstruct dollar basis and acquisition dates.IRS Publication 525; 26 U.S.C. § 988
Conversion versus quotaFor a foreign individual’s lawful current-account RMB income, SAFE describes identity and transaction-amount source documents including tax documentation. Foreign permanent residence card holders have access to the annual facilitation allowance. This is not proof that every American, source of funds, or transaction qualifies. Ask the bank which category and evidence apply.SAFE Tianjin 2026; SAFE Tianjin 2024
Quota splittingDo not borrow other people’s annual allowances to evade controls. A legitimate agent handling the owner’s documented transaction is distinct from using another person’s quota; bank acceptance and documentary requirements remain to be confirmed.SAFE Xiamen guidance; SAFE Inner Mongolia Q&A
Bank charges and cost ladderA published sender fee is not the total cost. Actual FX rate, eligibility, intermediary and recipient deductions, tax, and timing remain unverified for this account and each tier. Obtain an executable written quote with the exact CNY debit and USD credited, fee option and expiration. Do not treat illustrative figures as personal quotes.CFPB: sending money abroad
Past noncomplianceStreamlined foreign procedures require non-willful conduct and applicable non-residency among other conditions. The existence of a late FBAR alone does not prove eligibility, and relief is not a blanket amnesty. Review the actual filing history and procedure with counsel.IRS streamlined foreign procedures; IRS FBAR
