Executive conclusion

The safest first path remains a licensed-bank conversion and outward remittance supported by proof of ownership, lawful source of funds, and applicable PRC tax records. A transfer of already-owned principal is not automatically a new U.S. income-tax event, but underlying income, foreign-currency gain, FBAR/Form 8938 reporting, residence classification, and PRC eligibility are separate questions. Compare executable bank quotes only after a bank confirms the documentation and route that apply.

No lawful “tax bypass” was verified. Splitting transactions to evade controls, nominee transfers, false trade documents, undeclared physical cash, and unlicensed underground banks are not recommended. Review the published irregularities before acting.
