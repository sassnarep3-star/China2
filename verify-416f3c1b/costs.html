How the published fee is calculated

HSBC China's tariff sets a telegraphic transfer at 0.1% of the remittance amount, minimum CNY 100, maximum CNY 500 per transaction, plus a CNY 120 cable charge, for Premier and Advance customers; Private Banking individual customers are charged nothing for issuance. Figures below convert CNY at the IRS 2025 yearly average of 7.129 CNY per USD. That rate is a labelled reference conversion, not an executable rate S24 S05.

Worked example, USD 10,000: 10,000 × 7.129 = CNY 71,290. 0.1% = CNY 71.29, which is below the floor, so CNY 100 applies. Add CNY 120 cable = CNY 220. Divide by 7.129 = $30.86.

Worked example, USD 400,000: 400,000 × 7.129 = CNY 2,851,600. 0.1% = CNY 2,851.60, which is above the cap, so CNY 500 applies. Add CNY 120 cable = CNY 620. Divide by 7.129 = $86.97.

What is missing from every number above

  • FX spread. The bank buys your renminbi and sells you dollars at prices it sets. The tariff expressly excludes other institutions' charges and any duties, levies or taxes S24.
  • Correspondent and receiving-bank fees. Never in the sender's tariff; only in an executable quote.
  • Optional "Pay Exact". CNY 200 equivalent if you want the beneficiary to receive the full amount S24.
  • PRC income tax on the money when it was earned. Separate question, unknown.
  • U.S. tax and penalties. See Tax & penalties.
  • Professional fees for notarisation, translation, probate or advice.
