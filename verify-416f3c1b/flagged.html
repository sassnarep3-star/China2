G-06 The emigrant-transfer instalment rules conflict across published texts

Art. 6 of the 2004 Provisional Measures (S17, S18) describes one application with staged remittance for amounts above RMB 200,000, while Huifa [2014] No. 2 (S16) section VI(II) cancels the requirement that an immigrant's property be transferred several times. Both texts are still published. Branch practice therefore cannot be verified from the sources alone.

What this blocks: Affects: R03 remittance timing. Amount and eligibility unaffected.

Findings that were verified

F-01

There is no separate PRC tax on exchanging CNY for USD. The Individual Income Tax Law taxes nine enumerated categories of income; disposing of your own currency is not one of them.

S19

F-03

The U.S. does not tax the act of converting. A personal conversion produces, at most, a section 988 exchange gain, and no gain is recognised unless it exceeds USD 200.

S06

F-04

At the IRS's own yearly averages, RMB acquired in 2021-2024 and converted at the 2025 average shows an exchange LOSS for 2021/2022/2023 and a small gain only for 2024.

S05 S06

F-05

The USD 50,000 annual facilitation quota is not a general entitlement for a foreign individual. It applies ID-only to domestic individuals, and to a foreign individual only if they hold a Foreigner's Permanent Residence ID card.

S02 S04

F-06

For documented lawful current-account income, SAFE's published position requires ID plus source-of-funds evidence including tax vouchers - not a fixed dollar ceiling.

S02 S03

F-07

Published bank fees are small relative to the balance: at HSBC Premier/Advance a telegraphic transfer costs 0.1% with a CNY 500 cap plus a CNY 120 cable - about USD 30.86 on USD 10,000-equivalent and USD 86.97 on USD 400,000-equivalent.

S24 S05

F-08

The dominant penalty risk is not a conversion tax, it is non-compliance: FBAR non-willful maximum USD 16,536 and willful maximum USD 165,353 per violation, inflation-adjusted.

S08 S09

F-09

Cash or card cannot move USD 400,000: card cash withdrawals are capped at RMB 100,000 per person per calendar year and cash carried out is capped at USD 10,000 equivalent per exit.

S21 S14

F-10

Crypto and underground-bank conversions are prohibited in China, so any 'cheap' quote from those channels is the price of an unlawful transaction.

S23 S01

F-11

A foreign citizen who never held Chinese household registration has no emigrant-property-transfer route, and the emigrant route requires proof that Chinese residence was cancelled.
