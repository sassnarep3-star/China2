The conversion penalty, decomposed (5 layers)

  1. Chinese income/profit tax on the source — usually already suffered; not a “conversion” cost. IIT 3–45% / 20% flat; EIT 25% (company). IIT-1 EIT-1
  2. FX spread at conversion — unpublished; your real negotiation lever. Illustrative sensitivity on page 2.
  3. Fees — capped wire (≈$33–$93) + cable; receiving $15 (Chase example); Pay Exact +CNY200. HSBC-1
  4. Time/quota cost — USD 50,000/yr ID-only; documented routes uncap the wait. SAFE-1
  5. Failure/reporting exposure — FBAR $16,536/$165,353 (2025); CMIR; FX-regulation penalties; criminal law for unlawful routes. ECFR-1 CBP-1 PBOC-1
