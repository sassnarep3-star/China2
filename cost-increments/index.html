The true cost of getting CNY out of China ($10,000 → $400,000)

For a U.S. citizen holding CNY in a mainland China bank account. Most important findings first; every figure is either tied to a numbered official source (source registry), clearly labeled an estimate/analysis, or left blank as unverified.

The 60-second answer

  1. There is no “conversion tax” in China. SAFE’s individual regime imposes an annual USD 50,000 per person facilitation quota (valid ID, processed via the bank; authenticity review above it) — it is a documentation/administrative ceiling, not a percentage tax. SAFE-1
  2. The real variable cost is the bank FX spread — and it is not published anywhere official. Every “all-in 2% fee” quote must be tested: ask each bank for its executable CNY→USD quote and compare final USD credited. Get at least three written quotes.
  3. Bank wire fees are tiny and capped (HSBC Premier/Advance example: 0.1% min CNY100 / max CNY500 + CNY120 cable ≈ US$33–$93 per transfer; Private Banking individuals: Free). On $400,000 that is ~0.03% HSBC-1
  4. US tax on the conversion itself is usually $0. §988 does not apply to personal transactions; personal FX gain is not recognized at all unless it exceeds $200 per transaction. Business/investment FX gain is ordinary income. USC988
  5. The expensive mistakes are all on the compliance side: splitting quotas, underground banking, fake invoices, hidden crypto moves. FBAR penalties alone (2025 maxima): non-willful $16,536 / willful $165,353. ECFR-1
  6. Above $50,000/year, lawful headroom exists only with documents: after-tax employment income remittances (tax certificates), genuine trade FX settlement accounts, emigrant/inheritor approvals, or corporate dividends (10% treaty WHT). Time cost: 8 years at quota alone for $400,000.

Why the penalty feels big — and where it actually comes from

1 · Source-income tax (China)

IIT on salary/services (3–45% progressive, RMB 60,000 basic deduction) or 20% flat on interest/dividends/leases/asset transfers. Paid when income arose — usually already paid before conversion. IIT-1

2 · FX spread (the real cost)

Difference between the interbank rate and your executable rate. Not published. On $400,000 even 1% = $4,000 — larger than every wire fee above combined. Negotiate or walk away from bad quotes.

3 · Transfer & cable fees

Capped: 0.1% (min CNY100/max CNY500) + CNY120 cable (HSBC Premier/Advance). $33–$108 per transfer. Pay Exact +CNY200 guarantees beneficiary gets full amount. HSBC-1

4 · Quota / time cost

USD 50,000 per person per calendar year with ID only. $400,000 takes 8 years at quota alone; documented routes compress this legally. SAFE-1

5 · US currency-gain tax

§988(e)(1): personal transactions are outside §988. (e)(2): no gain recognized unless gain > $200 per transaction. Business/investment FX gain = ordinary income. USC988

6 · Reporting penalties

FBAR if aggregate foreign accounts > $10,000 any day (FinCEN 114). 2025 maxima: $16,536 (non-willful) / $165,353 (willful). CMIR > $10,000 physical. Streamlined procedures may cure past misses. ECFR-1 FBAR-1 CBP-1
