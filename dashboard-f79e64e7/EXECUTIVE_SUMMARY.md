Sources read 2026-11-11. Research, not tax or legal advice. Full site: [index.html](index.html).

## Bottom line

1. No tax on the act of converting RMB to dollars was found in the PRC exchange rules read. Banks convert a foreigner's lawful China income on ID plus papers showing the amount, including tax vouchers ([SAFE](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html)).
2. The tax that matters is PRC income tax on how the money was earned: 3%–45% on wages, 20% on dividends, rent and asset sales, none at present on bank interest ([tax law](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html)). If it was withheld already, converting adds none.
3. Cheapest lawful route: one documented bank wire. At Bank of China's posted rate (¥6.6911 per $1, 11 Nov 2026 10:25 Beijing) and fee table it costs $53.42 for $10,000, $271.26 for $100,000 and $901.20 for $400,000, before other banks' fees ([rate](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/enindex_1619.html), [fees](https://www.boc.cn/ebanking/service/cs1/200905/t20090506_990712.html)).
4. The U.S. taxes worldwide income when earned and credits PRC tax on it; currency gain above $200 on conversion is recognised ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/us-citizens-and-resident-aliens-abroad), [foreign tax credit](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-tax-credit), [26 U.S.C. 988(e)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm)).
5. The large U.S. penalties are for missed FBAR, Form 8938 and Form 3520 filings, not for the transfer.

## Still unknown

- Where did the money come from? List each deposit from the bank statements and match it to a contract, payslip, sale, will or exchange memo.
- Was PRC tax already paid on it, and can you prove it? Request your tax payment record from the PRC tax authority for each year the income was earned; ask the employer for withholding records.
- What exactly did the bank say the tax is? Ask the branch to state in writing the tax type, taxpayer, amount and legal basis, or the document it wants.
- How does SAFE classify you? Check your residence permit history and ask the bank which category its system applies to your ID.
- Whose name is on the account? Read the account opening papers and statements.
- How much is there and how soon do you need it? Take the balance from the bank app and use the cost tables to see how long each route takes.
