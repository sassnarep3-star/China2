Currency gain: a worked illustration

RMB 669,110.00 buys $100,000 at today's Bank of China rate. If that RMB was received in an earlier year, its dollar value then was lower when the yuan was weaker. The difference is the gain. How to keep the total lowest

  1. Prove the PRC tax already paid rather than paying again: collect the tax records for the years the income was earned. S01
  2. Use one documented bank wire instead of many small transfers; the fee is capped per wire. S18
  3. Claim PRC income tax as a foreign tax credit on the U.S. return for the year the income was earned. S34 S28
  4. Record the dollar value of each deposit on its date so the currency gain is measured, not guessed. S21 S29
  5. File any missing FBAR, Form 8938 or Form 3520 before the money moves; the streamlined procedures cap the cost for non-wilful cases. S22 S24 S25 S31 S33
  6. Stay away from the prohibited routes: a 30% fine is more than fifty times the lawful cost. S06
