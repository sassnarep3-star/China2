Questions to answer before deciding

No route or tax figure is personal until these are answered. Each has what the sources say, what is missing and how to get it.

QuestionWhat we knowWhat we do not knowHow to find outSources
Where did the money come from?The route and the PRC tax both depend on the source: wages, business, a home sale, inheritance, a gift, a company, or dollars you brought in.The source of this balance.List each deposit from the bank statements and match it to a contract, payslip, sale, will or exchange memo.S01 S14
Was PRC tax already paid on it, and can you prove it?Banks convert a foreign individual's income on source papers that include tax vouchers.Whether tax was withheld and whether you hold the records.Request your tax payment record from the PRC tax authority for each year the income was earned; ask the employer for withholding records.S01 S14
What exactly did the bank say the tax is?No tax on converting was found in the rules read. A bank asking for tax proof is not the same as a bank charging tax.Whether the bank named a tax, an amount and a legal basis, or asked for a document.Ask the branch to state in writing the tax type, taxpayer, amount and legal basis, or the document it wants.S06 S10
How does SAFE classify you?A foreigner who has lived in China continuously for one year is a domestic individual under the Regulations; only Foreign Permanent Resident ID holders among foreigners get the USD 50,000 quota.Your ID type, residence history and whether you hold a permanent resident card.Check your residence permit history and ask the bank which category its system applies to your ID.S06 S02 S07
Whose name is on the account?Routing your money through other people's quotas is prohibited; a relative's own money given as a gift is a different case.Whether the account is yours, a spouse's, a relative's or a company's.Read the account opening papers and statements.S08 S09
How much is there and how soon do you need it?One documented wire can move any documented amount; the quota, ATM and cash routes are capped per year or per trip.The balance in RMB and your deadline.Take the balance from the bank app and use the cost tables to see how long each route takes.S07 S04 S05
Can you go to the branch, or will someone act for you?Purchases by foreign individuals and above-quota purchases can be delegated with the supporting documents; a home-sale agent needs a notarised power of attorney.Whether you are in China and who could act for you.Ask the branch for its agent requirements and whether a consular notarisation is accepted.S07 S13
Did you report the income on your U.S. returns when it was earned?U.S. citizens are taxed on worldwide income in the year earned; PRC tax on it can be credited.Whether past U.S. returns included this income.Compare your U.S. returns for those years with the PRC payslips and tax records.S28 S34 S27
Were FBAR and Form 8938 filed for every year the account was over the thresholds?FBAR applies above USD 10,000 in total; Form 8938 starts at USD 50,000 for a single filer in the U.S.Your filing history.Check your FinCEN filing confirmations and the Forms 8938 attached to past returns; if any are missing, look at the streamlined procedures before moving money.S22 S24 S31 S33
When did you receive the RMB, and at what exchange rates?Currency gain above USD 200 is recognised when you convert; the yuan is stronger now than the 2023 to 2025 averages.The dates and dollar value of each deposit.For each deposit, record the date and the exchange rate that day; any posted rate used consistently is accepted.S21 S29
What will your bank actually charge?Bank of China's posted rate and fee table give about 0.2% to 0.5% all told; other banks differ.Your bank, your tier, and what intermediary and receiving banks will deduct.Ask for a written quote showing the rate, the commission, the cable fee and whether intermediary charges are deducted; ask your U.S. bank for its incoming wire fee.S17 S18
Is the money an inheritance, a gift, or the proceeds of a home?Each has its own route and papers. Inheritance and emigrant transfers need SAFE approval and can now go in one payment. Gifts or bequests above USD 100,000 need Form 3520.Whether any of these apply and what documents exist.Collect the will or inheritance notarisation, gift evidence or sale contract and tax papers.S11 S32 S13 S25
Which U.S. state do you live in?Nothing was researched today on state income tax.Your state and its treatment of foreign income and currency gain.Check your state revenue department's guidance or ask a preparer; federal rules are linked here.S28
