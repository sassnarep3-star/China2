How the estimate is made

For each amount, the cost is the bank's exchange spread plus its wire fees, using only prices the bank publishes. The spread is half the gap between the posted buying and selling rates. Fees taken by intermediary and receiving banks are not published, so they are left out and flagged, as is any tax. Where a provider publishes no price, the cost is a dash and only the number of transfers or the time is shown.
