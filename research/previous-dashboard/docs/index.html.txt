<h2>Executive Summary</h2>
<p>China controls capital-account outflows via SAFE; current-account income (salary, trade, dividends after tax) is convertible with documentation. A US citizen must also satisfy US worldwide-income, FBAR and FATCA reporting regardless of where money sits. The cheapest lawful path is almost always: (1) prove China tax was paid on the income, (2) convert at your Chinese bank at its quoted rate, (3) SWIFT-wire to your own US account, (4) report correctly in the US and claim Foreign Tax Credit where eligible. Avoid any scheme promising to bypass SAFE — splitting quotas, underground banks and fake invoices are illegal in China and can create US AML/structuring liability.</p>
