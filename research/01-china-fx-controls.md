# 1. China's Foreign Exchange Control Framework

**Research date:** 2026-11-07 · **Scope:** the rules that govern how a US citizen can lawfully move CNY held in a mainland Chinese bank account into USD and out of China.

> Every figure below was read from the official source linked in the Source column. Cells left blank are unverified and appear on the site's **Flagged for Review** page.

---

## 1.1 The individual FX purchase quota

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Annual FX purchase quota per individual (conversion of CNY into foreign currency under the current account) | USD 50,000 per person per year | [SAFE CN-1](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2007/0105/819.html) |
| Documentation required **within** the quota | Valid ID only | [SAFE CN-1](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2007/0105/819.html) |
| Documentation required **beyond** the quota | ID plus documents proving the transaction is genuine | [SAFE CN-1](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2007/0105/819.html) · [SAFE CN-3](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2015/1111/1175.html) |
| Is beyond-quota conversion prohibited? | No — it is permitted where the underlying transaction is genuine and documented | [SAFE CN-3](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2015/1111/1175.html) |
| The quota is an aggregate across all banks for the same individual | Confirmed by SAFE (the quota attaches to the individual, not the account) | [SAFE CN-2](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2017/0119/1244.html) |

**Reading of the rule.** The USD 50,000 figure is a *documentation* threshold, not a hard legal ceiling on getting money out. SAFE states explicitly that conversion beyond the quota can be done "with valid identity documents and supporting documents proving the authenticity of the transaction". The tax question and the paperwork question are therefore separable.

## 1.2 Consequences of violating the quota rules ("splitting" / borrowed quotas)

| Consequence | Source |
|---|---|

## 1.3 Administrative and criminal penalties under the FX Administration Regulations

PBOC's Regulations of the PRC on Foreign Exchange Administration (2008 revised), English text:

| Article | Conduct | Penalty | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Art. 39 | FX evasion (e.g. false documents, disguised remittance) | Repatriation of the FX, plus a fine of up to 30% of the evaded amount; 30–100% where the case is serious; criminal liability where a crime is constituted | [PBOC CN-9](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/english/130721/2025080815063231112/index.html) |
| Art. 40 | Illegal FX arbitrage | Fine of up to 30%; 30–100% where serious; criminal liability where a crime is constituted | [PBOC CN-9](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/english/130721/2025080815063231112/index.html) |
| Art. 42 | Illegally carrying FX into or out of China | Warning plus a fine of up to 20% | [PBOC CN-9](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/english/130721/2025080815063231112/index.html) |
| Art. 45 | Unauthorised or disguised FX trading | Warning, confiscation of illegal gains, fine of up to 30%; 30–100% where serious; criminal liability where a crime is constituted | [PBOC CN-9](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/english/130721/2025080815063231112/index.html) |
| Art. 46 | Conducting FX business without a licence | Confiscation of illegal gains plus a fine of 1–5× the illegal gains where gains exceed RMB 500,000, otherwise a fine of RMB 500,000–2,000,000 | [PBOC CN-9](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/english/130721/2025080815063231112/index.html) |

## 1.4 Underground banking ("对敲" / duiqiao) — a criminal offence

SPC/SPP Interpretation Fa Shi [2019] No. 1 (effective 1 February 2019) treats disguised cross-border FX trading as the crime of **illegal business operation** under Criminal Law art. 225(4):

| Threshold | Amount | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "Serious" case — starting point | RMB 5,000,000 in disguised FX transactions, **or** illegal gains of RMB 100,000 | [SPC CR-1](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/141562.html) |
| "Serious" case — with aggravating factors | RMB 2,500,000 / RMB 50,000 gains plus any of: prior conviction, administrative penalty within the past 2 years, refusing to disclose funds, other serious consequences | [SPC CR-1](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/141562.html) |
| "Especially serious" case — starting point | RMB 25,000,000, **or** illegal gains of RMB 500,000 | [SPC CR-1](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/141562.html) |
| "Especially serious" case — with aggravating factors | RMB 12,500,000 / RMB 250,000 gains plus aggravating factors | [SPC CR-1](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/141562.html) |
| Fine | 1–5× illegal gains; where gains cannot be determined, deemed to be 1/1000 of the transaction amount | [SPC CR-1](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/141562.html) |
| Custodial sentence | Up to 5 years for a serious case; 5 years or more where especially serious | [SPC CR-1](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/141562.html) |
| Concurrency | Where the conduct also constitutes money laundering or terrorist financing, the heavier penalty applies | [SPC CR-1](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/141562.html) |
| Entities | The entity is fined and the persons directly responsible are prosecuted | [SPC CR-1](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/141562.html) |

## 1.5 Virtual currency — prohibited as a conversion channel

The September 2021 PBOC notice (jointly issued with nine other agencies, including the SPC and SPP) declares that **all virtual-currency-related business activities are illegal financial activities**, and that overseas exchanges providing services to mainland residents are likewise illegal, with criminal liability pursued.

[PBOC CR-2](http://www.pbc.gov.cn/goutongjiaoliu/113456/113469/4348521/index.html)

## 1.6 Overseas cash withdrawals with a mainland bank card

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Aggregate overseas ATM withdrawals per person per calendar year (all cards combined) | RMB 100,000 | [SAFE CN-5](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2017/1230/1397.html) |
| Overseas ATM withdrawal limit per card per day | RMB 10,000 | [SAFE CN-5](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2017/1230/1397.html) |
| Consequence of exceeding the annual quota | Card is barred from overseas withdrawals for the current and the following year | [SAFE CN-5](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2017/1230/1397.html) |
| Borrowing or lending cards to circumvent the limits | Prohibited | [SAFE CN-5](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2017/1230/1397.html) |
| Relationship to the USD 50,000 purchase quota | Separate and independent — the ATM limit does not consume the purchase quota | [SAFE CN-5](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2017/1230/1397.html) |

## 1.7 Outward remittance of company profits — a current-account item

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Outward remittances of profits from direct investment are recorded under the **current account** | [SAFE CN-4](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2017/0126/1248.html) |
| A domestic institution only needs to present evidencing materials and can complete the outward remittance directly with the bank, without quota, provided the profits are genuine and compliant | [SAFE CN-4](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2017/0126/1248.html) |

This is a decisive point for the company-side routes: **China does not impose an FX quota on repatriating dividends.** The cost of a dividend route is tax, not FX permission.
