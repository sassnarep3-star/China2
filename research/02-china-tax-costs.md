## 2.4 Company Law constraint on dividend distribution

When a company distributes after-tax profit it must first appropriate 10% of the profit to the **statutory surplus reserve**, until that reserve reaches 50% of registered capital; prior-year losses must be made up first. Only the residual after-tax profit is distributable.

|---|---|---|

## 2.5 The arithmetic of a dividend repatriation

Using only officially verified rates (EIT 25%, treaty-capped WHT 10%):

```
Profit in China                          100.00
  less EIT at 25%                        (25.00)      <- STA EIT Law Art. 4
                                         -------
After-tax profit distributable            75.00
  less WHT at 10% (treaty cap, Art. 9(2)) ( 7.50)      <- IRS treaty PDF
                                         -------
Cash received abroad                      67.50
Effective combined leakage                32.50%
```

If instead the 20% statutory domestic WHT rate applied (no treaty benefit claimed), leakage on the second step would be RMB 15 and the effective total 40.00%. Claiming treaty benefits therefore saves 7.50 points of leakage.

*Arithmetic is a calculation from the cited rates, not a claim sourced from any single document.*
