# 4. US–China Tax Treaty: Verified Rates

**Source for every rate in this file:** the official IRS-hosted text of the Agreement between the United States of America and the People's Republic of China for the Avoidance of Double Taxation and the Prevention of Tax Evasion with Respect to Taxes on Income, signed at Beijing 30 April 1984, in force 1 January 1987, together with Protocol 1 (30 April 1984), Protocol 2 (10 May 1986) and the exchange of notes.

[IRS — official treaty text](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/china.pdf)

| Item | Value | Treaty provision | Verified in official text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maximum rate on dividends (beneficial owner) | 10% of the gross amount | Art. 9(2) | Yes |
| Maximum rate on interest (beneficial owner) | 10% of the gross amount | Art. 10(2) | Yes |
| Maximum rate on royalties (beneficial owner) | 10% of the gross amount | Art. 11(2) | Yes |
| Royalties on rental of industrial, commercial or scientific equipment | Tax imposed on 70% of the gross amount, i.e. an effective 7% | Protocol 1, para. 6 | Yes |
| Dividends/interest/royalties payable to a resident carrying on business in the source state through a PE | Art. 9(2)/10(2)/11(2) do not apply; Art. 7 (business profits) applies instead | Arts. 9(4), 10(5), 11(4) | Yes |
| Anti-treaty-shopping: competent authorities may deny Arts. 9, 10, 11 benefits to third-country conduit companies | Yes | Protocol 1, para. 7 | Yes |
| US relief for China tax paid by a US citizen or resident | Credit against US tax on that income | Art. 22(2) | Yes |
| Saving clause | "Notwithstanding any provision of the Agreement, the United States may tax its citizens." | Protocol 1, para. 2 | Yes |
| Tax-sparing credit | **Not** provided at this time | Exchange of notes | Yes |
| Business profits taxable only in the residence state absent a PE | Yes | Art. 7(1) | Yes |
| Students and trainees: personal-service income exempt up to USD 5,000 per taxable year | Yes | Art. 20(c) | Yes |
| Teachers, professors and researchers: exempt for up to three years in the aggregate | Yes | Art. 19 | Yes |
| Mutual agreement procedure: case must be presented within three years of first notification | Yes | Art. 24(1) | Yes |
| Agreement remains in force indefinitely; termination on notice on or before 30 June in any year after five years | Yes | Art. 28 | Yes |

## Not in the treaty

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Totalization / social security agreement between the US and China | **No such agreement exists.** Nothing in the treaty text addresses social security coordination. *(Existence is negative and can only be established from the absence of a provision in the official text and from the absence of any such instrument in the IRS treaty collection — the IRS treaty PDF contains no totalization protocol.)* |
| Exemption for capital gains on the disposal of personal property | The treaty has no capital-gains article for personal property; Art. 12 (Gains) covers real property and other cases only |
