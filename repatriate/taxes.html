How the layers stack (analysis)

  1. Source tax (PRC): owed when the income arose. If unpaid, resolving it is a prerequisite for the documented conversion route — not a penalty for converting. S15 ↗ S03 ↗
  2. US residual tax: worldwide income, reduced by FEIE (earned only) and/or limited FTC. Often small or zero for already-taxed salary, but never assume — the FTC is ratio-limited. S16 ↗ S20 ↗ S21 ↗
  3. FX spread: the bank’s real conversion margin; unpublished → blank everywhere on this site. F03
  4. Transfer fees: published tariff floors computed on Cost tables. S29 ↗
  5. Reporting penalties: avoidable entirely by filing (FBAR/8938/105) and, for past non-willful gaps, the streamlined procedures. S17 ↗ S18 ↗ S28 ↗ S27 ↗
