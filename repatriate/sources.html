S15Individual Income Tax Law of the PRC (7th amendment 2018-08-31, official English)
State Taxation Administration法规库 (fgk.chinatax.gov.cn) · statute · locator: Arts. 1-3, 6-11		fetchedResident individual: domiciled in China or >=183 days in tax year; residents taxed on China and overseas income; non-residents on China-source income.
