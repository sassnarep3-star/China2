Executive summary — getting money out of China with the least lawful cost

The premise matters: neither the US nor China taxes the act of converting CNY to USD. What people call a “conversion penalty” is really up to five separate things: (1) PRC income tax on the money’s source, (2) US tax on worldwide income (with credits/exclusion), (3) the bank’s FX spread, (4) transfer fees, (5) reporting penalties if accounts were never declared. Only #3–#4 are costs of moving the money itself. S15 ↗ S16 ↗ S24 ↗ S13 ↗

Best first move for a stuck bank balance

If the balance is documented, taxed China-source income (salary/services), SAFE’s 2026 guidance lets a foreign individual convert and remit it with ID + income/tax documents — no annual dollar cap is stated for that route. The famous $50,000/year quota is a separate facilitation channel, not the ceiling. S03 ↗ S05 ↗ S01 ↗

If the source income was never taxed, the tax must be resolved first — that is income tax under the IIT Law (3–45% progressive / 20% flat categories), not a conversion tax. S15 ↗

What it costs (published parts only)

HSBC China’s published tariff: 0.1% (min ¥100, max ¥500) + ¥120 cable per wire for Premier/Advance; free for Private Banking individuals; Global Transfer to your own overseas HSBC free for Premier/PB. The FX spread and correspondent fees are not published — left blank and flagged, never guessed. S29 ↗ F03

US side: the real penalty risk is reporting

FBAR required if all foreign accounts combined exceeded $10,000 at any time; Form 8938 thresholds are higher. Penalties (2025-adjusted): up to $16,536 non-willful per violation; willful up to $165,353 or 50% of the balance. Non-willful catch-up: Streamlined Foreign Offshore Procedures (3 returns + 6 FBARs, penalties waived if compliant). S17 ↗ S18 ↗ S19 ↗ S27 ↗

Do not use

Crypto bridges (illegal financial activity in China, 2026 notice), quota splitting, underground banks, fake contracts: fines up to 30% of the amount, serious cases 30–100%, plus confiscation and criminal liability. S14 ↗ S13 ↗ S06 ↗
