IR-11 gap The USD 50,000 annual quota is described inconsistently: SAFE's rule text ties it to settlement and to a domestic individual's purchase, while bank and third-party guides describe it as a general per-person ceiling. Hui Fa [2007] No. 1 Art. 2 (verified, V04): '对个人结汇和境内个人购汇实行年度总额管理…每人每年等值5万美元'. The earlier HSBC guide and several pages in this repository treat USD 50,000 as a blanket limit. Material to route choice: if the balance is lawful income of an overseas individual, the documentary route (not the quota) governs. This remains unconfirmed for any specific bank in writing; carried forward as an open flag. SAFE text re-read 2026-11-11. Bank practice not tested and cannot be tested without the account holder. open ↗

How the audit was run

  1. Enumerate, do not assume. Every directory with HTML was counted, every claim-count phrase on each landing page was extracted with a pattern scan, and the numbers were compared rather than eyeballed.
  2. Recompute money. Fees, caps and year counts were recomputed from the tariff text in build.py and re-derived independently in verify.py.
  3. Check links locally. Every internal href/src path on the three generated pages was resolved against the filesystem, and the whole tree was re-scanned for broken internal links (111 HTML pages, 0 broken on 2026-11-11).
  4. Read the corrections. Where a page corrects an earlier figure (the HSBC USD 7 wording), the occurrence list was generated so the correction itself is auditable.
  5. State the boundary. A structural scan does not prove a claim is unsupported, and a retrieval does not prove a rule applies to you.

Raw data: data/irregularities.json. Related: the verification ledger, the transfer guide’s own route audit and its flags.
