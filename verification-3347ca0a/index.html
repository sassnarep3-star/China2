Method, and its limits

What this pass did. Re-fetched 22 documents; extracted the sentence that a published claim depends on; recomputed every fee in renminbi and dollars from the tariff text rather than from another page; re-derived the CMS-style tier arithmetic, the ATM year count and both rate bases.

What it did not do. It did not contact a bank, did not obtain an executable quote, did not read any private account document, and did not obtain professional tax advice. Retrieval proves a sentence is on the page today; it does not prove the rule applies to any particular person, and sites change.

Why two rate bases appear. U.S. tax translation uses the IRS 2025 yearly average of 7.129 CNY/USD (V18). Cost illustration here uses the Bank of China board of 2026-11-11: 6.6911 CNY/USD (V20). Mixing them silently is one of the repository irregularities this pass found (IR-05).
