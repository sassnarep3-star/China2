EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Move funds lawfully.
Separate tax from conversion.

There is no verified universal “tax penalty” for converting CNY to USD. The most practical first step is a written review by the account-holding bank of the money’s source, the account holder’s SAFE classification, and the documentation needed for a direct bank conversion and remittance.
