01 / 04

If this is lawful salary or other qualifying current-account income of an overseas individual, the strongest first route to investigate is a direct bank conversion and wire using identity, source and tax documents. This is conditional—not a determination that the funds qualify or that no tax is owed.

A tax certificate may establish compliance with tax on the underlying income; it does not by itself prove a new tax is imposed on converting the balance. Compare the bank’s all-in USD proceeds, not just its quoted rate. SAFE salary guidance ↗
