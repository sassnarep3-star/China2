Start with the source of funds and tax evidence.

A perceived “conversion penalty” can involve several different things: tax on the underlying income or asset, a bank’s document review, exchange-rate cost and transfer charges, possible U.S. currency gain, and U.S. reporting duties. They have different fixes and different sources. This desk prices what is published, identifies its assumptions, and leaves blank what is not published.

Answer first

If the RMB is already-taxed salary

One documented bank conversion plus one wire is the route to assess first (M01). SAFE states there is no statutory time limit on remitting historic lawful salary [V09 ↗] and that a third party can run the formalities if you are abroad [V08 ↗]. What you must produce is the contract plus the tax record — that is the gate, not a tax.

The USD 50,000 figure is not a universal ceiling

The rule’s own words apply the annual total to settlement and to a domestic individual’s purchase (境内个人购汇) [V04 ↗]. Treating it as a blanket cap on an overseas individual’s documented income is not supported by that text — but bank practice varies and is unresolved here. Flag IR-11.

Cheaper routes exist; most are capped or closed

ATM cash is hard-capped at RMB 100,000 a calendar year [V19 ↗] — 27 years for $400,000 at the 2026-11-11 board (29 at the 7.129 tax rate). Wise is limited to CNY 300,000 per transfer and 3-year-old tax records [V14 ↗]. Physical cash hits a USD 10,000 declaration line every trip [V17 ↗].

Do not manufacture a route

Splitting across relatives’ quotas, underground swaps and crypto bridges are unlawful, and the statute prices them: fines of up to 30% of the amount, or 30%–100% in serious cases [V10 ↗]. Four of the 23 catalogued methods are rejected for this reason and are kept visible so they are not re-discovered as “ideas”.

The U.S. review includes tax, basis and reporting

A transfer of your own existing principal is not itself a new income category, but the underlying income and currency gain still require analysis. The personal-transaction exclusion in §988(e)(2) does not apply when the gain exceeds $200 [V15 ↗]. FBAR and Form 8938 are separate reporting tests; a foreign tax credit is limited and is not available for tax on excluded income.

How a $400,000 transfer actually works

  1. Establish what the money is. Pull the PRC individual income-tax payment record (个人所得税纳税记录) for every year that funded the balance, from the tax app or the tax office. This is the document the bank will ask for first. [V03 ↗]
  2. Match it to contracts and payroll evidence. Employment contract, payroll slips, and — for older income — anything showing the money was actually earned then. SAFE says there is no deadline on this [V09 ↗], so the constraint is your own paperwork, not a statute.
  3. Ask the bank, in writing, what it will accept. Request the refusal or the requirement in writing, naming the tax type, period, base and legal basis if a tax is asserted — and a separate all-in quote showing the applied rate, the commission, the cable, and any correspondent or receiving charge.
  4. Decide who executes. If you are outside China, SAFE’s published Q&A allows another person to run the remittance formalities [V08 ↗]; the bank will still want an authorisation it accepts.
  5. Convert at the counter, then remit. The conversion produces a memo (兑换水单) you must keep: it is the only thing that lets you reverse an unspent remainder later, and only for 24 months [V05 ↗].
  6. Price the wire two ways. Telegraphic transfer costs 0.1% (min CNY 100, max CNY 500) + CNY 120 cable for Premier/Advance, and $0.00 for Private Banking/Premier Elite individuals [V11 ↗]. “Pay Exact” (OUR) adds CNY 200 if you want the receiver credited in full.
  7. Check the U.S. side before the money lands. Principal is not income; personal FX gain above $200 is not de minimis [V15 ↗]; FBAR and Form 8938 depend on balances, not on transfers.
  8. File the reports the balance triggers. A closed account or a completed transfer does not erase an earlier reporting obligation for the year in which the threshold was crossed.
