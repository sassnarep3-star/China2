The bank's explanation matters. A requested tax certificate, an actual tax assessment, an FX eligibility question and a transfer fee are different things. This repository separates them; it does not assume that any private balance is taxable, already taxed, or eligible for a particular route.

What to ask the bank first

Request the refusal in writing. Ask it to identify the tax type (if any), taxpayer, period, tax base, amount and legal basis; distinguish a tax payment from a tax certificate or a bank fee; list acceptable source-of-funds evidence; confirm which FX category applies; and provide a current all-in quote showing the applied CNY/USD rate, sender fee, cable, correspondent/intermediary and recipient charges.
