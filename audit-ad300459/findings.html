#1 · V01PRC quota — what actually exists
V01 — PRC annual facilitation quota: each person may settle FX and each DOMESTIC individual may buy FX up to USD 50,000 equivalent per year with ID only; above-quota transactions need transaction documents.
VERIFIED · LIMITEDS06 · State Council of the PRC (State Council Gazette)
Hui Fa [2020] No. 14, Art. 54–55 (also Hui Fa [2007] No. 1, Art. 2)		Scope matters: the purchase side of the quota is for domestic individuals (境内个人). Overseas individuals purchase against income/source documents (see V03) unless they hold a foreigner permanent-residence ID (V04). Whether a US citizen is domestic or overseas depends on residence — see V02.
appears in: ladder/tax.html, repatriate/index.html, portal/tax-compliance.html, docs/routes.html
#2 · V02PRC classification of a US citizen
V02 — A foreign national who has been continuously resident in the PRC for one year or more counts as a domestic individual (境内个人) under PRC FX rules; below one year the person is an overseas individual (境外个人).
VERIFIEDS04 · State Council of the PRC (published via SAFE mirror)
State Council Order No. 532, Art. 52(2)		This classification decides which purchase/remittance rulebook applies (quota vs source-docs). The user's residence duration is unknown here, so no route is asserted as applicable to any private balance.

#3 · V03PRC route — documented income
V03 — Overseas individuals convert lawful RMB current-account income earned in China (e.g., employment pay) into FX at a bank with valid ID plus transaction-bearing source-of-funds materials that include a tax voucher (税务凭证); the bank then handles the outward current-account remittance under its document rules.
VERIFIEDS06 · State Council of the PRC (State Council Gazette)
Hui Fa [2020] No. 14, Art. 59 ¶1 (quoted verbatim by SAFE Tianjin S01; remittance side: Art. 65)		This is the central lawful 'get money out' path for documented income: FX purchase against proof of lawful income (incl. tax voucher), then account remittance with ID (Art. 65).

#5 · V05PRC outward remittance thresholds
V05 — Domestic individuals: FX-account remittance for current account up to USD 50,000 equivalent same day needs ID only (above: transaction documents); FX cash remittance up to USD 10,000 same day needs ID (above: customs-stamped declaration or original withdrawal slip plus documents). Overseas individuals: FX-account remittance needs valid ID; FX cash remittance up to USD 10,000 same day needs ID (above: customs-stamped declaration or original withdrawal slip).
VERIFIEDS06 · State Council of the PRC (State Council Gazette)
Hui Fa [2020] No. 14, Art. 64–65 (same structure in Hui Fa [2007] No. 1, Art. 14–15)		USD 50,000 here is a daily document-free threshold for outward remittance of FX already held — not a lifetime or annual cap on transfers, and not a tax.

#12 · V12PRC penalties for control evasion
V12 — PRC FX penalties (statutory maxima): evading FX controls (逃汇) — fine up to 30% of the amount, or 30% up to the equivalent amount if serious, criminal liability possible; illegal FX arbitrage via false documents (非法套汇) — same 30% structure; private/improper buying-selling or brokering FX in large amounts — warning, confiscation of gains, fine up to 30%, 30%+ if serious, criminal liability; operating settlement/sale FX business without approval — confiscation of gains plus 1–5× gains if gains ≥ CNY 500,000, or CNY 500,000–2,000,000 if no/low gains, license suspension/revocation, criminal liability; carrying FX across the border in violation — warning plus fine up to 20% of the amount.
VERIFIEDS04 · State Council of the PRC (published via SAFE mirror)
State Council Order No. 532, Art. 39, 40, 45, 46, 42		Arithmetic illustration only (not a forecast): a 30% fine on USD 400,000 is USD 120,000. These are maxima applied case by case; 2020 Guidelines violations are punished via the same Regulations (S06, Art. 188).

#18 · V18US currency-gain rule on personal conversions
V18 — Under 26 U.S.C. §988, gains or losses on foreign-currency transactions are ordinary income/loss, and disposing of nonfunctional currency (including bank deposits) is a §988 transaction — BUT for individuals, personal transactions are outside §988, and in a personal disposal no gain is recognized due to exchange-rate movement unless the gain would exceed $200.
