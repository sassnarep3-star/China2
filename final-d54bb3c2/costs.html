How each cell is calculated

Buying USD X at the bank's sell rate costs X × sell CNY. At the midpoint of the bank's buy and sell rates it would cost X × mid, so the spread cost in USD is X × (sell − mid) ÷ sell. The commission is the published percentage with its published floor and cap, plus the published cable charge, divided by the sell rate to express it in USD. Total = spread + fee. No correspondent charge is added, because neither bank publishes one.

Reading the numbers

  • Under USD 21,000 the HSBC Premier transfer is cheapest, because BOC's CNY 50 floor plus CNY 150 cable is a bigger drag than the wider HSBC spread at small size.
  • Above USD 21,000 the BOC wire is cheapest at every single step, because its commission stops at CNY 260 while HSBC's spread never stops.
  • Percentages fall as the amount rises for both banks: the capped fee is a smaller share, while the spread stays constant.
  • The Premier monthly fee of CNY 300 is excluded from the M03 column; see flagged item F-07.
