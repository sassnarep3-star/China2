Executive summary

One conclusion, the evidence for it, and the limits of what the evidence supports.

Conclusion

Move the money with a documented bank conversion and wire to your own US account, in the largest single transfer your documents support, and stop treating the conversion as the taxable event. On the rates read for this section that costs $49.79 on USD 10,000 and $898.75 on USD 400,000 (0.2247%). It is lawful, it uses only published prices, and it produces exactly the paper trail the US return needs.

Every alternative that looks cheap — a private swap, a quota split, a stablecoin leg — is priced in the penalty schedule, not in the fee schedule. At USD 400,000 the enforcement exposure is $120,000.00 to $400,000.00, against $898.75 for doing it properly.

Why the answer is not "pay the tax"

No page in the ledger imposes a tax, surcharge or levy on an individual's purchase of USD with CNY. The PRC rules that mention conversion are documentation and quota rules, and the enforcement provisions penalise evasion, arbitrage and unlawful trading — not lawful conversion [S08, S09, S19]. The US side taxes the currency gain, and excludes only the first USD 200 of a personal-transaction gain [S27]. So a lawful conversion is cheap, and the expensive outcomes in this problem come from how the money is moved, not from the fact that it is converted.

What the rules do constrain is volume and evidence: a domestic individual gets USD 50,000 a year without prior approval [S08 Art.2]; beyond that you need a documented China-source income stream [S10, S11] or an approved emigrant property transfer [S13, S14].

Action order

  1. Fix your US filing position first. FBAR above USD 10,000 aggregate [S21], Form 8938 at its own thresholds [S23]. A clean history costs nothing; a missed willful year is USD 165,353 [S22]. If years are missing, the streamlined procedures exist for non-willful cases [S30].
  2. Document the source of the balance. Match every large credit to a contract, payslip, deed or dividend notice, and obtain the tax record for each year [S14, S20].
  3. Pick the route by amount. Under about USD 21,000 the HSBC Premier transfer is cheapest in the model because BOC's minimum-fee and cable floor dominate; above that the BOC wire wins because its commission is capped [S03, S04].
  4. Send a small test transfer first and read the credited amount; correspondent charges are not published [S03, S04] and are flagged.
  5. Record the USD basis of every conversion at the rate used, so the section 988 gain is computed from evidence [S07, S27].

What this section cannot tell you

The model answers "what does a lawful conversion cost". It cannot answer "which route is available to you", because that depends on your nationality, hukou status, income history and filing record.
