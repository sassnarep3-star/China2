Questions that must be answered before choosing a route

Nothing on this site can choose a route for you until these questions are answered. Each one lists what is already known from official sources, what is missing, and a concrete way to obtain the missing fact. Questions are ordered by how much they change the answer.

All 15 questions

Q01 · Are you a Chinese national, a foreign national, or a former Chinese national who has taken US citizenship? Identity and residency

Why it matters. This single fact decides whether the USD 50,000 annual quota, the documented-income route, or the emigrant property transfer is even available.

What is already known. Domestic individuals get a USD 50,000 per year facilitation quota [S08 Art.2]. A foreign individual's China-source RMB income can be purchased with identity document plus source and tax documents [S08 Art.13, S10]; only permanent-residence holders get the annual quota [S11]. The emigrant route requires foreign permanent residence or citizenship plus cancelled mainland household registration [S14].

What is missing. Your current nationality, household registration status, and whether you ever held a mainland hukou.

How to find it. Check your passport, your 户口簿 or its cancellation stamp, and any 注销户口证明. If you naturalised in the US, the cancellation certificate is the document SAFE asks for [S14].

Sources: S08S10S11S14

Q02 · Where do you live for tax purposes now, and how many days were you in China this year? Identity and residency

Why it matters. China taxes residents on worldwide income after 183 days, and the US taxes citizens on worldwide income regardless of residence. The overlap decides whether a foreign tax credit is available and whether the foreign earned income exclusion applies.

What is already known. China: resident if domiciled or present 183+ days, taxed on worldwide income, with a credit for foreign tax paid [S20 Art.1, Art.7]. US: foreign earned income exclusion needs a foreign tax home plus bona fide residence or 330 days abroad [S25]; exclusion for 2027 is USD 137,300 [S26].

What is missing. Your day count, your tax home, and whether you claim the exclusion on Form 2555.

How to find it. Pull entry and exit records from both passports and count days; keep the count with your return file.

Sources: S20S25S26

Q03 · What is the exact balance, and how did each part of it arise? The money

Why it matters. Conversion cost is proportional to the balance, and the documentation you can produce depends on whether the money is salary, a property sale, a gift, or accumulated interest.

What is already known. Different document sets are prescribed for salary, business income, capital income, property transfer and incidental income, plus an original tax certificate [S14].

What is missing. Balance, currency composition, and a line-by-line origin for each deposit.

How to find it. Download 12-24 months of statements and tax payment records from the bank app; match each large credit to a contract, payslip, sale deed or dividend notice.

Sources: S14

Q04 · Was PRC individual income tax already paid on the income that produced this balance? The money

Why it matters. The conversion itself is not a taxable event, but unpaid PRC tax on the source income is the one thing that can turn a bank visit into a penalty case.

What is already known. Rates: comprehensive income 3%-45%, business income 5%-35%, interest, dividends, property transfer and incidental income 20% [S20 Art.3]. Late payment surcharge is 0.05% per day [S31 Art.32]. A taxpayer owing tax can be stopped from leaving China [S31 Art.44].

What is missing. Whether a 完税证明 exists for each income stream, and whether any year is unfiled.

How to find it. Request a 个人所得税纳税记录 from the 个人所得税 app or the district tax office for each year the balance was earned.

Sources: S20S31

Q05 · Have you filed FBARs and Forms 8938 for every year this account existed? US compliance

Why it matters. An unreported account is a far larger financial risk than the conversion spread: the penalty can exceed the entire cost of converting.

What is already known. FBAR required when aggregate foreign accounts exceed USD 10,000 at any point [S21]. Non-willful penalty USD 16,536; willful USD 165,353 [S22]. Form 8938 thresholds and a USD 10,000 penalty rising by up to USD 50,000 [S23]. Streamlined procedures exist for non-willful failures [S30].

What is missing. A year-by-year filing history and the peak balance in each year.

How to find it. Pull IRS account transcripts for the last six years and compare against bank year-end and peak balances.

Sources: S21S22S23S30

Q06 · What is your basis in the CNY, in USD? US compliance

Why it matters. The US taxes the currency gain on conversion, not the conversion itself. Without a documented USD basis you cannot compute the gain, and the exclusion is only USD 200.

What is already known. Disposing of nonfunctional currency is a section 988 transaction, gain is ordinary, and the personal-transaction exclusion covers only the first USD 200 of gain [S27]. The IRS publishes yearly average rates (China 2025: 7.129; 2024: 7.189) [S07].

What is missing. The USD value of the CNY at the time each deposit was received.

How to find it. Convert each deposit at the rate on the day received, or at the IRS yearly average if used consistently [S07], and keep the worksheet.

Sources: S07S27

Q07 · How much do you need to move, and by when? The money

Why it matters. Size decides which route is cheapest. Under about USD 21,000 the HSBC Premier route is cheapest in the model; above that the BOC wire is. Timing decides whether the USD 50,000 annual quota can be used across two calendar years.

What is already known. Cost rows are computed for every USD 10,000 increment to USD 400,000 and the cheapest priced route is marked in each row [model].

What is missing. Your required amount and deadline.

How to find it. Pick the row in the cost table that matches your amount; if the deadline allows, split across two calendar years to use two annual quotas [S08 Art.2].

Sources: S08

Q08 · Which banks do you actually hold accounts with, in China and in the US? Banking

Why it matters. The cheapest route in the model requires either a BOC mainland account or an HSBC pair, and HSBC Premier requires a CNY 500,000 relationship balance.

What is already known. BOC wire: 0.1% capped at CNY 260 plus CNY 150 cable [S03]. HSBC: 0.1% capped at CNY 500 plus CNY 120 cable, or free by Global Transfer for Premier [S04]. Premier carries CNY 300 per month below CNY 500,000 [S04].

What is missing. Your account list and current balances.

How to find it. Log into each bank and note account type, currency and balance; if you have HSBC in both countries, check whether you qualify for Premier.

Sources: S03S04

Q09 · What do the correspondent and receiving banks actually charge on your route? Cost detail

Why it matters. Both published fee schedules stop at the sending bank. The landing amount can be lower than the estimate.

What is already known. The BOC and HSBC pages read state the sending-bank fee only; correspondent and receiving charges are additional [S03, S04].

What is missing. A real remittance advice showing the amount credited.

How to find it. Send one small test transfer (for example USD 1,000) and read the credited amount; use the difference as the true all-in cost.

Sources: S03S04

Q10 · Does the bank apply the board rate or a different internal rate to this product? Cost detail

Why it matters. The cost table uses the published board rate. If a product applies a different rate, the spread changes and every row moves.

What is already known. Board rates are published and are stated to be for reference only; the actual transaction rate applies [S01]. The HSBC tariff does not state the conversion pricing for Global Transfer [S04].

What is missing. The rate actually applied on a Global Transfer or on a large wire.

How to find it. Ask the branch in writing for the rate applied to your product, or read the rate on the confirmation of a test transfer.

Sources: S01S04

Q11 · Will you be claiming the foreign earned income exclusion, or the foreign tax credit, or both? US compliance

Why it matters. You cannot take a credit for tax on income you excluded, so the choice changes the net cost of the whole exercise.

What is already known. The credit is only for foreign income, war profits and excess profits taxes, and no credit is allowed for tax on excluded income [S29]. Exclusion for 2026 is USD 132,900 and for 2027 USD 137,300 [S26].

What is missing. Your US taxable income and the PRC tax actually paid per year.

How to find it. Model both on Form 1116 and Form 2555 with your last return; the higher of the two results is the one to claim.

Sources: S26S29

Q12 · Is any part of the balance a gift from a relative, or an inheritance? The money

Why it matters. A gift is reported on Form 3520 above USD 100,000 but is generally not taxable, and it may not be convertible through the salary route.

What is already known. Form 3520 Part IV is required above USD 100,000 aggregate from a nonresident alien in a year, with each gift above USD 5,000 identified [S24].

What is missing. Whether any credit into the account was a gift, and from whom.

How to find it. Match large credits to gift deeds or transfer records; if none, treat as income and document it as such.

Sources: S24

Q13 · Has the account ever been flagged, restricted, or placed on a watch list? Banking

Why it matters. A prior flag changes what the bank will accept and can add two years of strict review.

What is already known. Individuals who borrow or lend quota, or split purchases, are placed on a watch list with strict review for two years [S15]. Exceeding the overseas cash withdrawal cap suspends the card function for two years [S16].

What is missing. Any notice from the bank or SAFE.

How to find it. Ask the bank for the account status in writing and keep the reply.

Sources: S15S16

Q14 · If the route needs an approval, how long will it take and what will it cost in professional time? Cost detail

Why it matters. The emigrant route has no SAFE fee but needs a full documentary chain, which usually means paid help.

What is already known. No fee is charged by SAFE; decision within 20 working days, extendable once by 10 [S14]. Filing is on site only [S14].

What is missing. Whether you qualify, and what a local agent or lawyer would charge.

How to find it. Ask the local SAFE branch and two local agents for a written quote and checklist before starting.

Sources: S14

Q15 · Do you have a USD 10,000-plus cash movement planned that requires a US customs filing? US compliance

Why it matters. Carrying cash is one of the priced routes, and failing to declare it can cost more than the transfer.

What is already known. FinCEN Form 105 is required above USD 10,000 entering or leaving the US; failure risks forfeiture and civil or criminal penalties [S28].

What is missing. Whether any route you choose involves physical cash.

How to find it. If it does, pre-fill Form 105 before travel and keep the receipt.

Sources: S28
