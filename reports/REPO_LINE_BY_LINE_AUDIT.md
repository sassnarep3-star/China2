## 1. Methodology & Zero-Hallucination Standard

Every file present in the repository at commit `6849db3` was read line by line and cross-checked by fetching the full text of each cited official URL (`safe.gov.cn`, `pbc.gov.cn`, `chinatax.gov.cn`, `customs.gov.cn`, `gov.cn`, `irs.gov`, `fincen.gov`, `cbp.gov`).

- **Verified:** Exact rule, number, and scope appear verbatim or substantively in the cited official government source.
- **Citation Mismatch / Incomplete:** Underlying legal rule is real, or partially real, but the cited URL on that row does **not** contain one or more specific figures/claims stated in the row (corrected primary official URL provided below).
- **Substantive Irregularity Flagged:** A statement misapplies PRC or US rules to a **US citizen** (`境外个人` / non-domestic individual) or omits a controlling statutory exception.

Per the `PARALLEL SESSIONS` protocol, files owned by earlier/other sessions (`docs/*`, `EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md`) were **not** overwritten during this task; instead, all audit findings and corrected primary links are recorded here and published on `portal/repo-audit.html` and `portal/flagged-for-review.html`.
