THE ANSWER

There is no conversion tax.
There is a spread, a fee, and a filing history.

The “penalty” people feel is four separate things stacked: PRC tax already due on the source, the bank’s spread, the wire fee, and U.S. reporting exposure. Only the first is a tax. Only the last is avoidable by doing nothing wrong earlier.
