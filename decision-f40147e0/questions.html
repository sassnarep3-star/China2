Open questions

What must be answered before a decision

Fifteen questions. Each one is tied to the rule it changes and to a way of obtaining the answer. Until they are answered, every number on this site is a range, not a quote.

No manual input is required to read this site. The questions are here because the answers live in your bank and tax records, not because the research is unfinished. Everything that can be established from public official sources has been, and is on the other pages.

Q01 Which FX rulebook applies to you: domestic individual or non-domestic individual?A foreign national continuously resident in China for one year or more is a “domestic individual” (Decree 532 Art. 52(2)), while a non-domestic individual’s China income follows a separate conversion route.It decides the document set and whether a USD 50,000 annual ceiling binds the plan.Ask the account branch to state the classification and the rulebook in writing.S07 · safe.gov.cn S09 · safe.gov.cn S10 · safe.gov.cn
Q02 What is the source of the balance — salary, business income, property sale, inheritance, gift, or trading gains?Each source has its own route and its own tax rate: 3%–45%, 5%–35%, or a 20% flat rate.A property sale needs a tax-clearance certificate; an inheritance needs SAFE approval; salary needs tax records.Reconstruct from payslips, contracts, the property transfer file and the tax record.S12 · safe.gov.cn S14 · fgk.chinatax.gov.cn S09 · safe.gov.cn
Q03 Do you hold a China tax record (纳税记录) for each year the balance was earned?Banks require source-of-funds materials including tax vouchers; Wise will not send without tax paid in the last three years.Without it the bank route stalls and the licensed-provider route is closed.Download the record from the individual income tax app or request it at the tax office.S10 · safe.gov.cn S24 · wise.com
Q04 When and at what rate was each tranche of RMB acquired?§988 measures exchange gain from the booking date to the payment date.It decides whether the conversion produces ordinary income and how much.Match bank statements to the date each payment landed; record the rate on that date.S19 · govinfo.gov S18 · irs.gov
Q05 Have you filed FBARs and Forms 8938 for every year the account exceeded the threshold?FBAR above a USD 10,000 aggregate; 8938 above USD 50,000/75,000 in the U.S. or 200,000/300,000 abroad.A late filing is far cheaper to fix than a transfer that draws attention to an unfiled account.Check your filed returns and the FinCEN BSA system; a CPA can run a quiet-disclosure analysis.S20 · irs.gov S22 · irs.gov S21 · ecfr.gov
Q06 Do you live in the U.S. or abroad now, and in which state?The 8938 thresholds and the foreign earned income exclusion depend on residence; state tax is separate.It changes which forms apply and which tax rates sit on top of §988.State your residence dates for each tax year; confirm state treatment with a preparer.S22 · irs.gov S18 · irs.gov
Q07 Is the account solely yours, or joint or with signature authority?The FBAR reaches signature authority; 8938 reaches specified individuals with an interest.It changes who must file what.Read the account opening documents and the bank's record of authorised signatories.S20 · irs.gov S22 · irs.gov
Q08 Has any emigration, hukou cancellation or inheritance event occurred?The emigration and inheritance transfer regimes are approved by SAFE at the branch of the original residence and allow a lump sum or batches.It is the only route designed for a large one-off transfer of pre-existing assets.Collect the emigration or inheritance documents and ask the relevant SAFE branch.S11 · safe.gov.cn S09 · safe.gov.cn
Q09 Is the money described as a gift from a relative?A foreign gift above USD 100,000 from a nonresident alien must be reported on Form 3520.It converts a transfer question into a gift-reporting question with its own penalty.Establish who the donor is and the amount for the year before moving anything.S23 · irs.gov
Q10 Do you hold an HSBC Premier, Advance or Private Banking relationship?The published tariff differs by tier: CNY 100–500 plus CNY 120 for Premier/Advance; free for individual Private Banking.It can remove the fee, though not the spread.Ask the relationship manager for the tier and the applicable tariff in writing.S06 · hsbc.com.cn
Q11 Is a single outward payment going to be USD 50,000 or more?A single outward payment at or above that level for listed items requires tax recordal.It adds a step and a lead time to the plan.Ask the local tax authority for the current electronic procedure (F10).S13 · fgk.chinatax.gov.cn
Q12 Do you have a U.S. receiving account in your own name?Banks and licensed providers require the beneficiary to be the sender, and Wise requires an account you own.A third-party destination can stop the transfer at the compliance stage.Confirm the exact beneficiary name and the bank's incoming-wire details.S24 · wise.com S09 · safe.gov.cn
Q13 How much of the annual FX quota have you already used this year?The USD 50,000 quota is an annual aggregate per person.It may already be partly consumed, which changes the timing of a quota-based plan.Ask the bank for your settlement/purchase record for the year.S09 · safe.gov.cn
Q14 What does the U.S. receiving bank charge for an incoming international wire?No U.S. bank fee schedule was read this pass.It is a missing cost in every total on this site (F06).Read the receiving bank's own published fee schedule.S20 · irs.gov
Q15 Has anyone proposed a route involving crypto, an underground swap, friends' quotas or a manufactured invoice?Those are prohibited, with fines of up to 30% of the amount, or 30% to an equal amount if serious.The fine exceeds the entire lawful cost; the answer to this question should be no.Nothing to discover — decline the route and document the refusal if pressure continues.S07 · safe.gov.cn S15 · pbc.gov.cn S08 · pbc.gov.cn

What is already established

  • The lawful routes and their statutory basis are settled: bank conversion of documented current-account income, quota purchase, emigration and inheritance transfers, property proceeds with tax clearance.
  • The published fee formulas and boards of three mainland banks are known, with the retrieval time on each.
  • The prohibited routes and their statutory penalty ranges are settled.
  • The U.S. reporting thresholds and the current penalty maxima are settled.
  • What is not settled is everything that depends on your private facts — which is why this page exists.
