§988: the tax that appears when you convert

Exchange gain or loss on disposing of foreign currency is computed separately and taxed as ordinary income or loss between the booking date and the payment date. If the RMB was acquired at a weaker rate than today’s, the conversion produces taxable ordinary income on top of the transfer cost.
