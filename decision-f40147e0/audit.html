Repository review

Line-by-line re-check of the sections already here

This repository already contains more than a dozen dashboards answering the same question. Rather than add a fifteenth unexamined copy, this pass re-read the load-bearing official pages and re-checked the numbers that the earlier sections rely on.

Scope limit, stated plainly. Fourteen checks were performed. They cover the rate boards, the fee formulas, the quota wording, the prohibition instrument, the cash ceiling, the missing deductions and the rebuild behaviour of the existing dashboards. They do not constitute a full re-verification of every source in every section — see F16.
