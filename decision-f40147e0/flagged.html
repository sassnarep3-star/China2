16 unresolved items, published rather than filled in

Everything on this page is a gap: a value that could not be read from an official page, a method of comparison that a reader should disagree with if they prefer, or a fact about the account holder that this study refuses to assume.

These do not block publication and they are not errors in the arithmetic. They are the boundary of what the sources support. Two of them — F11 and F12 — decide the answer and can only be closed with private documents.
