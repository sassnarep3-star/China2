China FX Rules

China's foreign exchange control framework

The rules that govern how a US citizen can lawfully move CNY held in a mainland Chinese bank account into USD and out of China.

The decisive point

The USD 50,000 individual quota is a documentation threshold, not a legal ceiling. SAFE states explicitly that conversion beyond the quota is permitted with valid ID plus documents proving the transaction is genuine. Separately, SAFE confirms that outward remittance of company profits is a current-account item handled directly at the bank with evidencing documents and no quota. The constraint on dividends is tax, not FX permission.
