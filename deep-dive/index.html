The key finding

China's USD 50,000 per person per year individual FX facilitation limit behaves largely as a documentation threshold rather than an absolute ceiling: SAFE states officially that amounts above the limit can be handled at banks with ID plus supporting materials when the transaction background is true (SAFE, 2015 press-conference Q&A). Caveat (integration review 2026-11-10): eligibility for the purchase facility depends on individual status — the 2007 SAFE rules tie FX purchases to domestic individuals, and State Council Decree No. 532 art. 52(2) counts foreign nationals resident in China ≥1 year as 境内个人 (see ladder/flagged.html F02). If the money is personal, lawfully-earned and its source already subjected to PRC income tax in the correct category, the conversion itself adds no further PRC income tax — that is not a personalized tax ruling; category, source, residency and U.S. FTC limits are not determined here. The bank's FX spread and, on the US side, any currency gain above USD 200 under § 988(e), plus FBAR/FATCA reporting while the money sits in China, still apply. See Flagged for review.

Layer 5 dwarfs layers 1–4

A willful FBAR penalty — the greater of USD 165,353 or 50% of the balance — can exceed the entire amount being repatriated. Optimising tax while risking a penalty is the wrong trade.

Practical sequence

  1. Establish what the money is. Personal taxed salary, or company profit? This single fact decides everything.
  2. Get the documentation in order before approaching the bank. Employment contract, IIT payment records, tax recordal form, source-of-funds evidence covering the whole period the money has been in the account.
  3. Clear the US compliance position first. File outstanding FBARs and Forms 8938, or use the Streamlined procedures for non-willful past non-compliance. Do this before moving large sums.
  4. Model the § 988(e) gain. Compare the CNY acquisition cost against the conversion rate. Above USD 200, the whole gain is ordinary income with no foreign tax credit.
  5. If it is company profit, claim the treaty — 10%, not 20% — and file Form 1116 for the credit.
  6. Prefer paying overseas obligations directly where you have genuine ones. It is the only route tax-free on both sides.
  7. Get the personal-property-transfer channel verified if you are emigrating. Its existence is confirmed on the official SAFE catalogue; its substantive conditions are not verified here.
