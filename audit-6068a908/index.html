Independent targeted review

Reviewed 10 November 2026. This is a spot check, not a line-by-line factual audit of the entire repository. Source availability and law can change. Research is not individualized tax or legal advice.

Bottom line: For documented lawful current-account income, ask an authorized bank about conversion using proof of source and tax documentation. Eligibility depends on the actual source of the money. We cannot establish the lowest-cost or lowest-tax method, the actual spread, or an individual tax liability from the information supplied. Do not treat a bank-fee illustration as the full delivered cost.

U.S. currency gainSection 988 includes disposition of nonfunctional currency and defines it to include bank deposits. The tax result depends on transaction facts and exceptions; it is not a flat conversion penalty.26 U.S.C. § 988(c) (2024 edition)
Exchange rates for taxFor a taxpayer whose functional currency is USD, the IRS generally instructs translation of relevant income and expenses at the rate prevailing when received, paid, or accrued. This is not necessarily the bank's executable rate today.IRS currency guidance
FBARGenerally applies to a U.S. person with a relevant foreign financial account when the aggregate value of foreign accounts exceeded $10,000 at any time in the year, subject to exceptions. Whether accounts generated taxable income does not determine the reporting obligation.IRS FBAR guidance
