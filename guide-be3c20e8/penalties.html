12 layers, not one number

Tax on underlying income, documentary review, exchange spread, sender fees, third-party charges, PRC fines, U.S. currency gain and reporting penalties are separate. Statutory ceilings below are published maxima, not predicted personal penalties.

P01 · Not identified

Separate conversion tax

The reviewed PRC FX statutes impose authenticity, quota and approval rules; none of the reviewed pages identifies a standalone tax on the act of converting CNY into USD. This is not a tax-free certification for the underlying funds.

P02 · Published rates and enforcement ranges

PRC tax on the underlying income or asset

Tax follows the income type and residence: comprehensive income 3.00%–45.00%, business income 5.00%–35.00%, and a 20.00% rate for interest/dividends/property/other enumerated income. Hukou cancellation requires tax clearance; late tax accrues a daily 0.05% surcharge and evasion can attract a 0.5–5.0× fine.

Flags: F-02 F-13

B13 B14 B20 B32

P03 · Document lists and review duty

Documentary gate

The practical barrier is often proof, not price: lawful-source materials, a tax certificate, ID and, for property transfers, immigration/inheritance/title documents. Banks must reasonably review authenticity, and SAFE may refuse property with unproven lawful source.

Flags: F-02 F-20

P04 · Board-derived illustration

Exchange spread

Buying USD at a bank's sell price and valuing it at the midpoint of that bank's published buy/sell prices produces a measurable board spread: 0.2094% for BOC and 0.4979% for HSBC. The boards themselves are reference-only.

Flags: F-04 F-05

B01 B02 B07

P05 · Published tariff components

Sender, cable, card and cash fees

BOC publishes 0.10% (min CNY 50, max CNY 260) plus a CNY 150 cable charge for non-HK/Macau/Taiwan e-banking wires. HSBC publishes a comparable capped TT fee, free notes withdrawal, overseas-ATM charges and Global Transfer charges by segment.

Flags: F-06 F-07 F-23

B03 B04 B16

P06 · Exclusion and optional charge

Intermediary and receiving charges

HSBC's tariff states that its charges exclude other institutions' charges and government taxes; BOC's schedule points to local/agreement differences. Pay Exact is an optional published charge, not a cap on shortfalls. No receiving-bank fee was found.

Flags: F-01 F-07

B03 B04

P07 · Statutory ranges

PRC evasion, splitting and prohibited-channel fines

Evasion, fraudulent FX purchase, private/underground FX dealing and disguised capital transfers can attract a fine up to 30.00% of the amount, or from 30.00% up to an equal amount in serious cases, plus criminal liability. Splitting or borrowing quotas can trigger a watch list; crypto business is declared illegal.

Flags: F-18 F-21

B09 B15 B18 B32

P08 · De-minimis threshold and character

U.S. §988 currency gain

Disposition of nonfunctional currency is a §988 transaction. An individual's personal-transaction gain is not recognized only when the otherwise recognized gain does not exceed $200; larger gains are ordinary income/loss. Acquisition basis and the ordinary tax rate are personal facts.

Flags: F-14 F-19

B06 B07 B27

P09 · Thresholds and inflation-adjusted maxima

U.S. information-reporting penalties

FBAR uses a 10,000 aggregate-account threshold; Form 8938 thresholds depend on residence/status; Form 3520 applies to large foreign gifts; CBP uses a 10,000 currency-reporting threshold. These are reporting duties, not taxes on the transfer. The eCFR table gives maximum non-willful and willful penalties but its amendment history ends in 2025.

Flags: F-15 F-17

B21 B22 B23 B24 B28 B30

P10 · Caps and interaction rules

FEIE, FTC and treaty interaction

The 2026 FEIE cap is $132,900 and the next-year cap is $137,300. A foreign tax credit uses qualifying tax on the same income, cannot be claimed on excluded income, and may be limited by a treaty-reduced rate. Treaty text was not read line by line here.

Flags: F-16

B25 B26 B29 B34

P11 · Caps and issuer ATM fee

Cash and ATM physical constraints

Mainland cards used abroad are capped at CNY 100,000 per person per calendar year and CNY 10,000 per card per day; carrying foreign notes above 5,000 needs a bank permit and above 10,000 is generally prohibited. Foreign ATM-owner fees, network rates, cash deposit/conversion and security costs are not published.

Flags: F-10 F-11

B04 B16 B17 B28

P12 · Published time/count limits

Time and future-rate exposure

Quota, ATM and cash routes can require multiple calendar years; the property-transfer approval has published acceptance and decision working-day limits. Future bank rates, tax years and approval outcomes are not published, so any multi-year all-in estimate would be invented.

Flags: F-01 F-04 F-19
