14 questions before a decision

No route can be chosen and no personal tax figure can be computed until these are established. Each has a concrete way to find out.

Q01 · decision gate

What is the legal and economic origin of every CNY in the account?

What is published / known: PRC law taxes enumerated categories including wages, services, business, interest, dividends, rent, property transfer and other income at different rules.

Missing private fact: Your contracts, statements, sale records, gift/inheritance documents and source-of-funds chronology.

Why it changes the answer: The origin decides whether the documented-income, emigrant/inheritance, or another approval route fits, and whether PRC tax was due.

Exact discovery action: Download all account statements; assemble employment contracts, payslips, invoices, sale agreements, gift or inheritance papers; ask the bank which source documents it will accept in writing.

Q02 · decision gate

Was all PRC tax on the underlying income or asset paid, and is there an official tax certificate?

What is published / known: SAFE's documented-income rule requires source materials including tax documents; property-transfer guides require a tax certificate or tax-payment proof; the tax authority issues a completion certificate and late tax carries a daily surcharge.

Missing private fact: Your 完税证明 / tax certificate and whether any amount is overdue.

Why it changes the answer: Without tax proof a bank may refuse FX purchase; unpaid tax can grow through surcharge and enforcement.

Exact discovery action: Request official tax records and a 完税证明 from the competent tax bureau; reconcile them to the source documents; ask a PRC tax professional to certify whether anything remains due.

Q03 · decision gate

Are you a PRC tax resident, a foreign individual in China, or a foreign permanent-residence ID holder?

What is published / known: The statute defines a domestic individual to include qualifying foreigners resident for at least 183 days; PRC tax residence uses a 183-day rule; only foreign permanent-residence ID holders are stated to use the annual purchase convenience quota in the SAFE FAQ.

Missing private fact: Passport, visas, residence records, days present, and permanent-residence card status.

Why it changes the answer: This determines whether the US$50,000 annual quota, documented-income route, or another route is even available.

Exact discovery action: Build a day-count calendar from entry/exit stamps; keep passport, residence permit and permanent-residence card copies; ask the bank to state your classification in writing.

Q04 · decision gate

Did you acquire foreign citizenship/permanent residence and cancel a PRC hukou before the funds arose?

What is published / known: Emigrant property transfer is for lawful property owned before the immigration status; the guide lists hukou cancellation and foreign settlement evidence and assigns approval to the original-hukou SAFE office.

Missing private fact: Naturalisation/permanent-residence dates, hukou cancellation certificate and property acquisition dates.

Why it changes the answer: These facts decide whether a one-off emigrant property transfer approval is possible.

Exact discovery action: Obtain certified immigration-status and hukou-cancellation documents and a dated property schedule; ask the original-hukou SAFE office whether it will accept an application.

Q05 · decision gate

Is any part an inheritance from a person whose hukou was in mainland China?

What is published / known: Inheritance transfer is a distinct route assigned to the deceased's hukou SAFE office; it requires inheritance, title and tax documents and must go to the heir's own overseas account.

Missing private fact: Death certificate, relationship/inheritance proof, title documents, tax certificate and the deceased's hukou location.

Why it changes the answer: Using the ordinary income route for inherited capital would misstate the transaction.

Exact discovery action: Collect notarised or officially accepted inheritance and title documents; ask the deceased's hukou SAFE office for its current checklist.

Q06 · decision gate

Which PRC bank holds the CNY, and exactly whose U.S. account will receive the USD?

What is published / known: The property-transfer rule requires remittance to the applicant's own overseas account; Wise says from-CNY transfers can go only to another account the sender owns; SAFE rules distinguish own-account and cross-border transfers.

Missing private fact: Bank name, branch, account title, your U.S. receiving bank and account ownership evidence.

Why it changes the answer: A third-party recipient, mismatched name or unsupported corridor can stop the transfer or trigger compliance review.

Exact discovery action: Get a bank letter with your exact account title and available services; open or confirm a U.S. account in the same name; ask the PRC bank to confirm the receiving-account details it will accept.

Q07 · decision gate

What is the exact amount, currency and desired timing?

What is published / known: This guide prices every US$10,000 step from the first to the last tier; published rate boards are dated and for reference only.

Missing private fact: Your actual balance, whether interest accrues, and whether you need one payment or staged payments.

Why it changes the answer: Wire caps, annual quota/cash limits, FX movements and future tax years all depend on amount and timing.

Exact discovery action: Obtain an official balance letter; decide the target amount in USD; ask the bank whether a single or several remittances are possible and what rate applies on each date.

Q08 · decision gate

Will the bank actually approve this source of funds and what exact rate/fees will it quote?

What is published / known: Banks must reasonably review authenticity; BOC's published fee schedule allows separate agreements, promotions and local differences; its linked promotion page currently returns a 404; HSBC's tariff excludes other institutions' charges and taxes.

Missing private fact: A written bank eligibility decision, executable rate, local fee schedule, correspondent/receiving fees and any monthly account fee.

Why it changes the answer: A published tariff is not approval, a quote, or an all-in cost.

Exact discovery action: Ask the branch for a written checklist, an executable rate and a full fee breakdown including intermediary and receiving charges; retain the reply. Do not rely on the web board alone.

Q09 · decision gate

What prior U.S. foreign-account, asset and gift reports have been filed?

What is published / known: FBAR is required when foreign-account aggregate value exceeds US$10,000 at any time; Form 8938 thresholds depend on residence and filing status; Form 3520 applies to large foreign gifts/bequests; Streamlined procedures require non-willful certification.

Missing private fact: Prior-year returns, FinCEN filings, account maxima, gift documents and whether the IRS has contacted you.

Why it changes the answer: A low-tax transfer can become expensive if past reporting is missing; penalties are separate from conversion.

Exact discovery action: Obtain IRS account transcripts and prior returns; list each foreign account and its annual maximum; give the filing history to a U.S. tax professional before choosing a correction route.

Q10 · decision gate

When and at what CNY/USD rate was the CNY acquired?

What is published / known: The IRS says a spot rate is generally used when an item is received/paid/accrued, has no official exchange rate, and accepts a consistently used posted rate; a personal currency gain above US$$200 is not excluded by §988.

Missing private fact: Acquisition dates, exchange rates, basis records and whether the later disposition is a personal transaction.

Why it changes the answer: Currency movement between acquisition and disposition can create U.S. ordinary gain or loss even though principal is not income.

Exact discovery action: Reconstruct each deposit's date and CNY/USD rate from bank and payroll records; choose a consistent posted rate with a tax professional; compute potential gain before transferring.

Q11 · decision gate

Do FEIE, foreign tax credit or a treaty apply to the underlying income?

What is published / known: The 2026 FEIE exclusion cap is $132,900 and the next-year cap is $137,300; the FTC is limited to qualifying tax on the same income and cannot be claimed on excluded income; treaty effects require the specific treaty text.

Missing private fact: Tax home, abode, bona fide residence/physical presence, foreign tax paid, and treaty residence.

Why it changes the answer: Exclusion and credit rules interact; neither changes the principal, and a wrong election can create double work or penalties.

Exact discovery action: Give a U.S. tax professional your travel calendar, foreign tax receipts and income character; read the current treaty publication before claiming a treaty position.

Q12 · decision gate

Is any part a gift or bequest from a foreign person?

What is published / known: A U.S. person reports gifts/bequests from a nonresident alien or foreign estate above $100,000 and from foreign entities above $20,573 in 2026; related-party gifts are aggregated.

Missing private fact: Donor identity, donor status, relationship, gift documents and aggregation across related parties.

Why it changes the answer: A mistaken gift label affects both Form 3520 reporting and PRC source-of-funds review.

Exact discovery action: Collect signed gift statements and donor-status evidence; aggregate all related-party gifts for the tax year; ask a U.S. tax professional to review Form 3520.

Q13 · decision gate

Will any cash or monetary instruments be physically carried?

What is published / known: PRC rules allow up to US$5,000 without a carry permit, require a bank permit above that up to US$10,000, and generally prohibit more except special SAFE permits; U.S. rules require reporting more than US$10,000 at one time.

Missing private fact: Travel plan, denominations, permits, security arrangements and group totals.

Why it changes the answer: Physical carrying adds permit, reporting, security and deposit/conversion unknowns; families/groups are aggregated for CBP.

Exact discovery action: Ask the bank and SAFE what permit is required; obtain the permit; keep purchase and withdrawal memos; file the U.S. report when the collective amount exceeds the threshold.

Q14 · decision gate

Has the bank/SAFE confirmed a direct cross-border RMB route in writing?

What is published / known: PBOC supports banks handling current-account cross-border RMB settlement for individuals, including lawful salary, under bank due-diligence and AML duties, but publishes no personal fee, rate or general capital/savings route here.

Missing private fact: Written legal basis, permitted purpose, tariff, limit, exchange rate and receiving-bank terms.

Why it changes the answer: A generic policy supporting current-account settlement does not establish a lawful route for moving an accumulated capital balance.

Exact discovery action: Ask the bank for the exact rule, permitted transaction category, written approval and full price; if it cannot provide them, leave the route unused and blank.
