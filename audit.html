What the audit changes about the answer

None of the irregularities change the headline conclusion, which rests on the IIT Law and §988 read directly: no PRC conversion tax, no US tax on moving your own funds. They do change which earlier page a reader should trust. The civil-law point in A07 does change a recommendation, which is why it is a flag rather than a footnote: whether a long-term US resident of China may use the USD 50,000 quota has not been settled by anything in this repository.

What this audit could not check

  • Whether any earlier session's external links still resolve — no global link-health check was run.
  • Whether earlier Chinese-language excerpts were translated correctly by their authors — only the cited locators were compared.
  • Whether the bank practice described anywhere is current at a particular branch.

Research, not a tax opinion. No personalised eligibility, lowest-cost provider or individual tax amount is certified. No funds were moved and no returns were filed. Blank cells are unverified or not stated by any official source — they are not zero.
