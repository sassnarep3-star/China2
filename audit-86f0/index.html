Executive summary

  1. First establish account ownership, original source, tax years and the written reason for the bank’s refusal. Do not assume conversion itself created the tax.
  2. For documented eligible current-account income, use a regulated bank or a provider that accepts the source and your identity. Compare same-time, all-in quotes and receiver fees; no cheapest provider is established. SAFE
  3. For qualifying foreign income taxes, the U.S. foreign tax credit may reduce U.S. tax; foreign earned income exclusion has separate requirements and the same excluded income cannot also generate a foreign tax credit. IRS foreign tax credit
  4. Foreign accounts may trigger FBAR if their aggregate value exceeded $10,000 at any time in a calendar year; Form 8938 is separate and depends on circumstances. IRS FBAR
  5. Do not use borrowed quotas, fabricated documents or underground swaps. If records or prior returns are missing, obtain regulated professional help before executing.
