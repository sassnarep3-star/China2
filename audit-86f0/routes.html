Methods & execution

Eligibility is conditional, never assumed. These are route families, not a promise that all are available for this balance. View the earlier 22-entry catalog; its entries include delivery arrangements and rejected workarounds, not 22 independent solutions.

Documented salary/current-account bank wire

Who it may fit: Foreign individual with lawful current-account RMB income, subject to bank review.

Pros: Strong audit trail; can address larger documented amounts. Cons: Proof, possible branch visit, spread and wire costs.

Steps: 1. Establish source and amount; collect ID, employment/income records and applicable tax evidence. 2. Ask the bank which documents and authorizations it accepts. 3. Obtain executable FX and all wire/receiver charges; compare. 4. Convert to USD and instruct remittance to your own U.S. account. 5. Retain receipts and reconcile final credit.

Official source

Wise, bank-app funding

Who it may fit: Provider says eligible users who work/worked in China can send from CNY with required tax record and their own account; check current conditions.

Pros: Alternative quote and electronic funding. Cons: Provider eligibility, limits and fees; not a bypass of compliance.

Steps: 1. Review current provider eligibility, recipient and tax-record requirements. 2. Set up your own USD recipient and request a quote. 3. Submit verification and fund only from your own eligible Chinese bank account as instructed. 4. Track provider review and delivery; retain records.

Official source

Wise, Alipay funding

Who it may fit: Same Wise service; eligible user and accepted Alipay payment required.

Pros: Alternative payment interface. Cons: Not an independent annual allowance; individual availability and cost unknown.

Steps: 1. Check Wise eligibility and quote. 2. Submit requested verification. 3. Follow the provider’s Alipay payment screen from your own eligible account. 4. Confirm delivery and retain documentation.

Official source

Reconvert unused previously exchanged RMB

Who it may fit: Only unused RMB from prior foreign-currency exchange; original slip normally valid 24 months, with specified small-amount exceptions.

Pros: May fit genuine leftover travel funds. Cons: Not a general shortcut for unexplained earnings; commercial costs unknown.

Steps: 1. Confirm money is unused RMB from a previous exchange. 2. Provide valid ID and original exchange receipt when required. 3. Ask the bank whether small-amount exceptions and current rules apply. 4. Obtain quote and comply with cash or remittance rules.

Official source

Property-sale proceeds

Who it may fit: Potential route only if owner, sale and supporting documents satisfy applicable rules.

Pros: May be available for genuine ownership and sale. Cons: Separate capital/real-estate rules, taxes, registration and bank review; not automatically available.

Steps: 1. Identify ownership and sale proceeds. 2. Obtain registered sale documents and applicable tax records. 3. Ask the bank and local SAFE what procedures and approvals apply. 4. Convert/remit only after authorization and quote.

Official source

Inheritance or qualifying emigrant property

Who it may fit: Specific legal-status and asset conditions; prior SAFE/bank procedures must be checked.

Pros: Potential route for qualifying property. Cons: Not a general salary channel; documentary and approval burden.

Steps: 1. Identify the specific property, beneficiary and qualifying status. 2. Ask the local SAFE and bank for the current personal-property transfer procedure. 3. Assemble legal and tax documents, then seek required approval. 4. Obtain quote and remit if approved.

Official source

ATM cash withdrawal abroad

Who it may fit: Requires working domestic card and compliant overseas withdrawal.

Pros: Can access spending cash abroad. Cons: Not a direct large bank transfer; annual/per-card controls, cash risk and unknown charges.

Steps: 1. Verify card use abroad and issuer limits. 2. Withdraw within applicable limits. 3. Check ATM and issuer charges. 4. Retain records; do not borrow cards to evade limits.

Official source

Lawful physical currency transport

Who it may fit: Must first obtain the foreign currency lawfully; separate China export and U.S. reporting rules apply.

Pros: A delivery method for actual cash. Cons: Loss/seizure risk, reporting and restrictions; no verified total cost.

Steps: 1. Verify current export permissions before travel. 2. Withdraw and carry only lawfully permitted amounts. 3. Declare physical currency as required when entering the U.S. 4. Deposit subject to receiving-bank rules.
